# L7: learning who to trust in context (EXPLORATORY)

**EXPLORATORY** (docs/PROTOCOL.md 10.1). Dev split only; not a finding. One training seed.

**Question.** When source reliabilities and overlaps are unknown and change between tasks, can an aggregator that attends over a short history of each source's past reports and outcomes (in context, no weight updates) learn reliability and overlap on the fly, and approach the oracle that knows the true structure as the history grows (H = 0, 4, 16, 64 past episodes)?

**Setup.** aggbench imported unmodified (harness sha256 `9c0f9c5a…79b0`, the harness at commit `391c6d1`; the JSON says `git_commit: unknown` because the VM copy had no `.git`). A *task* is one harness episode's source configuration (coverage, piece strengths, adversary roles and distortions, agent order). Its H past episodes keep that configuration and redraw Y and the evidence with the environment's own law (per-task RNG seeded by the episode seed; on all 2000 dev episodes, `report_from_x` reproduces the harness reports exactly, max diff 0.0). The query is the harness dev episode itself, so regret is measured on an unmodified harness Batch. Keys hidden (visibility `none`): the only signal about reliability and overlap is the track record. Envs: E2, E3-sybil, E3-biased, E3-collude at default config; E1/E4 not run.

**Arms.**
- `icl_attn@H`: a table transformer (4 blocks, d=64; attention across sources within an episode and across past episodes within a source). It outputs a trust weight per source plus an offset: logit = l0 + Σ w_i (r_i − l0) + c. One network, trained on 30k train tasks per env (all four envs mixed, H drawn from 0..64), BCE on truth, 18k steps on one L4. At H=0 it is the **fixed learned** aggregator, which sees current reports only.
- `bayes_logreg@H` (added post hoc, after conj_bayes looked weak; `posthoc_logreg.py`): online Bayesian logistic regression on the reports, with a prior centred on the extremized pool, MAP plus probit predictive, and the prior scale fitted once per env on train tasks.
- `conj_bayes@H`: online Bayesian generative learner, r | Y ~ N(c ± δ, Σ) with a conjugate matrix-normal-inverse-Wishart prior (empirical Bayes from train). With the true (c, δ, Σ) it equals oracle_clearing.
- `hedge_logpool@H`: Hedge/wealth weights exp(−η · cumulative log loss) on a log pool with extremizing α; (η, α) fitted on train for each H.
- `wealth_kelly@H`: Kelly-betting market. Each source's wealth is multiplied by the probability it gave the outcome; the price is the wealth-weighted linear pool.
- Fixed and ceilings: harness `extremized_log_pool` (α fitted on train), `oracle_clearing` (the posterior given the reports and the true structure: the asymptote for any learner of reports) and `bayes`.
- Controls (`aggbench.controls.run_with_controls`, through the same predict): `random_network`, `untrained_init`.

**Result (one line, EXPLORATORY).** The in-context aggregator learns whom to trust from the track record. Its regret falls monotonically with H in all four envs, and at H=64 it closes 67–82% of its H=0 gap to oracle_clearing, leaving 0.003–0.011 nats. At H=16 and H=64 it has the lowest mean regret of every learner in every env. Paired against the online Bayesian baselines (conj_bayes, bayes_logreg) at those H, the CIs exclude 0, with margins of 0.005–0.043 nats. Both controls are above 1 nat at every H, so they fail.

**Caveats.**
- One seed, dev split only.
- The network was meta-trained on the same four environment families, so it amortizes their structure prior. The generic-prior Bayesian baselines do not have that prior.
- Histories are stationary within a task.
- `bayes_logreg` was added after the first look at the results.
- The Kelly market converges toward the single best source and cannot combine sources, so it stays worst among the learners.
- At H=0 the learned aggregator already beats the extremized pool in E3-sybil and E3-biased (−0.034 and −0.019 nats paired; it spots duplicate and extreme reports). It is level in E3-collude (−0.001) and slightly worse in E2 (+0.004, CI includes 0). So the H=0 row is the fixed-learned-weights arm, and the H>0 gains are on top of it.
