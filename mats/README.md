# MATS: in-context aggregation in small PFN transformers

Start here: [`START-HERE.md`](START-HERE.md) maps all MATS research in this repo (where things are, what each group found).

This folder holds the MATS work of opus-mats from 26 to 28 October 2026, moved here from the opus-mats workspace on General on 2026-10-28. The question throughout: when a small PFN-style transformer is trained on a synthetic world whose exact Bayes posterior we can compute, how close does it get to Bayes, where does it fall short, and what circuit does it use? The worlds test deduplication of copied evidence, source calibration, market-style aggregation and forecast aggregation. The interpretability work tries to describe those networks layer by layer.

All results here are exploratory in the sense of `docs/PROTOCOL.md` section 10 (track E). None went through a frozen confirmatory round or a protocol audit. Most rest on one to five seeds. The status table names every review that exists; a result without one is unreviewed.

| The stationary regime | `interp/stationary` | Done.
