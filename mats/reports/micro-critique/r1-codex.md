**Verdict:** A is the best first experiment; B has stronger market content; C needs a corrected simulator. The proposal establishes tractable known-structure targets, but has not yet established a task that requires learning the information structure from history.

**World A.** The strongest objection is that the supplied graph already exposes commonality. A network can learn graph-dependent weights averaged over hidden parameters without using history. Commonality pairs reject “quote = posterior,” but do not reject this stronger shortcut. Test identical current inputs with different informative histories against an exact history-blind Bayes baseline. The equilibrium calculation is sound for \(0\le r<1\), and quadratic supervision is usable. However, a coordination quote has no execution, inventory, or counterparty; calling it the closest economic match to trader pricing overstates the connection. Layerwise correlation with level-k iterates also needs causal interventions before it supports a computational mechanism.

**World B.** This is the strongest economic model, but the newcomer interpretation requires precision. Replace one incumbent while freezing other strategies and the market maker’s pricing rule; otherwise adding a trader changes the equilibrium. With hidden structure, the target is a best response to those frozen strategies, not an equilibrium strategy. Public-signal coefficient reversals mostly reflect trading on residual information after public information is priced; the reported 100% reversal is therefore a weak test of sophisticated reasoning. Benchmark against residualized own-posterior trading. Profit supervision is valid when **maximized**; the document incorrectly calls its optimum a “minimiser.” Quantities rather than quotes remain a mismatch with Kelvin’s wording.

**World C.** Its numerical evidence does not test its mechanism. `worlds_check.py` sets the predecessor price to \(E[v\mid x_1,\ldots,x_{N-1}]\), directly pooling everyone’s private readings. Sequential scalar posterior reports generally do not reveal that pooled sufficient statistic under overlapping, multidimensional information. Consequently, the quoted KL gap validates a different task. Simulate each trader’s actual information set and retain the full price path. No naive/cursed type mixture is implemented. C2 also needs explicit signal distributions, arrival rules, and buy/sell/no-trade policies before its recursion or herding claim is checkable.
