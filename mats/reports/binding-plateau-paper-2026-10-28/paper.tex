\begin{abstract}
Small transformers trained for Bayesian prediction can stall at an intermediate
loss. In a three-source reliability task, we distinguish nonquery-context use
from source binding. Six retained stalled models outperform
exact query-only Bayes, contradicting a literal query-only account. Yet on a
prespecified subset of fresh episodes, one fixed 3-cycle of labelled-source identities
produces only 0.24--0.47\% of
Bayes's RMS predictive-mean change, versus 82--99\% in five acquired controls.
An exact reference that forgets source association separates information available
without binding from its additional value. Two new 50-seed baseline cohorts
meet a three-evaluation acquisition criterion in 39/50 and 47/50 cases by 80k
updates; early attention is associated with subsequent acquisition in the
width-16 cohort. The results support weak functional
source binding in these stalled checkpoints, without identifying their exact
algorithm or the cause of optimization failure.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and task}
Prior-data fitted networks learn predictive inference across sampled tasks
\citep{muller2021}. Related in-context and meta-learning studies report long
plateaus followed by abrupt acquisition \citep{kirsch2022,reddy2023,nguyen2024}.
A plateau's loss alone does not identify what information a network uses.
We study a small setting with exact conditional distributions and a targeted
input intervention.

Each episode has independent targets
$Y_1,\ldots,Y_4,Y_\star\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ and three sources. Source $s$ has
independently uniform variance $v_s\in\{0.1,0.5,2\}$ and reports
\begin{equation}
 O_{sj}=Y_j+\sqrt{v_s}\,\epsilon_{sj},\qquad
 \epsilon_{sj}\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}\sim\mathcal N(0,1).
\end{equation}
The four labelled targets are observed; $Y_\star$ is to be predicted.
Write $Q=(O_{1\star},O_{2\star},O_{3\star})$ and
$S_s=\sum_{j=1}^4(O_{sj}-Y_j)^2$. Full Bayes uses $(Q,S)$,
where $S=(S_1,S_2,S_3)$, marginalizing 27 variance configurations. Conditional
on $v$, the query posterior is Gaussian with variance
$V_v=(1+\sum_s v_s^{-1})^{-1}$ and mean $V_v\sum_s O_{s\star}/v_s$.

A set-style transformer receives 15 reading tokens and a query token. Tokens
contain source/question identity vectors, readings, observed answers and type
indicators. Identities are fresh each episode; there is no positional encoding.
Two pre-normalized attention/MLP blocks produce a Gaussian or four-component
Gaussian-mixture prediction. Context tokens attend to context; the query token
reads context and itself. Fresh identities prevent a persistent source-reliability
lookup. Finite task support and query-only behavior are distinct hypotheses:
inference over a fixed task pool can still use context \citep{raventos2023}.

\section{Finite-horizon plateaus and early associations}
We trained new Gaussian-head cohorts at widths 16 and 32, with two heads, two
layers, identity dimension 16, and seeds 100--149. AdamW uses learning rate
$10^{-3}$, epsilon $10^{-8}$, decay $0.01$, batch 256, 1k linear warmup,
gradient clipping at 1, and 80k updates without cooldown. Training episodes are
fresh. Evaluation uses 4,096 fixed episodes every 1k updates and a separate
4,096-episode terminal test. Vectorized forward/gradient/update checks against
individual models pass on CPU and GPU; prediction discrepancies are below
$8\times10^{-7}$. Softmax-invariant key-bias updates differ numerically.

The gap is model NLL minus exact-mixture Bayes NLL, in nats. A plateau requires
ten consecutive gaps in $[0.14,0.18]$, spanning 9k updates. Acquisition requires
three consecutive gaps below $0.10$, spanning 2k updates; escape requires a preceding
qualifying plateau. Counts require the third point. Unobserved first-point
event times are ascertainable through 78k at the 80k evaluation horizon.
Table~\ref{tab:cohorts} reports finite-horizon outcomes, not convergence
probabilities. The historical 21/95 plateau count pools 19 heterogeneous
configurations and is not an incidence estimate for either new cohort.

Four early metrics and their directions were specified prospectively: lower
validation gap, greater first-layer own-source attention contrast, greater
second-layer attention to query readings, and greater NLL increase after binding
disruption. Selected summaries of these prespecified analyses follow; all
20 width-16 baseline results are in evidence E1. Query-reading attention at 1k
is associated with acquisition by 80k: AUC $0.907$ (stratified seed-bootstrap 95\% interval
$0.814$--$0.972$). Own-source contrast at 5k has AUC $0.985$
($0.948$--$1.000$), among 48 seeds whose retrospectively identified onset is
after 5k; lower validation gap has AUC $0.791$. All 50 seeds were still
unconfirmed at 5k. A post-hoc sensitivity using that operational risk set gives
own-source AUC $0.986$ ($0.953$--$1.000$).
The highlighted summaries are selected across metrics and landmarks without
multiple-comparison adjustment. Their outcome is acquisition,
not escape conditional on plateau entry. Width 32 has only three
nonacquirers, below our five-per-class AUC reporting rule. These associations
do not establish causality or independent predictive validation.

\section{What information does the plateau retain?}
For any query-only density $q$, proper log loss gives
\begin{align}
&\E\KL\!\left(p(Y_\star\mid Q,S)\,\|\,q(Y_\star\mid Q)\right) \nonumber\\
&\quad=I(Y_\star;S\mid Q)
 +\E\KL\!\left(p(Y_\star\mid Q)\,\|\,q(Y_\star\mid Q)\right).
\end{align}
Exact query-only Bayes therefore minimizes expected log loss under that
information restriction. On 16,000 retained held-out episodes, its gap is
$0.184982\pm0.004321$ nats (paired episode SE). Four stalled mixture models
improve on it by $0.03861$--$0.04109$ nats (SE $0.00215$--$0.00220$);
two stalled Gaussian models improve by approximately $0.023$ nats
(SE $0.0028$). This is strong exploratory evidence against a literal
query-only description.

A reference retaining $U$, the unordered multiset of the three residual sums,
forgets which query source each labelled group belongs to. With
$L_s(v)=v^{-2}\exp[-S_s/(2v)]$, its variance weights are
\begin{equation}
p(v\mid Q,U)\propto p(v)p(Q\mid v)
\frac{1}{6}\sum_{\pi\in\mathfrak S_3}\prod_{s=1}^3 L_{\pi(s)}(v_s).
\label{eq:blind}
\end{equation}
Exchangeability justifies equal association weights; variance-independent density
factors cancel. Because $U$ is a function of $S$, the standard chain rule yields
\begin{equation}
I(Y_\star;S\mid Q)=I(Y_\star;U\mid Q)+I(Y_\star;S\mid Q,U).
\end{equation}
The two gains are estimated as $0.059459\pm0.002533$ and
$0.125523\pm0.003626$ nats. Association supplies about two thirds of the
available contextual log-score gain. These are Bayes information values,
not a causal decomposition of network error.

The unordered reference's moment Gaussian has gap $0.145922$, close in
sampled NLL to the stalled mixture models. They remain $0.0184$--$0.0209$
nats worse than the exact unordered mixture; integrated scores also distinguish
them from its Gaussian approximation. Score proximity cannot identify a
computation, and their residual cannot simply be called a Gaussian-head floor.
A stricter reference retaining only $T=\sum_sS_s$ has exact-mixture gap
$0.155333$, which the four stalled mixtures beat by $0.009$--$0.011$ nats
(paired SE $0.00136$--$0.00145$).
The two Gaussian models beat its optimal conditional-moment Gaussian by about
$0.011$ nats (SE $0.00154$--$0.00155$). These comparisons oppose a scalar-total-residual account within
the respective output classes, subject to sampling and selection uncertainty.
Richer pooled representations remain possible.

\section{A fresh intervention isolates source association}
Before evaluating seed-40000 episodes ($n=4096$), we fixed the intervention,
metrics and checkpoint-selection rule. We cyclically reassign source IDs of all
four labelled readings per source, using $(1,2,0)$, while holding readings,
labels and query-source IDs fixed. Bayes uses the inverse assignment. The
unordered reference is invariant. Token-order equivalence checks verify that
the changed neural input and recomputed Bayes describe the same assignment.

The retained set uses best-validation checkpoints and contains six stalled
models: four mixture models spanning
three architecture families and two width-16 Gaussian models. Controls are the
lowest successful sibling seed in each of four families plus the documented
Gaussian seed that escaped a plateau late in training. Checkpoint selection is
historical; evaluation episodes and intervention metrics are fresh.

We measure predictive-mean response RMS and signed slope relative to Bayes,
and distribution change $J(p,q)=\tfrac12[\KL(p\|q)+\KL(q\|p)]$.
The prespecified subset $J(p_B,p_B')\geq0.3$ contains 1,988 episodes.
Stalled models have only $0.24$--$0.47\%$ of Bayes's RMS mean response;
controls have $82$--$99\%$, signed slopes $0.76$--$0.98$ and correlations
$0.92$--$0.99$. The ratio of mean distribution changes,
$\E[J(p_M,p_M')]/\E[J(p_B,p_B')]$, is
$8.7\times10^{-6}$--$3.3\times10^{-5}$ for stalled models and
$0.802$--$0.986$ for controls; paired episode SEs range from
$4.0\times10^{-7}$ to $1.7\times10^{-6}$ and $0.0044$ to $0.0147$,
respectively. Full-set results agree qualitatively.
Quadrature passes grid/tail expansion, analytic Gaussian and independent
adaptive-mixture checks. Unordered-reference weight invariance holds within
$2.5\times10^{-15}$.

First-layer attention from a source's query-reading token to its own labelled
tokens, divided by attention to other sources' labelled tokens \emph{per token},
is $0.997$--$1.240$ in stalled models and $14.6$--$233$ in controls, pooling
over heads and sources. There are four own-source and eight other-source labelled
tokens. Earlier acquired-network analyses found source-selective first-layer
pooling and showed that post-MLP1 source-query states carry much of the response
to changed labelled residuals (E5). Our ID-only intervention isolates association
itself. It supports weak functional binding in the stalled checkpoints, without
establishing attention as the unique mediator.

\section{Interpretation and limitations}
ICL studies connect plateaus to small optimizer updates, intermediate strategies
and initialization-sensitive circuit formation
\citep{kirsch2022,reddy2023,nguyen2024}. These offer hypotheses, not a transferred
explanation: tasks and optimizers differ, and removing an easy strategy can
impair acquisition. Our contribution is to distinguish contextual evidence use
from source binding in an exactly tractable task, then test the latter directly.

The Bayes references were developed after inspecting historical data. Numerical
checks establish implementation accuracy, not confirmatory model identification.
The intervention uses fresh episodes and prospective metrics, but historically
selected checkpoints and one source 3-cycle. Approximate invariance to this
cycle does not imply invariance to all source permutations or prove that a
network computes Eq.~\eqref{eq:blind}. Cycled inputs remain on support but have
a shifted joint distribution; the intervention measures functional sensitivity
under that shift. The world is small, the cohorts use one
baseline recipe, and Gaussian and mixture heads must be distinguished.
Finite-horizon nonacquisition is not a capacity limit or permanent attractor.
Predictor bootstrap intervals condition on the fixed evaluation set. The results
motivate interventions on learning source routing; they do not establish a
training remedy.
