1. HIGH: C4 does not identify the proposed residual-consensus algorithm. In all 400 regenerated episodes, simply comparing the sample variances of A’s and B’s 12 other-item quote values identifies the low-noise source in 93% of episodes. This ignores item identities, known-source consensus and bias estimation. It is a classification result, not a demonstrated 93% closure score or proof that the nets use this shortcut. Nevertheless, it supplies a concrete competing explanation for source pooling, pH decodability and reliability-sensitive answers. C4 uses exactly two unknown sources, opposite noise levels, only SPOT calibration quotes and Bayes-separated selected pairs. Test cases matching raw spread while changing consensus-relative residuals, and vice versa, before claiming residual-square pooling. The overlap intervention retains 70–74% of the shift and does not meet its preregistered causal threshold.

HIGH: Random-key controls do not establish computational specificity. intervene.py excludes ALL A/B tokens from controls, hence excludes every token whose input changes across members; the preregistered exclusion was narrower. My full-set rerun measured block-3 removed attention mass, averaged over Aq/Bq: D4 heads 0/3 lose 83.9%/90.9% under the edge cut versus 1.63%/0.68% under control; Q6 loses 91.1%/90.5% versus 0.081%/0.104%. Renormalization therefore makes very different interventions. The cuts establish dependence on this heavily used evidence route, not that it specifically transmits a Bayesian noise posterior. Use mass-matched controls and value/content interventions that preserve attention while exchanging reliability evidence, with source identity and other nuisance information controlled.

3. HIGH: Read-out percentages are not a causal decomposition. D4 retaining 65% after equalization does not mean precisely 35% of weighting resides in attention and 65% in values. Residual paths, total attention to the quote pair, other keys, nonlinear updates and downstream interactions remain. Q4 retains 40%, which is “mixed” under the preregistered thresholds, contradicting the blanket statement about “the Q nets.” Equalization supports a substantial role for relative attention in Q6/D6; identifying the complementary value computation needs the unperformed value swaps/replacement tests.

4. MEDIUM: Probes establish accessibility, not use or first computation. Episode-held-out evaluation and trained/untrained comparisons are useful. But L0 already decodes item means at R²≈0.645 and residuals at ≈0.545. A token-local nonlinear baseline using y, y² and token features, plus source-spread baselines, is missing. Row-shuffled targets destroy within-episode/source dependence and provide a weaker null than the preregistered grouped shuffle. Pooled attention-versus-|beta| correlation can reflect query-item relevance without precision weighting; test within relevant quotes against relevance-only controls. Nothing intervenes specifically on the decoded bias, residual² or precision representation. Loss of EV patch effectiveness also means diminished downstream influence, not literal disappearance of information.
