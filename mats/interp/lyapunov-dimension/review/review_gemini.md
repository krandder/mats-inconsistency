### 1. Audit of Numbered Mathematical Results (Ordered by Severity)

1. **Corollary C1 (Lyapunov exponents of a single ReLU path) — WRONG as stated (FIXABLE GAP with conditioning and re-indexing)**
   - **Divergent expectation & almost-sure extinction at fixed $n$:** Because $m_l \sim \text{Bin}(n, 1/2)$ is random, $\mathbb{P}(m_l \le j) = \sum_{r=0}^j \binom{n}{r} 2^{-n} > 0$ for every $j \ge 0$ at fixed $n$.
     - Analytically, $\psi((m - j)/2)$ has poles at $m = j, j-2, j-4, \dots$ and is undefined/complex for $m < j$ odd, so $\mathbb{E}[\psi((m-j)/2)]$ does not exist for $m \sim \text{Bin}(n, 1/2)$ (even at $j=0$, $m=0$ hits the pole $\psi(0) = -\infty$).
     - Dynamically, as $N \to \infty$ at fixed $n$, Borel–Cantelli implies $\min_{1 \le l \le N} m_l = 0$ almost surely; once $m_l - 1 < j$, $\text{rank}(H_l) < j$ and $s_j(\hat{J}) = 0$ for all subsequent layers, so $\lim_{N\to\infty} \frac{1}{N}\log s_j = -\infty$ a.s.
   - **Bartlett degrees-of-freedom & index mismatch:** An orthonormal $k$-frame $Q$ in $\mathbb{R}^{m_{l-1}-1}$ exists only on $\{m_{l-1} - 1 \ge k\}$. There, $H_l Q$ is $(m_l - 1) \times k$ iid $N(0, 2/n)$, whose $i$-th Bartlett diagonal ($i = 1, \dots, k$) is $(2/n)\chi^2_{(m_l - 1) - i + 1} = (2/n)\chi^2_{m_l - i}$. This gives $\frac{1}{2}[\log(4/n) + \mathbb{E}\psi((m - i)/2)]$ for the $i$-th **angular** exponent $\lambda_i(\hat{J})$ ($i \ge 1$). Since the radial exponent $\frac{1}{2}[\log(4/n) + \mathbb{E}\psi(m/2)]$ ($i=0$) sits on top as $\lambda_1(J)$, the $j$-th exponent of $J$ is the $(j-1)$-th angular exponent, with degrees of freedom $m - j + 1$, not $m - j$.
   - **Fix:** Condition the process on the survival event $\{m_l > k \text{ for all } l\}$ (or state as a formal $n \to \infty$ expansion after $N \to \infty$), and shift the index of $\lambda_j(J)$ to $m - j + 1$ for $j \ge 1$.

2. **Corollary C2 (Finite-depth counts $N_{\text{surv}}(J; \delta)$) — FIXABLE GAP**
   - **Missing $k=1$ upper bound in rank-one interlacing:** Rank-one interlacing for $J^T J = \hat{J}^T \hat{J} + r r^T$ gives $s_k(\hat{J}) \le s_k(J) \le s_{k-1}(\hat{J})$ for $k \ge 2$, but at $k=1$ only gives $s_1(\hat{J})^2 \le s_1(J)^2 \le s_1(\hat{J})^2 + \|r\|^2$. Because $N_{\text{surv}}(J; \delta) = \#\{k : s_k(J) \ge \delta s_1(J)\}$ normalizes by $s_1(J)$, interlacing of $s_k$ for $k \ge 2$ does **not** imply $N_{\text{surv}}(J; \delta) / N_{\text{surv}}(\hat{J}; \delta) \to 1$ unless one also proves $s_1(J)/s_1(\hat{J}) \to 1$ (i.e., $\|r\| / s_1(\hat{J}) \to 0$, or that the radial direction does not create an outlier above the Fuss–Catalan edge $x_+$).
   - **Unjustified interchange of limits at fixed $L'/n = \tau$:** The Fuss–Catalan law $\nu_{L'}(\delta)$ and the cited strong edge convergence (Nait Saada & Naderi 2024, proven for deterministic square matrices) hold as $n \to \infty$ at **fixed** $L'$, and $G(\delta)$ then takes $L' \to \infty$ ($1 \ll L' \ll n$). When $L' = \tau n$ grows proportionally to $n$, $N_{\text{surv}}(J; \delta) \sim G(\delta)/(2\tau)$ is an $O(1)$ discrete count of extreme singular values at the spectral edge (where the bulk law collapses by Hanin & Jiang 2025, and $N_{\text{surv}}$ is a non-self-averaging random integer).
   - **Fix:** Prove $\|r\|^2 / s_1(\hat{J})^2 = o_P(1)$ as $n \to \infty$ at fixed $L'$ (and extend strong edge convergence to rectangular products with binomial dimensions $m_l \sim \text{Bin}(n, 1/2)$); reclassify the proportional-depth application ($L' = \tau n$) as an asymptotic heuristic rather than a corollary of the fixed-$L'$ free limit.

3. **Theorem A (Exact error identity and $\chi^2$ bound) — FIXABLE GAP (attainment claim is WRONG for unbounded $F - F_B$)**
   - **Identity and bound:** Correct (assuming $F \in L^2(P)$ and $Q \ll P$ fibrewise, which follows from $\chi^2_B(Q,P) < \infty$). Note that the proof's "Cauchy–Schwarz fibre by fibre" actually requires a second Cauchy–Schwarz across the fibres $b = B^T z$, or a single global Cauchy–Schwarz in $L^2(P)$ using $\mathbb{E}_P[(dQ/dP - 1)^2] = \chi^2_B(Q, P)$.
   - **Counterexample to attainment:** Equality in global Cauchy–Schwarz holds only if $\frac{dQ}{dP}(z) - 1 = c \, u \cdot (F(z) - F_B(z))$ $P$-a.e. for a **global** constant $c > 0$ (not normalized fibre-by-fibre as written in `dQ_b/dP_b`, unless $\text{Var}_P(u \cdot F \mid B^T z)$ is constant across fibres). Moreover, for $Q$ to be a valid probability measure, we need $\frac{dQ}{dP} \ge 0$ $P$-a.e., which requires $\text{ess inf}_P \, u \cdot (F - F_B) > -\infty$. In the report's setting ($x \sim N(0, I_n)$ and piecewise-linear $F$), $u \cdot (F - F_B)$ is unbounded below on $P$, so for **every** $t > 0$, $1 + t \, u \cdot (F - F_B) / \|u \cdot (F - F_B)\|_{L^2(P)} < 0$ on a set of positive $P$-measure.
   - **Fix:** State that the bound is the **supremum** over the $\chi^2_B$-ball in general, and is **attained** for small $\chi^2_B$ only if $\text{ess inf}_P \, u \cdot (F - F_B) > -\infty$ (using the global $L^2(P)$ norm in the perturbation).

4. **Theorem B (Linear response and optimal block) — FIXABLE GAP**
   - **Missing zero-mean and invertibility hypotheses:** $\mathbb{E}[\Delta^T \bar{M} \Delta] = \text{tr}(\bar{M} \mathbb{E}[\Delta \Delta^T])$ equals $\text{tr}(\bar{M} \text{Cov } \Delta)$ only when $\mathbb{E}[\Delta] = 0$ (which holds for the linear-regression residual swap, though not for arbitrary couplings with $B^T \Delta = 0$). Also $B = \Sigma^{-1/2} V_k$ and $(B^T \Sigma B)^{-1}$ require $\Sigma \succ 0$ and $\text{rank}(B) = k$ (or pseudoinverses on $\text{range}(\Sigma)$ if dead neurons make $\Sigma$ singular).
   - **Vacuity of Condition (I):** Because $\bar{J} = \int_0^1 J(z_P + t\Delta)\,dt$ is a function of $(z_P, \Delta)$ and the regression residual $r = z_P - \hat{z}_B$ is only uncorrelated with (not independent of) $B^T z_P$ when $z_P$ is non-Gaussian, $\Delta$ cannot be strictly independent of $\bar{J}$ unless $J$ is constant.
   - **Fix:** Add $\mathbb{E}[\Delta] = 0$ and $\Sigma \succ 0$ (or restrict to $\text{range}(\Sigma)$), and state Condition (I) + $\bar{M} \approx M$ explicitly as a small-$\Delta$ first-order Taylor approximation rather than an exact independence hypothesis.

5. **Two-Replica Wick Recursion (Section 5) — FIXABLE GAP (1-step Wick formulas CORRECT; solved PR denominator off by a factor of 2 in the constant term)**
   - **One-step Wick formulas:** With $W_{ij} \stackrel{\text{iid}}{\sim} N(0, v)$ independent of $(D, D', Y)$ and $Z = D W Y W^T D'$, the 4th-moment Wick contractions yield
     $$\mathbb{E}\|Z\|_F^2 = v^2 [p p' \|Y\|_F^2 + o((\text{tr } Y)^2 + \text{tr } Y^2)],$$
     $$\mathbb{E}(\text{tr } Z)^2 = v^2 [o^2 (\text{tr } Y)^2 + o(\|Y\|_F^2 + \text{tr } Y^2)], \quad \mathbb{E}\,\text{tr } Z^2 = v^2 [o^2 \text{tr } Y^2 + o((\text{tr } Y)^2 + \|Y\|_F^2)].$$
     All three formulas are **exact** under the annealed approximation, and $\text{tr}(M^2) = \mathbb{E}_{x,x'}\|J_x J_{x'}^T\|_F^2 = \mathbb{E}\|Y_{L'}\|_F^2$ is exact.
   - **Error in the solved Participation Ratio:** At the cut $l=0$, $Y_0 = D_0 D'_0$ has $b_0 = (\text{tr } Y_0)^2 = o_0^2 = \frac{n^2}{4} r_0^2 = O(n^2)$, whereas $c_0 = \text{tr}(Y_0^2) = o_0 = \frac{n}{2} r_0 = O(n)$ and $a_0 = \|Y_0\|_F^2 = \frac{n}{2} r_0 = O(n)$. Iterating $b_l = r_l^2 b_{l-1} + O(n)$ and $c_l = r_l^2 c_{l-1} + O(n)$ shows that $c_{l-1} = O(n)$ is subleading to $b_{l-1} = O(n^2)$, so $a_l = a_{l-1} + \frac{2 r_l}{n}(b_{l-1} + c_{l-1})$ increments by $\frac{n}{2} r_l \prod_{m=0}^{l-1} r_m^2 + O(1)$ (not twice that). Dividing $(\text{tr } M)^2 \approx (n/2)^2$ by $a_{L'}$ gives
     $$\text{PR}(M) \sim \frac{n/2}{r_0 + r_0^2 \sum_{l=1}^{L'} r_l \prod_{m=1}^{l-1} r_m^2} = \frac{n}{2/r_0 + 2 r_0 \sum_{l=1}^{L'} r_l \prod_{m=1}^{l-1} r_m^2},$$
     and for one path ($r_l \equiv 1$), $\text{PR}(J^T J) \sim \frac{n/2}{1 + L'} = \frac{n}{2 + 2L'}$ (matching the 2nd moment $1 + L'$ of $FC_{L'}$ on $n/2$ active dimensions), **not** $\frac{n}{1 + 2L'}$.
   - **Fix:** Replace $1 + 2L'$ by $2 + 2L'$ (and $1$ by $2/r_0$ in $\text{PR}(M)$).

6. **Theorem C (Single-path reduction to half-width Gaussian product) — FIXABLE GAP (core argument CORRECT)**
   - **Scrutiny checks that pass:**
     - *Dependence on $g_l$ and conditional independence of $H_l$:* Provided $h_{l-1} \neq 0$, $W_l h_{l-1} = |h_{l-1}| g_l$ with $g_l = W_l e_{l-1}$, so $D_l, m_l, e_l$ depend on $W_l$ only through $g_l$. Conditioned on $\mathcal{F}_{l-1}$, $[e_{l-1}, B_{l-1}]$ has orthonormal columns, so $g_l = W_l e_{l-1}$ and $W_l B_{l-1}$ are independent iid $N(0, 2/n)$ matrices. Choosing $B_l$ as a deterministic measurable function of $g_l$, $H_l = B_l^T (W_l B_{l-1})$ is conditionally iid $N(0, 2/n)$ of size $(m_l - 1) \times (m_{l-1} - 1)$ given $(\mathcal{F}_{l-1}, g_l)$, and hence conditionally independent across $l = 1, \dots, N$ given $(m_0, \dots, m_N)$.
     - *Handling of $D_0$, rank-one decomposition, and interlacing:* Because $\text{range}(D_l) = \text{span}(e_l) \oplus^\perp \text{range}(B_l)$, the orthogonal projector $D_l$ decomposes as $D_l = e_l e_l^T + B_l B_l^T$ for all $l \ge 0$. At $l=0$, for any $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $D_0 v = (e_0^T v) e_0 + B_0 (B_0^T v)$. Inducting $D_l W_l (\beta_{l-1} e_{l-1} + B_{l-1} c_{l-1}) = \beta_l e_l + B_l H_l c_{l-1}$ yields $J = e_N r^T + \hat{J}$ with $\hat{J} = B_N H_N \dots H_1 B_0^T$ and $e_N^T \hat{J} = 0$, so $J^T J = \hat{J}^T \hat{J} + r r^T$ and Weyl's rank-one interlacing $s_k(\hat{J}) \le s_k(J) \le s_{k-1}(\hat{J})$ holds.
   - **Gaps to fix:**
     1. *Zero-mortality event:* At each step $\mathbb{P}(m_l = 0 \mid m_{l-1} \ge 1) = 2^{-n} > 0$. If $m_l = 0$, $h_l = 0$, $e_l = h_l/|h_l|$ is undefined, and $m_{l+1} = \dots = m_N = 0$ deterministically, so $m_1, \dots, m_N$ are **not** unconditionally iid $\text{Bin}(n, 1/2)$ (and if $m_l = 1$, $H_l$ has dimension $0$, making $\hat{J} = 0$).
     2. *Differentiability at the cut:* Requires $z_i \neq 0$ for all $i$ (or fixing the convention $1[0 > 0] = 0$ as right-derivative) and choosing $B_l$ measurably in $g_l$ alone.

7. **Theorem D (Input averaging and class partitioning) — CORRECT (minor boundary caveat)**
   - The Ky Fan variational inequality $\sum_{j>k} \lambda_j(M) \ge \mathbb{E}_x \sum_{j>k} s_j(J_x)^2$ and its class-partitioned extension are exact.
   - *Minor caveat:* The "only if almost every path has the same top-$k$ right singular subspace" uniqueness statement requires $s_k(J_x) > s_{k+1}(J_x)$ on a set of positive measure (when $\text{rank}(J_x) < k$, any $k$-plane containing $\text{row}(J_x)$ is optimal).

8. **Edge Law $G(\delta)$ (Section 3) — CORRECT**
   - From Haagerup–Møller (2013), $x(p)\rho(x(p)) = \frac{\sin((N+1)p)\sin p}{\pi \sin(Np)}$. Substituting $p = \theta/(N+1)$ for $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ gives $\frac{x(p)}{N+1} = \frac{\sin\theta}{(N+1)\sin(\theta/(N+1))} \left(\cos\frac{\theta}{N+1} - \cot\theta\sin\frac{\theta}{N+1}\right)^{-N} \to \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta} e^{\theta\cot\theta} = f(\theta)$ with $f(0^+) = e = \lim_{N\to\infty} x_+/(N+1)$, and $\rho(x)\,dx = x(p)\rho(x(p))|d\log x(p)| = \frac{\theta}{\pi(N+1)}|(\log f)'(\theta)|\,d\theta\,(1 + O(1/N))$ uniformly on $[0, \theta_\delta]$ for $\theta_\delta < \pi$.

### 2. Audit of Status Labels (Section 11)

- **Overstated labels that must be downgraded or qualified:**
  - **Corollary C1 (`PROVEN`):** Too strong. Should be **`PROVEN UNDER non-extinction conditioning` (or `ASYMPTOTIC` as $n \to \infty$)** once the index shift $m - j + 1$ is fixed.
  - **Corollary C2 (`PROVEN UNDER the free limit for the half-width product`):** Too strong when applied to fixed $L'/n = \tau$ (Summary #1, Sections 3, 4, 9, 10). Even at fixed $L'$, it needs $s_1(J)/s_1(\hat{J}) \to 1$; at fixed $L'/n$, where $N_{\text{surv}} = O(1)$, it is an **`ASYMPTOTIC HEURISTIC`**, not a proven limit theorem.
  - **Theorem A (`PROVEN`):** Attainment needs **`PROVEN UNDER essential boundedness below`** (otherwise the bound is a supremum).
  - **Theorem C (`PROVEN for sigma = 0`):** Needs the qualifier **`on the non-extinction event m_l >= 1`**.
  - **Theorem B (`the bound PROVEN UNDER independence (I)`):** Because (I) cannot hold literally for non-constant $J(z_P + t\Delta)$, label the bound **`FIRST-ORDER APPROXIMATION`** (the linear-algebra optimization of $\text{tr}(M\,\text{Schur}_B)$ is **`PROVEN`**).
- **Properly calibrated labels:** Theorem D (`PROVEN`), Edge law $G(\delta)$ (`PROVEN` in the sequential limit $n \to \infty$ then $N \to \infty$), Two-replica formula (`HEURISTIC (annealed)`), empirical scaling $\text{nMSE} = C \cdot \text{tail}^\beta$ (`MEASURED`), class-count extrapolation (`HEURISTIC`), and Section 10.2–10.3 (`CONJECTURE`).

### 3. Do the Conclusions in the Summary and Section 10 Follow?

- **Summary #1 & Section 10.1 (per-path count):** **Follows quantitatively, with a rigor caveat.** Theorem C + the edge law $G(\delta)$ genuinely explain why single-path surviving counts are $\approx n G(\delta)/(2L')$ (24 vs. Newman's 75 at $256 \times 64$, $L'=32$), but the $O(1)$ scaling at fixed $L'/n$ is an accurate continuum-edge approximation to discrete top singular values rather than a proven joint-limit theorem.
- **Summary #2 & Section 7 (failure of fixed and class-conditional blocks):** **Follows.** Theorem D proves averaging cannot help, and the empirical class measurements ($K=64$) plus the two-replica coherence scale (~6–9 layers) substantiate why coarse input partitioning fails.
- **Summary #3 & Section 10.1 (single-cut and composed block sizes):** **Follows empirically, not purely deductively.** The optimal block $B = \Sigma^{-1/2} V_k$ follows from Theorem B's linear algebra, while the numerical dimension requirements (20–60 single-cut, 100–130 composed) rest on the **measured** $\beta = 1$ scaling law (Section 6) and empirical layer-composition runs rather than a proven bound (since $\epsilon \sim 0.2\text{--}0.6$ is well outside the pathwise linear-response regime $\epsilon \lesssim 10^{-2}$).
- **Summary #4 & Section 10.3 ("nothing beats sampling without closed-form structure"):** **Plausible conjecture, not established by Theorems A–D.** Theorems A and B analyze worst-case $\chi^2$ perturbations and linear-regression residual scrambles of a marginal block $B^T z_c$; they do not rule out carrying nonlinear sufficient statistics $F_B(z)$ directly or non-local estimators that avoid layer-by-layer marginal re-projection (as Section 10.3 rightly notes at the end).

### Verdict

**TL;DR:** The core single-path reduction (Theorem C), input-averaging inequality (Theorem D), Fuss–Catalan edge law $G(\delta)$, and one-step Wick formulas are **sound**, and the main quantitative conclusions hold. Four mathematical fixes are required before publication: (1) **Corollary C1** ignores binomial extinction $\mathbb{P}(m_l \le j) > 0$ (making $\mathbb{E}\psi((m-j)/2)$ undefined at fixed $n$) and shifts the angular index $j$ by 1; (2) **Corollary C2** needs $s_1(J)/s_1(\hat{J}) \to 1$ to close the $k=1$ interlacing gap and is a heuristic when $L' \propto n$; (3) **Theorem A**'s bound is a supremum (not attained) for unbounded $F - F_B$; and (4) the solved **PR denominator** in Section 5 is $2 + 2L'$ (not $1 + 2L'$). No decision needed from Kelvin; apply these four corrections and downgrade the C1/C2 status labels accordingly.
