# Provenance

Working notes and code of the explicit mesa-optimizer toy studies of 10 November 2026, from the MATS working
folder `mats/work/deep-study/`: `wf13/` (language-model pilot, full-loop test, consistency study),
`wf12/amplified-gflownet/` (exact table world) and `wf7/sudoku/` (search over a learned world model). Each
study has a `check/` folder with the independent rerun. Copied on 2026-11-14. The earlier theory notes of
9 November were discarded by the author and are not included. Left out: checkpoints, arrays, files over 5 MB.
