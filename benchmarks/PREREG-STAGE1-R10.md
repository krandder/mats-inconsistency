## 0. Global Pre-Registration Governance, Seed Protocol & Bitter-Lesson Constant Ledger

### 0.1 Locked Statistical Protocol (`Seeds 101, 102, 103`)
1. **Seeds:** Every experiment in Stage 1 (`R10-E1`, `R10-E2`, `R10-E3`, `R10-E4`) is evaluated across **three fixed, independent random seeds**:
   $$\mathcal{S}_{\text{R10}} \;=\; \{101,\; 102,\; 103\}.$$
   Each seed controls (i) training/calibration board generation and permutation sampling, (ii) parameter initialization, and (iii) test-time tree rollout / Gumbel-top-$k$ sampling streams.
2. **Paired 3-Seed Confidence Intervals:** For any scalar metric $M(s)$ or paired treatment-vs-control contrast $\Delta(s) = M_{\text{treat}}(s) - M_{\text{ctrl}}(s)$ across $s \in \{101, 102, 103\}$, let
   $$\bar{\Delta} \;=\; \frac{1}{3}\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{R10}}} \Delta(s), \qquad \mathrm{SE}(\Delta) \;=\; \sqrt{\frac{1}{3 \times 2}\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{R10}}} \big(\Delta(s) - \bar{\Delta}\big)^2}.$$
   The locked two-sided $95\%$ Student-$t$ confidence interval ($\mathrm{df} = 2$, $t_{0.975, 2} = 4.30265 \approx \mathbf{4.303}$) is:
   $$\mathrm{CI}_{95\%}(\Delta) \;=\; \Big[\, \bar{\Delta} - 4.303 \cdot \mathrm{SE}(\Delta), \;\; \bar{\Delta} + 4.303 \cdot \mathrm{SE}(\Delta) \,\Big].$$
3. **Data Split Separation (Zero Leakage):**
   Every environment instance generator uses disjoint deterministic seed offsets per split:
   - `split="train"`: `seed_offset = +0` ($N_{\text{train}} = 4{,}000$ boards/episodes per seed).
   - `split="cal"` (divided $50/50$ into `cal_fit` and `cal_val` for aggregator/gate calibration): `seed_offset = +10_000` ($N_{\text{cal}} = 2{,}000$ boards: $1{,}000$ `cal_fit` + $1{,}000$ `cal_val`).
   - `split="test"`: `seed_offset = +20_000` ($N_{\text{test}} = 500$ held-out boards/episodes per seed, $1{,}500$ pooled across 3 seeds).
4. **Compute Accounting Unit (`WTE`):**
   Search budget $B \in \{0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 64, 128\}$ is measured in **World-Model Token Evaluations (`WTE`)**, where $B = 0$ is the single-pass root evaluation $C_0$ and each step $b \to b+1$ executes one `SELECT_NODE(j)` + `STEP(v, z)` single-step KV-cache page extension in `TreePagedKVBank`.

## 1. Study `R10-E1` (`E-AUX-QUERY`): Bidirectional Latent Lemma Space & Direct Combinatorial Query Rollouts

### 1.1 Scientific Objective & Target Failure Modes
`R10-E1` tests **Convergence 4** (`ROUND1-SCOREBOARD.md` §2.4 & `02-bidirectional-aux-and-memory.md` Theorem 1):
1. **The Equivalence Barrier in Marginal-Only Pooling:** On symmetric relational problems where $P^*(X_A = 1 \mid x_O) = P^*(X_B = 1 \mid x_O) = 0.5$ but a multi-step hidden constraint chain forces $X_A \equiv X_B$, opposite-sign sibling branches ($A=0 \implies B=0$ vs. $A=1 \implies B=1$) cancel to zero in first-order variable marginals ($\frac{1}{2}(+\Lambda) + \frac{1}{2}(-\Lambda) = 0$).
2. **Even-Parity Bilinear Lemma Emission & Structural Partition:** The expanded consensus space $D_{\text{amp}} = D_V + D_{\text{aux}}$ ($D_{\text{amp}} \gg |V|$) partitions $Z_{\text{aux}} = Z^W_{\text{aux}} \oplus Z^\Pi_{\text{aux}}$ (`LIRMask.epistemic_belief()` $\chi = (0,1)$ on $Z^W_{\text{aux}}$; `LIRMask.policy_search()` $\chi = (1,1)$ on $Z^\Pi_{\text{aux}}$, masked out from belief decoding). `BilinearAuxLemmaEmitter` projects parent-relative branch shifts $\Delta \ell_V$ via the even bilinear co-fluctuation $(U^\top \Delta \ell_V) \odot (V^\top \Delta \ell_V)$ into $Z^W_{\text{aux}}$, writing identical positive lemma codes on both sibling branches, and trains under the random-permutation loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{any-order}}(h_{\text{cons}}; x^*)$ plus VICReg variance-covariance anti-collapse regularization.
3. **Direct Combinatorial Query Rollouts (`Q(A, B)`):** `CombinatorialQueryHead` maps a relational query $Q(A, B)$ (`QueryType.EQUAL`: $\mathbb{I}[X_A = X_B]$) to an initial prospectus direction $p_0 = k_{Q(A,B)} \in \mathbb{S}^{D_{\text{amp}}-1}$ that steers tree search to fork on $\{A, B\}$, and reads out $\hat{P}_{\text{direct}}(Q(A, B) \mid x_O, h_{\text{cons}})$ alongside the 2-step autoregressive equivalence readout $\hat{P}_{\text{AR}}(X_A = X_B \mid x_O, h_{\text{cons}}) = \sum_z q_\theta(X_A=z \mid x_O, h_{\text{cons}})\, q_\theta(X_B=z \mid x_O, X_A=z, h_{\text{cons}})$ with self-consistency discrepancy $\delta_{\text{rel}}(A, B) = |\hat{P}_{\text{direct}} - \hat{P}_{\text{AR}}|$.

### 1.2 Environments, Datasets & Compute Budgets

   - Includes both **within-clique** and **cross-clique / hard-negative** query pairs $Q(A, B)$ with balanced marginal equivalence base rates ($50\%$ `EQUAL`, $50\%$ `NOT_EQUAL`).
2. **`sudoku_6x6` (`GAME_ID = 20`, $|V| = 36, K_{\text{dom}} = 6$, $2 \times 3$ boxes):**

## 2. Study `R10-E2` (`E-KV-TREE-CLEARING`): $80\%$-Shared-Prefix Tree Deduplication, Disjunctive Bayes Mixture & Strong-Root Monotonicity at $M_0 = 32$ and Literal $M_0 = 34$

### 2.1 Scientific Objective & Target Failure Modes
`R10-E2` directly executes the pre-registered falsifiers **`RT-D1`** and **`RT-D2`** (`03-redteam-and-monotonicity-proofs.md` §1.1–1.2, §2 Fixes 1–2, §4 Table 1; `ROUND1-SCOREBOARD.md` §2.1 & §2.5):
1. **Failure Mode 2A ($80\%$-Shared-Prefix Multi-Counting vs. Orthogonal Leaf Crushing):** When $M$ tree leaves $C_j$ share an $80\%$ prefix ($8$ of $10$ steps) up to ancestor $C_a = \mathrm{LCA}$, root-relative shifts $\Delta \ell_j = \ell(C_j) - \ell(C_0)$ have key cosine $\langle k_j, k_{j'} \rangle \approx 0.94$ even when the $20\%$ post-fork increments $\delta_j = \ell(C_j) - \ell(C_a)$ inspect disjoint cells. Root-relative `K3` normal equations either multi-count the prefix (if unpreconditioned) or crush the $20\%$ orthogonal leaf discovery by dividing the entire root-relative vector by $M+1$. `TwoStageTreeClearer` resolves this via **parent-relative telescopic edge increments** $\delta_j = \ell(C_j) - \ell(C_{\mathrm{pa}(j)})$, bag-centered edge-salience keys $k^{\text{edge}}_j$, canonical assignment-set transposition merging $\mathrm{canon}(O_j)$, and **Gershgorin Row-Crowding Preconditioning** $D_{\text{crowd}, r} = \lambda(\beta_r) + \sum_{r'=0}^b \beta_{r'} \max(0, \langle k^{\text{edge}}_r, k^{\text{edge}}_{r'} \rangle)$.
2. **Failure Mode 2B (Disjunctive Case-Splits vs. Conjunctive Log-Odds Pooling — `R5-E2` Piece-Count Veto):** Clamping mutually exclusive piece hypotheses ($V_v = \text{Marshal}$ vs. $V_v = \text{Flag}$) at a branch point and pooling their log-odds conjunctively caused `R5-E2` to assign $p < 0.001$ to the true piece on $21.4\%$ of claims (blowing up KL `0.047 -> 0.539`). Stage 1 of `TwoStageTreeClearer` applies the **Bottom-Up Counterfactual Bayes Mixture** over explored children $\mathrm{Ch}(a, v)$ plus unexplored complement mass $U_{a,v} = 1 - \sum_{j \in \mathrm{Ch}(a,v)} q_{C_a}(z_j)$ before Stage 2 conjunctive clearing.
3. **Failure Mode 1 (Strong-Root Degradation at $M_0 = 32$ and Literal $M_0 = 34$ — `R9-E1` Mode B Disclosure):** In `R9-E1`, under 6-cell local Mode B reads on `shiphunt_6x6`, non-anchored pools degraded $M_0 = 32$ by $+0.0437$ to $+0.1761$ nats, and under literal strong-root selection $M_0 = 34$ ($\mathrm{KL}(0) = 0.0169$), softmax attention weights in `looped_attn_T3` violated the $+0.0005$ step-rise bar (`+0.00062` in Seed 102) with $\mathrm{KL}(0) - \mathrm{KL}(16)$ CI crossing zero (`[-0.0004, +0.0010]`). Stage 3 of `TwoStageTreeClearer` enforces the **Nested Precision-Gated Residual Update** $\ell^{(b+1)} = \ell^{(b)} + \alpha^{(b+1)}(\tilde{\ell}^{(b+1)} - \ell^{(b)})$ with convex gain certificate $\Gamma_b$.
