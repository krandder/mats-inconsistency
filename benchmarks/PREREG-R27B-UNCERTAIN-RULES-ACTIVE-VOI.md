## 1. Research Question

1. **Rule-Label-Free Partial-Trajectory World Model Self-Supervised on Simulator Hidden State (`D1`):** When an environment's transition/sensor rule $R_{\text{rule}} \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ is **unknown at episode start** and **never passed as a label during SGD training** (`sgd_rule_label_access_counts == 0` across all 6 neural arms; `WatchedLabelArray` asserts `access_count == 0` before and after `train_model_rule_label_free`), can a pure-neural world model (`PureNeuralRuleWorldNet`) trained self-supervised on simulator hidden-state and clue trajectories $(z_{\text{tr}}, c_{\text{tr}})$ discover the 4 underlying 2D relative-offset sensor stencils $K_\theta^{(r)}(\Delta r, \Delta c)$, 2D slot occupancy geometry, and conditional clue distributions via Sinkhorn EM (`Phase 1`, followed by `1` calibration board per rule from `train_idx` with `0` gradient steps to break $S_4$ slot-index permutation symmetry) + 3-step Bethe / Iterative Proportional Fitting (`IPF`) log-odds propagation (`Phase 2`), beating `Control_Uniform`, `Control_TrainMean`, `Control_ZeroParam_LLR`, `Control_Helper_Stencil_Only` (which receives the ground-truth `neigh_masks` directly!), `Control_Random_Network`, `Control_Untrained_Init`, and `Ablation_NoRuleSlots` on identical 5-cell observation masks — **both overall and on each environment (`shiphunt_6x6` and `minesweeper_6x6`) individually**?
2. **Learned Neural Value-of-Information (`VoI`) Probing (`D2`):** Can a learned neural VoI head (`self.voi_head`, `nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(20, 48), nn.GELU(), nn.Linear(48, 1))`) trained via self-supervised Law-of-Total-Variance regression on slot clue variance $\mathrm{Var}_r(\hat{c}_v^{(r)})$, slot occupancy variance $\mathrm{Var}_r(\hat{p}_{z,v}^{(r)})$, expected unrevealed stencil disagreement variance $\sum_u \mathrm{Var}_r(K_{\theta,vu}^{(r)})\hat{\sigma}_{z,u}^2$, and revealed-cell stencil disagreement variance $\sum_{u \in \text{rev}} \mathrm{Var}_r(K_{\theta,vu}^{(r)})(c_u + 3z_u + 1)$ actively select $K=4$ probes from 1 initial revealed cell that reduce true posterior rule entropy $H(p^*(R_{\text{rule}} \mid \text{obs}_K))$ by $\ge 25\%$ relative to `Control_Uniform` and by $\ge 15\%$ relative to both `Ablation_NoRuleSlots` and `Control_Helper_Stencil_Only`, achieving $\ge 80\%$ top-1 rule identification accuracy — **both overall and on each environment (`shiphunt_6x6` and `minesweeper_6x6`) individually**?
3. **By-Construction Structural LIR Firewall vs. Genuine Learned On-Policy Trajectory Bias (`D3`):** Under realistic utility-biased trajectory exploration (`u_sel_m`, where $U=+1$ trajectories sample occupied cells more frequently and $U=-1$ trajectories sample empty cells more frequently, with the identical `0.60 * z_res` residual scale across all 4 modes), `Ablation_Fused_Utility` and `Ablation_Token_Masked_Utility` couple their latent state beliefs to $U$, exhibiting genuine learned belief drift (`mean_belief_jsd >= 0.004`) under $\text{do}(U=+1 \leftrightarrow U=-1)$ on both environments. In contrast, `Inconsistency_ActiveVoI_LIR` ($\chi_{\text{world}} = (1, 0), \chi_{\text{policy}} = (0, 1)$, where $U$ never enters the world trunk and policy gradients are blocked via `.detach()`) guarantees **exact `0.0` belief JSD by construction** (`max_belief_jsd_ours <= 1e-7`) while its learned policy head (`self.pol_head`, `nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(15, 48), nn.GELU(), nn.Linear(48, 1))`) achieves `utility_seek_score >= 0.35`, `utility_avoid_score >= 0.80` ($\ge 85\%$ of exact Bayes ceiling), and `utility_action_reversal_gap >= 0.30` (beating `Ablation_NoRuleSlots` with `ci95_low > 0`) — **both overall and on each environment (`shiphunt_6x6` and `minesweeper_6x6`) individually**.

## 2. Exhaustive Synthetic Environments, Verified Exact Bayesian Oracle, Board-Disjoint Splits & Zero Rule-Label Leakage (`PROTOCOL.md` §§3–5, §§9.1–9.8)

1. **Exhaustively Enumerated Finite Synthetic Hypothesis Spaces (`ExhaustiveUncertainRuleEnv`, `lines 88-213`, explicitly labelled `synthetic` per `PROTOCOL.md` §3):**
   - `shiphunt_6x6`: 4 rule variants (`0`: horizontal $1\times 3$ pairs + range-2 horizontal radar; `1`: vertical $3\times 1$ pairs + range-2 vertical sonar; `2`: $2\times 2 + 1\times 2$ + King-1 neighborhood; `3`: diagonal pairs + X-diagonal range-2 sensor). All `802` valid board configurations across the 4 rules are enumerated with zero truncation.
   - `minesweeper_6x6`: 4 sensor rule variants (`0`: von Neumann 4-neighbor; `1`: Moore 8-neighbor; `2`: Knight's-move 8-neighbor; `3`: Distance-2 cross+diagonal sensor). All `360` valid configurations (`90` unique $6\times 6$ mine boards $\times 4$ rules) are enumerated with zero truncation.
2. **Exact Bayesian Oracle Verified Against Brute-Force Enumeration (`verify_oracle_exactness`, `lines 309-346`, `PROTOCOL.md` §9.6):**
   - `exact_joint_bayes()` (`lines 215-230`) computes the exact indicator posterior over all hypotheses $\mathcal{H} = \bigcup_{r=0}^3 \mathcal{H}_r$ (`z_match & c_match`, zero soft exponential approximation).
   - Before training begins, `verify_oracle_exactness()` checks `exact_joint_bayes()` against an independent brute-force loop over all hypotheses across `60` episodes (`30` per environment) and verifies `max_tv_z <= 1e-6` (`5.96e-8` in smoke) and `max_tv_rule <= 1e-6` (`0.0` in smoke), recorded in `results["oracle_check"]`.
3. **Strictly Disjoint `train_idx`, `smoke_idx`, and `test_idx` Splits — Both by Hypothesis Index AND by Underlying $6\times 6$ State Board $z$ (`lines 845-907`, `PROTOCOL.md` §9.8):**
   - With fixed seed `27200`, on `minesweeper_6x6` the `90` underlying $6\times 6$ mine boards are partitioned first into `60` boards (`240` hypotheses) for `train_idx`, `10` boards (`40` hypotheses) for `smoke_idx`, and `20` boards (`80` hypotheses) for `test_idx`, so zero $z$-boards are shared across splits even under different rules. On `shiphunt_6x6`, the `55%` per-rule training split (`440` hypotheses) receives all 5 cross-rule colliding $z$-boards while `smoke_idx` (`40` hypotheses, `10` per rule) and `test_idx` (`80` hypotheses, `20` per rule) are drawn from strictly unique $z$-boards.
   - `disjointness_checks` verifies `0` overlap across all **12** checks (`train_test_overlap`, `smoke_test_overlap`, `train_smoke_overlap`, `board_z_train_test_overlap`, `board_z_smoke_test_overlap`, `board_z_train_smoke_overlap` for both `shiphunt_6x6` and `minesweeper_6x6`).
4. **Rule-Label-Free SGD Training Self-Supervised on Simulator Hidden State (`WatchedLabelArray`, `lines 34-46`, `654-718`, `957-966`):**
   - During training, `train_model_rule_label_free()` supervises hidden-state predictions via BCE on simulator hidden state $z_{\text{tr}}$ and clues $c_{\text{tr}}$, while `env.hypotheses_rule` is wrapped in `WatchedLabelArray` (`lines 34-46`) and asserted to have `watched_rules.access_count == 0` both before and after `train_model_rule_label_free()` (`sgd_rule_label_access_counts == 0` across all 6 neural arms).
   - Because rule-label-free 4-slot clustering is invariant up to the $4! = 24$ permutations of slot indices $S_4$, after Phase 1 Sinkhorn EM completes, `train_model_rule_label_free` uses `1` calibration board per rule from `train_idx` (`cal_idx`, `post_em_slot_alignment_episodes_per_rule == 1`, `lines 697-705`, with `torch.no_grad()` and `0` optimizer steps) solely to permute the 4 learned slots (`net.permute_slots(best_perm)`) into canonical order `0, 1, 2, 3` so `kl_rule` and `rule_top1_acc` can be evaluated against ground-truth rule indices `0..3`.

## 3. Mandatory Controls, Ablations & Ceiling (`PROTOCOL.md` §§3–4, §9.1)

- `Control_Uniform`: Predicts $0.5$ on unrevealed cells and $(0.25, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25)$ over rules; deterministic uniform probing (`lines 987-989`, `1040-1045`).
- `Control_TrainMean`: Predicts the empirical training-split cell marginal `train_mean_z` on unrevealed cells and uniform rules; its action score is `train_mean_z` under both $U=+1$ and $U=-1$, so `utility_action_reversal_gap == 0.0` (`lines 1046-1051`).
- `Control_ZeroParam_LLR`: Zero-parameter local King-neighborhood log-likelihood ratio readout (`lines 1052-1059`).
- `Control_Helper_Stencil_Only`: Non-neural analytical stencil helper (`helper_stencil_control_beliefs`, `lines 275-306`) using the environment's true `neigh_masks` with zero learned neural parameters (`PROTOCOL.md` §4).
- `Control_Random_Network`: A separate randomly initialized `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(seed + 9000)` that is never trained (`line 941`); every belief, probe (`out["probe_score"]`), and action (`out["act_logits"]`) is produced by running `net.forward()` (`lines 995-1004`, `1020-1029`, `1072-1091`).
- `Control_Untrained_Init`: `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(seed)` at initial weights before `train_model_rule_label_free` (`line 943`), evaluated through the identical `net.forward()` path.
- `Ablation_NoRuleSlots`: `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(mode="no_rule_slots")` trained with the exact same `train_model_rule_label_free` loop, but with rule slots collapsed to 1 shared kernel and 1 shared state slot (`lines 432-433`, `488-493`, `504-505`, `544-545`).
- `Ablation_Fused_Utility`: `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(mode="fused_utility")` where utility sign $U \in \{-1, +1\}$ is concatenated directly to the cell input channels (`in_dim = 4`, `lines 464-466`) without the LIR `.detach()` firewall (using the identical `0.60 * z_res` residual scale as `lir_separated`, `line 508`).
- `Ablation_Token_Masked_Utility`: `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(mode="token_masked_utility")` where $U$ is injected as 4 utility tokens in self-attention (`lines 472-476`) without the LIR `.detach()` firewall (using the identical `0.60 * z_res` residual scale as `lir_separated`, `line 508`).
- `Inconsistency_ActiveVoI_LIR` (**Ours**): `PureNeuralRuleWorldNet(mode="lir_separated")` with 4 rule-label-free learned relative-offset kernels, 3-step Bethe/IPF log-odds propagation, learned neural VoI head `self.voi_head`, learned action policy head `self.pol_head`, and structural LIR firewall ($\chi_{\text{world}} = (1, 0), \chi_{\text{policy}} = (0, 1)$, `lines 560-568`).
- `Ceiling_Exact_Joint_Bayes`: Exact Bayesian posterior `exact_joint_bayes` + exact 1-step Bayes-optimal VoI probe `exact_bayes_voi_probe` (`lines 215-273`).
