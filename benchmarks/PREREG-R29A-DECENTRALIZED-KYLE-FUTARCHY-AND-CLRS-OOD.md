## 1. Research Questions

1. **Zero-Global-Backprop Decentralized Kyle-Futarchy Market vs. Greedy Layerwise & Simultaneous LMSR (`D1`):** When $M=3$ specialist neural modules (`DecentralizedSpecialistModule`) observe strictly disjoint local inputs $x^{(m)}$ and communicate only through `.detach()` security quotes (`decentralized_cross_module_grad_edges == 0` verified at runtime by traversing `loss.grad_fn.next_functions`), can a **Sequential Hanson-LMSR + Kyle Correlation-Preconditioned Inconsistency Market (`Inconsistency_Kyle_Futarchy_LIR`)** overcome the zero-mutual-information multi-hop parity barrier ($I(x^{(m)}; y_k) = 0$) where `Ablation_Local_Greedy_Layerwise` fails and break the simultaneous Jacobi XOR cobweb oscillation and $3\times$ Sybil clone rent over-extraction where `Ablation_Naive_Simultaneous_LMSR` degrades — achieving accuracy $\ge 0.85$, Sybil clone profit invariance `max_sybil_profit_diff_ours <= 1e-7` (vs `>= 0.15` for `Ablation_Naive_Simultaneous_LMSR`), and beating all 4 controls (`Control_Chance_Floor`, `Control_Linear_Readout_Only`, `Control_Random_Network`, `Control_Untrained_Init`) and both ablations by $\ge +0.20$ accuracy (`ci95_low > 0`) as well as Brier and KL score (`ci95_low > 0`) on **both `multihop_3xor_equivsat` and `6x6latin_parity` individually**?
2. **Zero-Shot $4\times$ Out-of-Distribution (`OOD`) Graph Length Extrapolation (`D2`, $n \in [8, 16] \to n=64$):** Trained strictly on graphs of size $n \le 16$ (`d2_n_train_max == 16`) with `2` raw node channels (`[seed_polarity, normalized_in_degree]`) and `0` ground-truth hint features (`d2_hint_channels_in_input == 0`), can a recurrent message-passing network regularized by Local Inconsistency Resolution (`Inconsistency_Bellman_LIR_GNN`) extrapolate zero-shot to $n=32$ (disjoint smoke split in `--smoke-cpu`) and $n=64$ (disjoint $4\times$ OOD test split in the full GPU run) across `bfs_shortest_hop`, `bellman_ford_shortest_path`, and `2sat_implication_closure`, achieving OOD accuracy $\ge 0.85$ overall ($\ge 0.84$ per task), test-time fixed-point inconsistency energy $\mathcal{L}_{\text{inc}} \le 0.05$ (vs $\ge 1.0$ for `Ablation_Unregularized_Looped_GNN`), and beating `Control_Chance_Floor`, `Control_Degree_Distance_Heuristic`, `Control_Random_Network`, `Control_Untrained_Init`, fixed-depth `Baseline_Standard_MPNN` (our training, equal SGD steps), and `Ablation_Unregularized_Looped_GNN` with `ci95_low > 0` on **all 3 tasks individually**?

## 2. Synthetic Environments, Exact Oracle Checks, Disjoint Splits & Runtime Autograd Firewall (`PROTOCOL.md` §§3–5, §§9.1–9.9)

1. **Explicitly Labelled Synthetic Combinatorial & Graph Environments (`PROTOCOL.md` §3):**
   - `multihop_3xor_equivsat` (`synthetic`, `lines 124-146`): $M=3$ disjoint 4-bit blocks $b^{(m)} \in \{0, 1\}^4$ ($12$ bits total, sampled without replacement from the $2^{12} = 4096$ binary configurations) with local 3-XOR parities $z_m^{(0)} = b_0^{(m)} \oplus b_1^{(m)} \oplus b_2^{(m)}$, $z_m^{(1)} = b_1^{(m)} \oplus b_2^{(m)} \oplus b_3^{(m)}$ and 4 cross-module XOR/equivalence targets $y = (z_0^{(0)} \oplus z_1^{(0)},\; z_1^{(0)} \oplus z_2^{(0)},\; z_0^{(1)} \oplus z_2^{(1)},\; z_0^{(0)} \oplus z_1^{(1)} \oplus z_2^{(0)}) \in \{0, 1\}^4$.
   - `6x6latin_parity` (`synthetic`, `lines 148-188`): Valid $6\times 6$ Latin squares generated via row/column/symbol isotopy permutations (`latin_row_col_collisions == 0` verified across all generated squares), partitioned horizontally into $M=3$ disjoint $2\times 6$ strips where module $m$ observes only local strip cell features `2.0 * latin_bits - 1.0 + 0.05 * (cells - 2.5) / 2.5` from its own strip and predicts the 4 cross-strip parity securities.
   - `bfs_shortest_hop`, `bellman_ford_shortest_path`, `2sat_implication_closure` (`synthetic`, `lines 531-651`): Directed layered graphs with positive/negative seed terminals `(0, 2)` vs `(1, 3)` at Layer 0 and 4 nodes per layer ($\text{depth} = (n - 4)/4 + 1$), trained strictly at $n = 16$ (`64` graphs) and evaluated on disjoint smoke graphs at $n = 32$ (`32` graphs in `--smoke-cpu`) and disjoint zero-shot $4\times$ OOD test graphs at $n = 64$ (`48` graphs in full run).
2. **Exact Oracle Verification Against Independent Brute-Force Enumeration (`verify_oracle_exactness`, `lines 719-778`, `PROTOCOL.md` §9.6):**
   - Checks `multihop_3xor_equivsat` (`30` episodes) and `6x6latin_parity` (`30` episodes + all `640` Latin squares checked for row/column permutation validity) against elementwise integer XOR verification (`max_err == 0.0`, `latin_row_col_collisions == 0`).
   - Checks `exact_bfs_oracle` (`lines 654-673`), `exact_bellman_ford_oracle` (`lines 676-699`), and `exact_2sat_closure_oracle` (`lines 702-716`) across `25` episodes per task (`75` graph episodes; `135` total episodes checked $\ge 20$) against ground-truth path/implication construction (`max_err <= 1e-6`), recorded in `results["oracle_check"]` and gated in `R29A_D0`.

## 3. Mandatory Controls, Ablations, Matched-Training Baselines & Ceiling (`PROTOCOL.md` §§3–4, §9.1, §9.5)

### `D1` Arms (`multihop_3xor_equivsat` & `6x6latin_parity`)
- `Control_Chance_Floor`: Predicts the empirical training-split class marginal `y_tr.mean(dim=0)` (`lines 343-345`).
- `Control_Linear_Readout_Only`: Zero-hidden-layer linear readout `nn.Linear(12, 4)` trained for `n_steps` with `AdamW` (`lines 347-360`).
- `Control_Random_Network`: Randomly initialized 3-module ensemble (`seed + 9000`) evaluated through the exact `market_clear_from_quotes` pipeline (`lines 362-368`, `Rule 9.1`).
- `Control_Untrained_Init`: Initial 3-module ensemble at `seed` before training, evaluated through `market_clear_from_quotes` (`lines 370-376`, `Rule 9.1`).
- `Ablation_Local_Greedy_Layerwise`: Detached modules trained via greedy 1-hop local BCE against `y_tr` (`lines 378-402`).
- `Ablation_Naive_Simultaneous_LMSR`: Detached modules trained via simultaneous Jacobi unpreconditioned LMSR perturbations around the unconditioned opening market prior `0.50` without Kyle correlation preconditioning or sequential AMM settlement (`lines 404-448`).
- `Inconsistency_Kyle_Futarchy_LIR` (**Ours**): Detached modules trained via Sequential Hanson-LMSR counterfactual pivoting conditioned on detached live quotes `q_all` + Kyle empirical covariance preconditioning `kyle_prec` + pinned negative-attention LIR targets `tau_m.detach()` (`lines 450-498`).
- `Ceiling_Global_EndToEnd_Backprop`: Centralized end-to-end backpropagation through `market_clear_from_quotes` without `.detach()` (`lines 500-521`).

### `D2` Arms (`bfs_shortest_hop`, `bellman_ford_shortest_path`, `2sat_implication_closure`)
- `Control_Chance_Floor`: Empirical training-split node class prior (`lines 940-942`).
- `Control_Degree_Distance_Heuristic`: Non-learned 2-hop local signed affinity/degree propagation heuristic `degree_distance_heuristic_predict` (`lines 886-897, 944-948`).
- `Control_Random_Network`: Randomly initialized `FactoredJointAttGNN(seed + 9000, mode="bellman_lir")` run through the full $T_{\text{eval}}$-step test-time loop (`lines 950-958`, `Rule 9.1`).
- `Control_Untrained_Init`: Initial `FactoredJointAttGNN(seed, mode="bellman_lir")` before training, run through the full $T_{\text{eval}}$-step test-time loop (`lines 960-968`, `Rule 9.1`).
- `Baseline_Standard_MPNN`: Matched-architecture `FactoredJointAttGNN(mode="standard_mpnn")` (`our training, d2_steps SGD steps`, `PROTOCOL.md` §9.5) with fixed $T_{\text{mpnn}} = 3$ message-passing steps matching the in-distribution $n=16$ graph radius (`lines 972, 976-996`).
- `Ablation_Unregularized_Looped_GNN`: Recurrent `FactoredJointAttGNN(mode="unregularized_looped")` trained at $T_{\text{train}} = 5$ without LIR fixed-point consistency regularization ($\lambda_{\text{inc}} = 0$, undamped residual update `h + 0.65 * cand`) and unrolled to $T_{\text{eval}}$ steps at test time (`lines 875-876, 973, 976-996`).
- `Inconsistency_Bellman_LIR_GNN` (**Ours**): Recurrent `FactoredJointAttGNN(mode="bellman_lir")` trained at $T_{\text{train}} = 5$ with multi-step LIR consistency equilibrium loss `loss_ce + 0.50 * inc_e.mean()` and contractive state damping `0.35 * h + 0.65 * torch.tanh(cand)`, unrolled to $T_{\text{eval}}$ steps at test time (`lines 877-878, 974, 976-996`).
