### Gate `R26B-E1a`: Powered `10x10` Stratego Paired Head-to-Head Matchups on Held-Out `arrangements[p][512:]` (`50` Seat-Swapped Pairs = `100` Games Per Seed, `300` Games Per Focal Matchup Total)
- **Power Calculation:**
  Each focal matchup between `GLC_v3_AtaraxosSearchBot` and opponent $B$ runs **`K_pairs = 50` seat-swapped board pairs (`N = 100` games) per seed (`150` seat-swapped pairs = `300` games across the `3` seeds `101, 102, 103`)** using `SearchBot(max_num_samples=20, depth=2, stepsize=10.0, temperature=0.006, td_lambda=1.0)`.

  - Per-seed win rate $\bar{Y}_s$, standard error $\text{SE}_s$, two-sided 95% CI $[\bar{Y}_s - 1.960\,\text{SE}_s,\; \bar{Y}_s + 1.960\,\text{SE}_s]$, and one-sided 95% LCB $\bar{Y}_s - 1.645\,\text{SE}_s$ (`N = 100` games per seed), and
  - Pooled win rate $\bar{Y}_{\text{pool}}$, standard error $\text{SE}_{\text{pool}}$, and two-sided 95% CI $[\text{LCB}_{95\%},\; \text{UCB}_{95\%}]$ across all `300` games (`150` seat-swapped pairs).

- **`R26B-E1a.2` (`GLC_v3_AtaraxosSearchBot` vs. `Ataraxos_SearchBot_Uniform` — 300 Powered Games):**
