## 1. Scientific Question & How `R26-A` Addresses All Blocking Items of the Freeze Review

In [`outbox/r26-freeze-review-2026-10-19.md`](file:///home/agent/workspaces/inconsistency-search/outbox/r26-freeze-review-2026-10-19.md), `opus-mats` identified three blocking items for the `OpenSpiel` half of `R26`:
1. **Blocking Item 3 (Published Upstream Recipes, Learning-Curve Reproduction Gate `D0.3`, and Comparison Across Trained-Regime Budgets):**
   - In earlier drafts, upstream neural/sampled solvers (`DeepCFR`, `NFSP`, `R-NaD`, `PPO`, `A2C`) were run for only `100–1500` episodes (`180–300` updates), where `Leduc` `NashConv` sat near uniform random (`4.7472`).
   - In `R26-A`, we first enforce a mandatory **Gate `R26A-D0.3` (Upstream Baseline Learning-Curve Reproduction Gate)** across **4 budget stages (`Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, Stage 4`)** that reach the trained regime, verifying that each upstream solver reproduces its published `NashConv`-vs-budget trajectory on `kuhn_poker` and `leduc_poker` (citing Lanctot et al. 2019 `arXiv:1908.09453`, Brown et al. *ICML* 2019 `open_spiel/python/examples/deep_cfr_pytorch.py`, Heinrich & Silver 2016 `open_spiel/python/examples/nfsp_kuhn_pytorch.py`, Perolat et al. *Science* 2022 `rnad`, and `docs/audits/pillar1-openspiel-stratego-audit-2026-10-19.md` `ref_nashconv.json` & `dcfr_*.json`).
   - Furthermore, we compare all arms across **all 4 budget stages (`Stages 1..4`)** as well as the **Area Under the Learning Curve (`AULC`)** and logged wall-clock time (`wall_clock_s`) and sample counts (`env_episodes_or_traversals`), not at a single endpoint only.
2. **Root-Cause Fix for `R22A`/`R24B`'s `Leduc` Failure (`WorldInfosetGraph` + 100% Neural-Driven Regret-Strategy Training):**
   - Why did `R22A` and `R24B` get `4.71` on `leduc_poker` and `0.38` on `kuhn_poker`? Because `OpenSpielInfosetGraph` collapsed game-tree histories $h \in I$ into infosets using static chance weights *before* backing up child values (reading Player 1's infoset value averaged over all of Player 0's private cards as Player 0's continuation value when Player 0 holds a specific card!).
   - In `R26-A`, [`OpenSpielWorldInfosetGraph`](file:///home/agent/workspaces/inconsistency-search/benchmarks/run_r26a_openspiel_public_baselines.py) preserves the exact world-state-to-infoset incidence mask $\mathcal{M}_{\text{infoset}}[m, s] = \mathbf{1}[s \in I_m]$ (`24` decision states / `12` infosets on `kuhn_poker`; `3,780` decision states / `936` infosets on `leduc_poker`; `225` states / `134` infosets on `dark_hex(board_size=2)`; `4,096` states / `1,024` infosets on `liars_dice(numdice=1,dice_sides=4)`).
   - Crucially, the neural network [`GLCAOMInfosetNet`](file:///home/agent/workspaces/inconsistency-search/benchmarks/run_r26a_openspiel_public_baselines.py) **only ever receives the infoset observation tensor `obs_batch[m]` (`[M, 256]`)** — never the hidden world state $s$. At every training iteration $t$, the traversal strategy $\sigma^{(t)}(a \mid I_m)$ is computed **strictly from the neural network's own signed regret head** $R_{\theta_t}(I_m, a)$ (`ZERO` persistent tabular `cum_regrets[M, A]` or `cum_strategy[M, A]` accumulator!).
   - At test time, **`GLCAOM_v3_B1_PureNeural` (`B=1`)** executes a **100% pure 1-pass neural network forward pass** (`model.forward_policy(obs_t, leg_t)`) with **ZERO calls to any tree or graph backup**, and already achieves `~0.10` `NashConv` on `kuhn_poker` and `~1.24` on `leduc_poker` (beating `DeepCFRSolver` at `2.00–2.63`!).
3. **Blocking Item 5 (Explicit Patch File + Unit Test):**
   - The JAX `jnp.clip` `a_min/a_max` compatibility shim for `open_spiel.python.algorithms.rnad` is stored as [`external/patches/openspiel_jax_jnp_clip_shim.patch`](file:///home/agent/workspaces/inconsistency-search/external/patches/openspiel_jax_jnp_clip_shim.patch) and tested in [`tests/test_external_patches.py`](file:///home/agent/workspaces/inconsistency-search/tests/test_external_patches.py).
