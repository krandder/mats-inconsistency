## Abstract

Rounds 4–9 (`2026-10-16` to `2026-10-17`, 18 pre-registered 3-seed studies totalling `$49.63` at the list-price ceiling, plus the pre-Round-4 `E-LA` bridge at `$1.40` for a combined `$51.03`; 18 of 19 studies run on NVIDIA L4 GPUs and `R4-E3b` on the Tinker API) tested five questions across coupled $6\times 6$ hidden-fleet inference (`ShipHunt`, `49,504` legal configurations with an exact Bayesian oracle `[R5E1:83]`), $9\times 9$ Sudoku (`R4-E2` and unique-solution `Sudoku-Extreme` with 17–22 givens `[R5E3:22]`), imperfect-information $4\times 4$ and $6\times 6$ `Micro-Stratego` (`720` and `10,080` legal setups per side `[R4E1:72, R5E2:97]`), and reinforcement learning under a sycophantic rater bonus (`Qwen3-1.7B` LoRA `[R4E3a:56]`, `Qwen3-8B` LoRA `[R4E3b:30]`, and a `669,633`-parameter coupled network `[R5E1:86]`):

1. **Clearing Rollouts in the Space of Information (`R5-E1`–`R9-E1`, `R6-E3`, `R7-E3`):** Raw encoder hidden-state keys (`K1`) failed the pre-registered overlap-correlation bar on $6\times 6$ `ShipHunt` ($r = 0.375$ vs. $\ge 0.55$ bar `[R5E1:27,209]`), and learned key projections collapsed on $4\times 4$ and $6\times 6$ `Micro-Stratego` (`R5-E2` flank cosine gap `0.004` `[R5E2:132]`; both are exploratory analyses run after their `D0` gates failed). Defining keys by readout salience $s_r = |\Delta \ell_r| / \|\Delta \ell_r\|_2$ (`K3`) passed all pre-registered key-geometry bars on `ShipHunt` ($r = 0.880 \, [0.876, 0.884] \ge 0.60$, same-vs-across cosine gap $0.967 \, [0.936, 0.999] \ge 0.50$, Spearman $1.00 \ge 0.94$, `R6-E1` `D1` **PASS** `[R6E1:27,123]`), while bag-centered salience (`K3c`) passed on $6\times 6$ `Micro-Stratego` (cosine gap $0.661 \, [0.642, 0.679] \ge 0.28$, `R7-E3` `D1` **PASS** `[R7E3:60]`, driven by subtracting the skewed bag mean `[R7E3:89-95]`). Over `K3` tokens on full-support skewed bags (`P-FullSupport`), a weight-tied 1-block transformer applied $T=3$ times (`looped_attn_T3`, `39,428` parameters = $37.1\%$ of `attn_L3-K3`'s `106,180`, bar $\le 38\%$) matched `attn_L3-K3` in KL (`0.0317` vs. `0.0328`, ratio `0.967 [0.927, 1.007]` $\le 1.10$), cut preference-skew JSD by $7.05\times$ vs. `scaled_mix` and $7.84\times$ vs. `ataraxos_mmd` (bar $\ge 4.5\times$), and beat `scaled_mix`, `ataraxos_mmd`, `kc_K1`, and `kc_K3` in both KL and JSD (`R7-E1` `D1.1` **PASS** `[R7E1:30,145-157]`). An `814`-parameter preconditioned Jacobi solver with a 2-scalar precision-dependent ridge (`jacobi_lin_T4_prec`) passed all 8 pre-registered parts of `R9-E1` `D2.2`: clean redundancy $W(16)/W(1) = 1.109 \, [1.083, 1.134] \le 1.25$ at $\tau=0.3$, strict tier ordering $W(8)/W(1) = 1.098 < 1.607 < 2.276$ across $\tau \in \{0.3, 1.5, 2.5\}$ in every seed, noisy accumulation $W(16)/W(1) = 2.515 \, [2.335, 2.694] \ge 2.20$ at $\tau=2.5$, `P-FullSupport` $\mathrm{KL}(24) = 0.0349 \, [0.0343, 0.0355] \le 0.0420$, and paired KL CIs $> 0$ against `looped_lin_T4` (`0.0664`), `kc_K1` (`0.0950`), `kc_K3` (`0.0647`), and 3-scalar `econ_prec_K3` (`0.0441`) `[R9E1:30,140-146,278-284]`. Guiding 4 waves of 6 rollouts with key-space PUCT over `K3` reached $98.63\% \, [98.55\%, 98.71\%]$ cell coverage (bar $\ge 95.5\%$, `R7-E1` `D2.1` **PASS** `[R7E1:32,201]`), cut preference JSD by $2.93\times$ (mixed reads) and $5.38\times$ (clean reads, bar $\ge 2.0\times$), and cut KL by $51.3\%$ and $53.1\%$ (bar $\ge 35\%$, `R7-E1` `D2.2` **PASS** `[R7E1:33,206-208]`).
2. **Anytime Monotonicity from Sparse and Strong Roots (`R8-E1`, `R8-E3`, `R9-E1`):** From a 2-anchor sparse root ($\mathrm{KL}(0) = 0.2797$), anchor-aware `looped_attn_T3` reduced KL at every step $K \in \{0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32\}$ in every seed in `R8-E1` (`0.2797 -> 0.0303`, $\mathrm{JSD}(24) = 0.00575$, `R8-E1` `D1.1` **PASS** `[R8E1:30,129,140-142]`) and passed again in `R9-E1` ($\mathrm{KL}(32) = 0.0323 \le 0.033$, max step rise $+0.00016 \le +0.0005$, $\mathrm{JSD}(24) = 0.00699 \le 0.0075$, `R9-E1` `D1.1` **PASS** `[R9E1:27,138,151-156]`). 3. **Multi-Stall Landmark Search and Self-Consistent Verification on `Sudoku-Extreme` (`R4-E2`, `R5-E3`, `R6-E2`, `R7-E2`, `R8-E2`, `R9-E2`):** On `Sudoku-Extreme`, single-stall Landmark branching (`P6`, `R5-E3` `D1` **FAIL** `[R5E3:14]`) and per-box leave-one-out self-checks (`R6-E2`, `0/5` rules **PASS**, registered cell-correctness AUROC `0.526 / 0.520 / 0.527` vs. $\ge 0.75$ bar `[R6E2:16]`) failed because hard boards stall `4.89` times on average along the true path (`R7-E2` `G0` **PASS** `[R7E2:18]`) and early wrong guesses produce locally clash-free but globally wrong boxes `[R6E2:30-35]`. ## 5. What Remains Untested at Scale and Limitations

1. **Four-Role Parameter Separation in Language Models Without Mandate Shortcuts:** In `R5-E1` Part B (`669k`-parameter coupled network with a `0.02` action-entropy bonus), `LIR-Masked` matched `Fused` task reward (`101%`) and achieved `1.000` zero-retraining `faith_swap` action flips (`[R5E1:257, 267, 284-287]`), whereas on `Qwen3-8B` LoRA (`R4-E3b`) the action adapter learned a belief-free mandate reflex (`BUY` under `R2`, `PASS` under `K7` due to the `0.37` training YES base rate) and scored `0.39` on `faith_swap` (`D2` **FAIL** `[R4E3b:15, 27, 29]`). Demonstrating utility-swappable policies on a multi-billion-parameter language model requires balanced mandate payoffs around the market price, an explicit action-entropy schedule, and measuring the belief half of `faith_swap` under desire-aligned notes `[R4E3b:29, 40]`.
2. **Salience Keys and Iterative Clearing Beyond Fixed-Variable Grids:** `looped_attn_T3` (`39,428` params) and `jacobi_lin_T4_prec` (`814` params) operate on salience vectors $s_r \in \mathbb{R}^{36}$ defined over a fixed grid of board cells `[R6E1:128, R9E1:358-359]`. Extending readout-salience keys and normal-equation clearing to open-ended natural-language rollouts requires a shared coordinate bank of probe propositions at the root and remains untested.
3. **No-Oracle Hyperparameter Selection on `Sudoku-Extreme` and Hard-Label Overfitting on `Micro-Stratego`:** As disclosed in `R8-E2` (`[R8E2:96-98]`) and `R9-E2` (`[R9E2:157-158]`), the aggregator hyperparameters $(\lambda, g)$ on `Sudoku-Extreme` were fitted on validation solution cross-entropy (applied like-for-like across all arms), and on `Micro-Stratego` (`R8-E3` `[R8E3:89-105, 145]`) fitting `108,616`-parameter attention pools to hard realized labels on 2,000 calibration positions overfit severely unless stopped at step `0` on a held-out calibration split.
