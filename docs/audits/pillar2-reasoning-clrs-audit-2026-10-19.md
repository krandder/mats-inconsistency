Public reasoning: all six baselines one 4-layer d=136 class (run_r25:844-1013); AR_GRPO not autoregressive (955) and its reward includes matching target tokens (1277-1303); the BoN verifier term cancels in the softmax (1529-1531); Searchformer ≈ Stream_of_Search, A* traces never used (ENV:949-986); lr 2.0e-3 baselines vs 2.6e-3 GLC (run_r25:3687).
