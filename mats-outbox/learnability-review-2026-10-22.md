## 1. Bugs (confirmed and plausible), with the check to run first

- B1 [C] The closed forms decode with the true per-episode structure; the learned decoders see only symbols. `raw_code_forecast` (compression.py:498-513) and the Gaussian `llr_quantizer` (217-226) read `batch.oracle` (which bit each agent read, its accuracy, redrawn per episode). The learned decoder is `decode(m)` (1381). The learned encoder does see its own key (1131). So "2 bits reach 0, the gap is learning not bits" (README:1129-1130; overnight-results:68) is unsupported. Blind references for Z9-p2 (bits/agent → KL): raw 2-reading symbols, no identity: 0.309-0.323 (2); count of 1s among parity readings: 0.160 (log2 3); best 2-bit code from coordinate search: 0.151 (2); bit identity + value, no accuracies: 0.0147 (5.49). Learned label-only at b=2: 0.26-0.35. First check: feed `keys["full"]` to `ConsensusSetDecoder` as a side input (outside the channel) and rerun Z9-p2 at b in {2,4,inf}.
- B2 [C] The Z9-p2 label-only plateau at 0.158 is a specific solution, not "no learning". With `readers_per_bit`=3 for every bit, the total count of 1-readings among parity readings is informative about Y (optimum 0.160; observed 0.1578-0.160 across dvib/coa/fixed at b=inf; per-seed gains +0.098 and +0.293 match the "at least one 1" code and the count code to 2 decimals). Fix: randomise readers per bit per episode (removes the leak) or report 0.16 as the DeepSets floor.
- B3 [C] The ISAB stall on Z9 k=4 is the same count floor: optimum of P(Y | count) is 0.281 at N=128 (observed 0.2778-0.2780 for m=4..128) and 0.289 at N=256 (observed 0.2904-0.2909). So the stall is the inducing-point path plus optimisation, not m and not capacity; "m=N also fails so not a bottleneck" is a non sequitur. - B4 [C] The uncompressed Z9-p2 anchor memorises its 100k pool (8000x512 = 41 epochs, final model reported, no best-val): train loss 0.14-0.21 vs E[H(Bayes)] 0.236, best val 0.022-0.031 at steps 3600-4200 vs final dev 0.056-0.068. ## 3. The deeper problems, in plain language, and a short learning-theory primer

- Three distinct things are being called "learning failure". (i) Information: a structure-blind decoder cannot reach Bayes at finite b on Z9-p2 (Bayes depends on accuracies drawn from a continuum; N*b bits give 2^(N*b) outputs). The blind floor at b=2 is somewhere at or below 0.151; nobody has computed the exact value. (ii) Benchmark leaks: Z9 with fixed readers-per-bit has a cheap sum statistic worth 0.29 of 0.45 nats, and every mean-pooling architecture (DeepSets, ISAB's inducing-point path, label-only dvib) finds it and stops. (iii) Optimisation: once (i) and (ii) are removed, the remaining question is whether SGD finds a code that spends bits on bit identity. Label-only training never did (B9, M2).
- Parity from Y labels alone: any function of fewer than k of the right bits has zero correlation with the label (Fourier argument [C]). The expected gradient on one agent's message is zero while the other messages carry no partial-parity signal, so end-to-end discrete coding of parity from Y alone starts at a saddle; the latent-label arms work because prefix parities give low-order signal. The same barrier applies to the decoder's readout, not only the encoder.
- Discrete codes and decoders are a chicken-and-egg problem [C as theory]: while the decoder ignores the messages the encoder gets no gradient, and vice versa; straight-through and Gumbel gradients are biased (1308.3432); codes collapse to few symbols (1711.00937). The emergent-communication literature shows the same (1605.06676, 1705.11192, 2006.02419). Team decision optimality is NP-hard in general (Tsitsiklis & Athans 1985, recall).
- E2: the b=2 gap (0.069 vs 0.033) splits evenly: decoder 0.018 (0.0338 vs 0.0158 at b=inf) and compression 0.018. The per-agent-token "floor" 0.035 is itself a learning gap (per-piece bidirectional reaches 0.0026).
- E1 [P]: exact GLS needs a 10x10 and a 4x4 inverse; attention and MLPs do inversion only by iteration, about one solver step per layer (2212.07677, 2310.17086), and the variance ratio here is 44. Softmax weights are non-negative; GLS often needs a negative weight on a source sharing noise with a better one.
- Primer. Three strands matter here, one does not. (a) Statistical-query and leap complexity [C as theory, P as applied]: learning a k-parity from labels with gradient methods takes about n^k steps when no lower-order signal exists; "leap" = how many new coordinates must be found at once; curricula and intermediate labels turn leap k into leap 1 (2202.08658, 2302.11055, 2207.08799, 1703.07950). Caveat: with n=6 variables n^k <= 4e4, far below the 2.3M samples used, and the bit identity is in the token, so SQ hardness does not bind any cell here; the stalls are the count leak and inducing-point/optimisation effects. (b) Hidden progress and plateaus (2207.08799): flat loss then a sudden drop; seed- and lr-dependent escapes, as seen on Z9 k=6. (c) Information theory: the CEO problem under log loss (1110.3069) and distributed IB (1709.09082) give the b-bit floors for a decoder that knows the joint structure; they assume conditional independence given the latent omega, not given Y. (d) Classical generalisation theory (PAC, VC, Rademacher) does not matter: episodes are fresh from a simulator, and the only overfitting is the reused pool (B4).
- Read, in order: Barak et al. 2207.08799 (intro and sec. 3); Abbe et al. 2406.06467 (globality barrier and scratchpad: the sequential consensus is a scratchpad); Wies, Levine & Shashua 2204.02892 (intermediate supervision makes parity learnable: learned securities are such labels); Abbe, Boix-Adsera & Misiakiewicz 2202.08658 (leap). All resolved on export.arxiv.org.

## 4. Objective (1): is G(Sigma) / log-likelihood-span sound, and how to make it trainable

- G(Sigma) and the span criterion are different [C]. G({Y})=0 on Z1, Z4, Z5, Z1-p2, Z5-p2 while the Y-span residual is 0.97-1.00; Z1 is common-cause, so agents are not CI given Y. Mechanism: a real-valued forecast is injective in a binary reading, so on tabular envs G({Y}) only tests "is the agent's Y-forecast lossless in x_i" (zero exactly when a single reading has zero marginal information, as in Z6/Z9: 0.451, 0.2566). G says nothing about market adequacy.
- The number of securities is not a cost under G [C]: one generic real-valued security sum_j c_j b_j sends G to 0.0000 on Z6, Z9 k=4 and k=6. Either count forecast precision in bits or restrict the decoder to additive pooling (the span version); only the second makes d meaningful.
- The "if" direction needs CI given omega (holds in the zoo by construction; not in E1/E2 with omega=Y) [C]. The "only if" direction fails when omega has parts irrelevant to Y: the minimal span dimension on Z9 k=4 is 5/5/6 (k=4 plus 1-2 distractor bits read) [C]. d is the market-state size, not the per-agent message size: naming which 2 of 5-6 securities a trade touches costs 4.00 bits of support pattern on Z9-p2 [C].
- Bound [C, corrected]: with pool q proportional to p e^{-r}, KL(p||q) = int_0^1 (1-t) Var_t(r) dt <= osc(r)^2/8, a sup-norm bound on the summed residual; same-sign residuals add with N. LMSR trades are moment-matching (M-projection), not L2; sequential trades are ADF/EP only for traders who move the price to their own posterior [P].
- Making it trainable. With known likelihood tables, the best L2 span is a truncated SVD of the row-centred log-likelihood matrix: closed form, nothing to learn [C]. Without them, minimising G through a variational decoder is the existing task loss with the encoder restricted to forecasts on phi; it inherits the parity stall and mixes "securities insufficient" with "decoder failed" [C]. Rule: report exact G(phi) by enumeration and decoder regret separately; compare learned phi to the true span by principal angles (phi is defined up to an invertible linear map); rule out the all-indicators and one-real-security degeneracies. Of the three signals left, settled securities (phi(omega) observed at settlement, a local loss per agent) is the one with a usable gradient; unsupervised latent-class fits need about 3 CI views per latent (0809.5032) and Z9 has exactly 3 readers per bit [P]; labels on Y alone stall. Even with the right span the readout is nonlinear (Z9: P(Y) = (1 - prod(1-2p_j))/2; E1: a matrix inverse), so the span certifies that summing trades suffices, not that the readout is learnable [C].
- Bottom line: use d_omega and the Y-span residual (additive pooling) as the objective; drop G({Y}) on tabular envs; make securities-as-message the experimental link to (2) (step 7 below).

## 5. Plan for (2): learned compression on parity, in sequence, with what each result means

0. Reframe (no compute): table with blind references (0.31 raw, 0.160 count, 0.151 search, 0.0147 at 5.49 bits), symbol entropies, FCT relabel. Target at b=2 for a blind decoder is <=0.151, not 0; "match the closed form" needs decoder-side keys to be well-posed.
1. Decoder-side keys, learned encoder, Z9-p2 b in {2,4,inf}, fresh episodes, best-val. Near 0 at b=2: encoder learns, and the whole gap was B1. Stuck at 0.16-0.35: encoder/channel learning is the problem; go to 2.
2. Decoder-only diagnostic on frozen raw_code messages, with keys (expect ~0) and without (expect ~0.31). A decoder that fails with keys is a decoder bug; a decoder that passes isolates the encoder.
3. Repairs B4, B6, B7, B10 and a label-only curriculum arm; aux only at b>=4. If curr label-only now reaches ~0 with keys, latent labels are unnecessary; if only aux arms do, intermediate labels are the mechanism (Wies et al.).
4. Count-leak-free Z9 (randomised readers per bit). If label-only arms fall to the prior, every earlier plateau was the leak; if they still reach ~0.16, there is another sum statistic to find.
5. Biased-bit curriculum (new knob), b in {inf, 4}, 3 seeds. Escape at b=inf and not at b=4: compression, not parity, is the limit; no escape at b=inf: the optimisation problem is not low-order signal.
6. Symbol-usage fixes (FSQ or VQ resets) at b=2 with entropy logged. If entropy rises and KL falls toward 0.151 without keys, the code was wasting bits; if entropy rises without gain, the limit is the blind floor.
7. Securities as the message (link to objective 1): message = (security id, quantised amount), id bits inside b; decoder = sum then readout; arms: oracle bit securities (closed form 0.0147 at 5.49 bits), learned phi with settlement (latent supervision, tagged), Y-only. Sweep b in {2,4,6,8}. Predictions: Y-only stalls; settlement escapes; b>=6 brings both near 0.015; at b=2 no blind arm reaches 0. Exact G, additive-pool regret, learned regret, bits used and principal angles reported per arm.
8. Only then compare architectures (ISAB vs SAB vs trader) at matched budget, with the DeepSets control in every table.
