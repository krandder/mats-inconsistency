## A. gemini 50b060e
- **(i) Decoder over frozen raw_code: HOLDS, with wording fixes.**
  - D Z9-p2 per_seed: 0.3237/0.3231/0.3221 against the floor 0.3234. I recomputed the floor: same 100k tabulation 0.3234; 1M-train tabulation 0.3224 on the same dev; 20k dev 0.3125.
  - The floor is a plug-in table over the 120 sorted symbol multisets (diag:84-105), not "exact". It is correct up to about 0.001 of plug-in bias, and the 2000-episode dev CI is ±0.014. Sorting is valid: base readers are permuted per episode (zoo.py:789), and the unsorted table is not better (0.3220).
  - With keys: 0.0073 is the gauss channel only. Gumbel is 0.0638, and it overfits: train BCE 0.16-0.19 is below E[H] 0.236; best val 0.019-0.031, final 0.041-0.075.
  - On E2, keys add nothing (0.0563 vs 0.0554, oracle 0.0331).
- **(ii) Learned encoder + "exact Bayes" decoder: ARTEFACT of an oracle decoder; the numbers are real.** The fixed decoder is built from per-episode `oracle["var"]` and `oracle["acc"]` (diag:135-154). On E2 it uses `gaussian_report_model`, which reads `batch.oracle` (compression.py:220), and the oracle quantizer's own cell thresholds (diag:226-232).
  - So the decoder fixes both the structure and the codebook. On Z9-p2 obs-only, the encoder only has to learn the identity map from 2 binary readings to 4 symbols, so 0.0000 is exact Bayes by construction. On E2, 0.0334 just reproduces the oracle quantizer (0.0331).
  - Correct statement: "the encoder can learn to match a known codebook when the decoder knows the structure". It says nothing about blind compression.
  - It is also channel-dependent: obs-only gauss fails, 0/3 at 0.683 with 1.7 symbols.
- **(ii) "Fails with keys": PARTLY, and it is not the input-scale bug.**
  - The decoder is fixed, and the encoder inputs are standardised per feature (diag:499-500), so keys cannot swamp the message. Val KL stays above the prior from step 0, at 0.63-0.89 (D runs[12-17]).
  - On E2 the same keys arm works (gumbel 0.0362). On Z9 the gauss arm fails without keys too. Cause unknown: likely an overconfident fixed decoder plus a straight-through estimator.
- **(iii) Both learned with keys, 1/3: ARTEFACT (overfitting plus the unfixed key path).**
  - In 5/6 runs val KL climbs from 0.458 to 0.55-0.59, above the prior, while train BCE is 0.58-0.59, below ln 2. That is memorising the 100k pool through the 14 key dims: 8000 x 512 = 41 epochs, final model, no best-val (diag:741-746, 597).
  - The decoder concatenates [m, keys] with no per-part norm or key scale (diag:407-417), the path the builder found harmful.
- **FSQ/VQ: PARTLY.** The numbers match F, and both READMEs are generated: I re-rendered them from the JSON and they are byte-identical. All arms share the encoder, decoder, data, 4000 steps and the llr skip (fsq:139-157, 329).
  - Confound 1: the gauss reference trains with channel noise and scores sign (fsq:168-177). FSQ trains and scores the same rounding (fsq:186-193). So the ~0.012 gain mixes "all 4 symbols" with removing the B8 train/score mismatch (~0.01 on E2 b=2).
  - Confound 2: FSQ and VQ add usage-entropy losses (fsq:196-198, 213-214).
  - "Beats blind extremizing on Z8" (0.0491 vs 0.0504) is a tie: the FSQ CI is ±0.0045, it is unpaired, and the closed form is fit on 2000 episodes (fsq:527-535).
  - On E2, FSQ 0.0578 does not reach the blind closed forms (extremized 0.0565, msg-only floor 0.0555).
  - The FCT anchor (0.0350) is a cached fresh-regime run (compression.py:991-999), not trained on the same data. E2's best uncompressed reader is 0.0026.
  - The JSONs record no git commit.
