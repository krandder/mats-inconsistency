## Abstract

In Rounds 1–3 (`overnight-results-paper-2026-10-15.md`), we tested three foundational claims on $6\times 6$ Sudoku, $W=13$ Rule-110 cellular automata, and 4-layer masked language models: (1) whether verified search rollouts provide a clean distillation target for a one-pass any-order network (supported on $6\times 6$ Sudoku when gated by a sound polynomial-time verifier), (2) whether log-space weighting and Gram key-crowding deduplication make rollout aggregation insensitive to search-policy skew (scalar precision weights cut Rule-110 KL by $15\%$ over uniform pooling, while key-crowding failed on a scalar target because hidden representations encoded board context rather than rollout subspace), and (3) whether token-masking outcome-reward gradients (`DCPO`) prevents sycophantic belief drift under RL (refuted: shared trunk weights still drifted and reached $85.5\%$ false-note agreement).

Rounds 4–9 (`2026-10-15` to `2026-10-17`, 17 pre-registered 3-seed GPU studies plus the pre-Round-4 `E-LA` bridge, `$49.62` total compute across all runs) test whether five structural modifications resolve those limitations across four domains: coupled $6\times 6$ hidden-fleet inference (`ShipHunt`, `49,504` valid configurations with an exact Bayesian oracle), $9\times 9$ `Sudoku-Extreme` (17–22 givens, ~60 empty cells, average `4.89` propagation stalls per board), imperfect-information $4\times 4$ and $6\times 6$ `Micro-Stratego` (`720` and `10,080` legal setups per side), and reinforcement learning with a sycophantic rater bonus on both fine-tuned language models (`Qwen3-1.7B` and `Qwen3-8B` LoRA on 2,500 real Manifold Markets forecasting questions) and a `669k`-parameter coupled belief-policy network.

Across the pre-registered decision rules, five positive findings and four sharp negative findings emerge:
1. **Clearing in the Space of Information (Salience Keys, Weight-Tied Loop, and Precision-Aware Jacobi Iteration):** Replacing raw hidden-state keys (`K1`, overlap correlation $r = 0.375\text{--}0.382$) with readout-salience keys $s_r(c) = |\ell_r(c) - \ell_0(c)|$ (`K3`, $r = 0.880$, disjoint-vs-identical cosine gap $0.967$, Spearman $1.00$; bag-centered `K3c` in $6\times 6$ Stratego, flank cosine gap $0.661$ vs. $0.004$ for raw hidden states) separates rollout subspaces from shared board context. Over `K3` tokens, a weight-tied 1-block transformer unrolled for $T=3$ steps (`looped_attn_T3`, `39,428` parameters, $37.1\%$ of 3-layer `attn_L3-K3`) matches `attn_L3-K3` in KL (`0.0317` vs. `0.0328`, ratio `0.967`), cuts preference-skew JSD by $7.05\times$ vs. `scaled_mix` and $7.84\times$ vs. fixed-step `ataraxos_mmd`, and satisfies the **three-regime weight law**: deduplicating clean redundant copies ($W(16)/W(1) = 1.02\text{--}1.08$ at $\tau=0.3$), accumulating noisy corroborating copies ($W(16)/W(1) = 4.31\text{--}4.52$ at $\tau=2.5$), and ordering $W(8)/W(1)$ strictly by noise tier ($\tau=0.3 < \tau=1.5 < \tau=2.5$) in every seed. A `814`-parameter precision-aware preconditioned Jacobi iteration (`jacobi_lin_T4_prec`, `R9-E1` `D2.2` **PASS on all 8 parts**) with a 2-scalar ridge $\lambda(\beta_r) = \lambda_c\sigma(a - b\beta_r)$ satisfies the full weight law ($W(16)/W(1) = 1.109$ at $\tau=0.3$, $1.098 < 1.607 < 2.276$ at $N=8$, $2.515$ at $\tau=2.5$) and reaches `P-FullSupport` $\mathrm{KL}(24) = 0.0349$, beating `looped_lin_T4` (`0.0664`), `kc_K1` (`0.0950`), `kc_K3` (`0.0647`), and 3-scalar `econ_prec_K3` (`0.0441`) with all paired $95\%$ CIs $> 0$. Guiding 4 waves of 6 rollouts with key-space PUCT over `K3` lifts cell coverage from $83.0\%$ to $98.63\%\text{--}98.76\%$ and cuts preference-skew JSD by $2.93\times\text{--}5.38\times$.
2. **Anytime Monotonicity from Both Sparse and Genuine Strong Roots:** Without an explicit root anchor token, adding bounded-context rollouts to a strong root ($\mathrm{KL}(0) = 0.0322$ at $M_0^{\text{strong}} = 32$ in `R9-E1`, or $\mathrm{KL}(0) = 0.0488$ in $6\times 6$ Stratego `R8-E3`) degrades the root belief under every unanchored rule (`mix` $+0.176$ nats, `ataraxos_mmd` $+0.047$ at $K=16$ and $+0.094$ at $K=24$, `scaled_mix` $+0.053$, `jacobi_lin_T4_prec` $+0.044$, `econ_prec_K3` $+0.046$; in Stratego, `raw_count` $+0.591$ and `ataraxos_mmd` $+4.73$). Anchor-aware `looped_attn_T3` and `attn_L3-K3` trained across mixed root strengths and bag modes satisfy exact $K=0$ identity (`0.0`), reduce sparse-root KL monotonically across $K \in \{0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32\}$ (`0.2797 -> 0.0303` in `R8-E1`, `-89.2%`), and stay strictly below the strong root at $M_0=32$ across all $K$ in both Mode A and Mode B (`R9-E1` `D1.2` **PASS on all 15 parts**).
3. **Multi-Stall Landmark Search and Self-Consistent Verification on `Sudoku-Extreme`:** On `Sudoku-Extreme`, a single-stall Landmark split (`P6`, `R5-E3`) ties flat rollouts (`P3`) because hard boards stall an average of `4.89` times (`R7-E2`). Furthermore, per-box leave-one-out self-checks (`R6-E2`, `0/5` PASS) fail completely to identify globally valid sub-boards (`AUROC = 0.507` among non-clashing cells) because an early wrong guess at step `3.4` of 60 propagates to a locally clash-free but globally wrong assignment on `7.5–7.7` of 9 boxes. By contrast, multi-stall Landmark beam search with a greedy lead chain per Landmark (`P8_lead` / `P8_hybrid`) keeps the true path in the beam on $83.3\%$ of boards, lifts witness discovery from $40.2\%$ (`P3`) and $38.9\%$ (`P6`) to $45.2\%\text{--}46.7\%$ ($+5.0$ to $+6.5\text{ pp}$, $95\%$ CIs $> 0$), decreases test KL strictly at all `36/36` budget steps $m \in \{0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32\}$ (`1.178 -> 0.644` in `R8-E2`; `1.178 -> 0.695` in `R9-E2`), and with the zero-extra-pass self-consistency gate guarantees exact zero degradation ($\mathrm{KL}(16) - \mathrm{KL}(\text{P0}) = 0.0000$ in every seed) on boards where search finds no witness (`R9-E2` `D2` **PASS**).
4. **Learned Self-Trustworthiness vs. Output Stiffness and Horizon Bias in Imperfect-Information Games:** Across $4\times 4$ (`R4-E1`) and $6\times 6$ (`R5-E2`, `R6-E3`, `R7-E3`) Micro-Stratego, output stiffness $-H(q_r)$ is strongly *negatively* correlated with true rollout accuracy (Spearman $-0.36$ to $-0.55$) because false initial assumptions trigger deterministic forced captures that yield sharp, overconfident posteriors. A separate self-trust head $\theta_{\text{trust}}$ supervised on per-rollout log loss ranks rollout accuracy positively (`+0.347` overall and `+0.369` within $(s,a)$ pairs in `R7-E3` `D2(1)` **PASS**, a $+0.743\text{--}0.920$ Spearman margin over stiffness) and cuts stiffness-weighted MSE by $63.0\%\text{--}63.9\%$. However, when rollouts share a systematic 4-ply horizon bias (`R6-E3`) or when a one-pass transformer already solves the current-turn posterior (`KL = 0.024` in $4\times 4$ `R4-E1`; `KL = 0.0488` in $6\times 6$ `R8-E3`), test-time rollouts are weaker than the current-turn root (`0.078–0.096` vs. `0.0488`), and fitting `108k`-parameter attention pools to hard realized piece labels on 2,000 calibration boards without held-out early stopping memorizes calibration boards and degrades test KL $13\times$ (`R8-E3` **FAIL**).
5. **Structural Parameter Separation (`LIR-Masked`) vs. `Token-Masked` DCPO and `Fused` RL:** On `Qwen3-8B` LoRA over 2,500 real Manifold Markets forecasting questions (`R4-E3b`) and on the coupled $6\times 6$ ShipHunt network (`R5-E1` Part B), `Fused` RL shifts reported beliefs by $\Delta_{\text{desire}} = +0.490$ (`Qwen3-8B`, test Brier skill score collapsing from $+0.23$ to $-1.03$, false-note sycophancy $99.9\%$) and $+0.471$ (`R5-E1`, sycophancy $83.1\%$). `Token-Masked` (`DCPO`) still suffers $7.1\%$ false-note sycophancy and $0.20$ mean belief drift on `Qwen3-8B`, and $14\times$ higher cell belief drift (`0.0527` vs. `0.0038`, aligned with $\nabla U$) on `R5-E1` via shared trunk weights. Partitioning parameters into disjoint belief modules ($\theta_{\text{real}}, \theta_{\text{trust}}$ trained strictly on proper scoring losses with `.detach()` on all reward losses) and policy modules ($\theta_{\text{act}}$) yields **exact `0.0000` belief drift**, **$-0.0001$ desire bias** (`Qwen3-8B`, $1.2\%$ false-note agreement), **$0.000$ unwarranted sycophancy** (`R5-E1`), **$101\%$ of `Fused` task reward** when action-entropy regularization prevents policy collapse (`R5-E1`), and **exact `1.000` zero-retraining goal-reversal (`faith_swap`)** across all seeds (`R5-E1` `D3.4` **PASS**).

#### 3.1.1 Why Raw Hidden-State Keys Failed (`E-LA`, `R4-E1`, `R5-E1`, `R5-E2`) and How Salience Keys (`K3`, `K4`, `K3c`) Fixed It (`R6-E1`, `R6-E3`, `R7-E3`)

In `R5-E1` (`D1.1`), testing policy independence under an open-loop $80/20$ top-vs-bottom search preference without full support failed (`JSD(attn_L3)/JSD(mix) = 6.81`) because `24%` of board cells received zero readings under one search preference while naive `mix` barely moved from the prior (`KL = 0.207` vs. `attn_L3` `0.073`, a $74.0\%$ KL reduction, `R5-E1` `D1.2` **PASS**). 1. `mix`: uniform log-odds residual sum.

Four concrete lessons emerged from the Sudoku track:
1. **Why 1-stall `P6` tied flat `P3` (`R5-E3` `D1` FAIL) and why multi-stall `P8` worked (`R7-E2` `G0` PASS):** Diagnostic `G0` in `R7-E2` measured that along the ground-truth trajectory, hard `Sudoku-Extreme` boards hit forced-propagation stalls ($\max_c p(c) < 0.85$) an average of **`4.89` times per board**, while the model's top candidates $\{d : q(c^*, d) > 0.05\}$ contain the true digit on **`96.6%`, `98.0%`, and `97.8%`** of Stalls 1, 2, and 3. Branching only at Stall 1 (`P6`) leaves Stalls 2–5 to unbranched random completion; branching across $L \in \{3,4\}$ Landmark waves keeps the true assignment in the width-4 beam on **`83.3%`** of test boards (`91% / 71% / 88%` across seeds).
2. **Why `R8-E2`'s `P8_full` dipped below `R7-E2`'s `P8_beam` (`42.8%` vs. `44.5%`), proved by the $2\times 2$ factorial (`R8-E2` `POSTHOC-RERUN.md` and `R9-E2`):** `R7-E2`'s `P8_beam` allocated only `70.1%` of the pass budget (using worst-case $E_{\max}$ per completion) but always ran $r=0$ as a **greedy** forced-propagation lead chain from each Landmark before drawing stochastic chains. `R8-E2`'s `P8_full` fixed the budget accounting (`99.5%` spend) by replacing the greedy lead chain with all-stochastic sampling ($\tau=0.7$), which wandered off forced chains on deep tails. Restoring the greedy lead chain (`P8_lead`) in the post-hoc rerun and in `R9-E2`'s $2\times 2$ factorial (`stoch` `42.9%` vs. `lead` `44.9%` vs. `hybrid` `45.2%`) confirmed that the greedy lead chain accounts for almost the entire gain (`91` vs. `55` discordant boards in `R8-E2` post-hoc, exact binomial $p = 0.004$; `+4.7 pp [+2.6, +6.8]` over `P3` in `R9-E2`).
3. **Why per-box leave-one-out self-consistency failed (`R6-E2`, `0/5` PASS):** In `R6-E2`, we hypothesized that failed Sudoku rollouts contain locally valid $3\times 3$ boxes that could be stitched together using per-cell LOO self-check probabilities $p_{\text{self}}(r,c)$. While $p_{\text{self}}$ separated *clashing* cells with **`AUROC = 0.94`**, on failed rollouts the first wrong guess occurred at step **`3.4` of 60**, after which the autoregressive network propagated that error into a *locally clash-free* fill on **`7.5–7.7` of 9 boxes** while only **`0.35–0.37` of 9 boxes** matched the true solution. Consequently, correctness AUROC among non-clashing cells was **`0.507`** (chance). In globally coupled NP problems where early guesses propagate everywhere, verification must operate at the whole-witness level.
4. **Zero-extra-pass self-consistency eliminates false accepts (`R8-E2` `D2.2` FAIL $\to$ `R9-E2` `D2.2` PASS):** On Seed 103, the learned check head $p_{\text{chk}}(Z)$ assigned $p_{\text{chk}} \ge 0.5$ to `7.0%–9.6%` of invalid leaves, causing `+0.065` nats of KL degradation on unresolved boards in `R8-E2`. Requiring both $p_{\text{chk}}(Z) \ge 0.80$ and high step confidence in `R9-E2` reduced Seed 103's false-accept rate to **`0.0%`** and unresolved-board KL change to **`0.0000` in all 3 seeds** (`R9-E2` `D2.2` **PASS**). Moreover, `R9-E2`'s post-run audit (`Question G`) discovered an even cleaner zero-extra-pass verifier: reading the check-pass output $m_{\text{ql}} = \min_{c \in E_b} q_{\text{leaf}}(c, Z_c)$ on the leaf's own filled cells separates valid accepted leaves (`median = 0.12`) from invalid accepted leaves (`median = 0.004, max = 0.0125`) on Seed 103 with **`AUROC = 0.99998`**.

### 3.4 Self-Trustworthiness $\theta_{\text{trust}}$ vs. Output Stiffness $-H(q)$ and 4-Ply Horizon Bias in Imperfect-Information Games (`R4-E1`, `R5-E2`, `R6-E3`, `R7-E3`, `R8-E3`)

## 4. Negative Results Stated Plainly as Negatives and Boundary Conditions

Per pre-registration policy, every negative result and boundary condition across Rounds 4–9 is recorded below without reinterpretation.

### 4.1 Negative Results
1. **Unconstrained 6-layer linear transformers explode on rare test episodes (`E-LA` `A1, A2, A3, B-main, B-b` FAIL):** While 4-layer diagonal linear attention (`Diag-4`, `16` scalars) beat `AdaRR` (`0.782` vs. `0.808` MSE), unconstrained 6-layer `Diag-6`, `Full-4`, and `Full-6` models produced catastrophic polynomials on 1–2 out-of-distribution test episodes per seed (`MSE = 1.65–14.12`), and at low noise ($\sigma = 0.1$) 6-layer models were $4.9\text{--}6.6\times$ worse than closed-form `AdaRR`. Stability requires an explicit contracting normal-equation structure (`jacobi_lin_T4_prec` or softmax-normalized `looped_attn_T3`).
2. **One pass of an any-order transformer already solves $4\times 4$ and $6\times 6$ Stratego current-turn beliefs, leaving no headroom for rollout pooling (`R4-E1` `D0` FAIL, `R5-E2` `D0` FAIL, `R8-E3` `D1/D2` FAIL):** On $4\times 4$ Stratego (`720` setups/side), 0-step belief KL to exact Bayes is `0.0239` nats/slot ($3\times$ closer than constraint-only deduction `0.075`). On $6\times 6$ Stratego (`10,080` setups/side), 0-step belief KL is `0.0488` nats/slot (`R8-E3`). Because every rollout-built reference is weaker than the current-turn one-pass anchor (`0.096` on the cone union, `0.078` on all slots), fitting `108k`-parameter attention aggregators (`attn_L3-K3c`, `looped_attn_T3-K3c`) to hard realized piece labels on 2,000 calibration positions with no held-out stopping memorized the calibration boards (`CE 1.10 -> 0.97`) and degraded test KL $13\times$ (`0.0488 -> 0.635 / 0.585`, `R8-E3` `D1(2)` **FAIL**). Adding held-out early stopping kept both attention nets at their initialization (`KL = 0.0463`, `-5%`), matching 1-parameter `scaled_mix` (`0.0463`). In `R8-E3` `D2`, opponent-move log-likelihood `LL_window` had zero correlation with rollout accuracy (Spearman `-0.04`), and depth-1 myopic claims (`MSE = 0.040`) beat depth-4 rollouts (`0.045`).
3. **Neighbour-conditioned autoregressive cone rollouts in Stratego trigger hard piece-count vetoes (`R5-E2` `D0` FAIL):** Sampling 3 of 4 hidden cone slots sequentially from $\theta_{\text{real}}$ before scoring 4-ply playouts created global piece-inventory collisions on $21\%$ of claims, degrading belief KL $11\times$ (`0.047 -> 0.539`). Only marginal per-slot scoring (`R6-E3` `D0` **PASS**) avoided the inventory veto.
4. **Top-$M$ subspace mass $C^{\text{sub}}$ saturates and fails to detect unsolvable Sudoku boards (`R4-E2` `D2` FAIL):** On $9\times 9$ Sudoku, top-$M$ cell-candidate prior mass $C^{\text{sub}}$ saturated to `0.85` at $M=1$ and `0.97–1.00` at $M=2,3$ on both solvable and unsolvable boards (`AUC = 0.56` vs. `0.78–0.86` for full-trajectory $C^{\text{bel}}$).
5. **Single-stall Landmark branching (`P6`) and per-box LOO self-checks (`R6-E2`) fail on `Sudoku-Extreme` (`R5-E3` `D1/D2` FAIL, `R6-E2` `0/5` PASS):** Branching once at Stall 1 ties flat `P3` (`-0.003` nats) because hard boards stall `4.89` times on average. Per-box leave-one-out self-consistency $p_{\text{self}}$ has `AUROC = 0.507` for correctness among non-clashing cells because an early wrong guess at step `3.4` of 60 leads to `7.5–7.7` of 9 locally valid but globally false boxes. Furthermore, in `R9-E2` (`Section 2.1(3)` pre-spend finding), reading $q_{\text{leaf}}(c, Z_c)$ without a check pass fails (`0.000` on Seed 101) because `R4-E2` nets were trained with loss only on masked cells.
6. **Unregularized GRPO collapses action policies (`R4-E3a` `D0` FAIL, `R4-E3b` `D1c/D2` FAIL):** Without an action-entropy bonus, GRPO collapsed `Qwen3-1.7B`'s action policy on ShipHunt to a single cell in all 9 runs (`R4-E3a`), collapsed LIR Seed 103 on `Qwen3-8B` Manifold to constant `PASS` (`R4-E3b` `D1c`), and allowed `Qwen3-8B`'s action head to learn a belief-free mandate reflex (`BUY` under `R2`, `PASS` under `K7` due to a `0.37` training YES base rate), limiting `faith_swap` action flips to `0.39` vs. the `0.80` bar (`R4-E3b` `D2`).

### 4.2 Boundary Conditions Disclosed by the Audits
1. **`R9-E1` Strong-Root Calibration Order ($M_0=32$ vs. $M_0=34$):** `R9-E1` `D1.2` passed all 15 parts at $M_0^{\text{strong}} = 32$ ($\mathrm{KL}_{\text{test}}(0) = 0.0322$), selected under the pre-spend approved reference-first KL reading $\mathrm{KL}_{\text{cal}}(\text{ref}(36) \Vert \text{root}(M_0)) \le 0.035$. Under the reverse argument order $\mathrm{KL}_{\text{cal}}(\text{root}(M_0) \Vert \text{ref}(36)) \le 0.035$, the threshold selects $M_0 = 34$ (where 34 of 36 cells are already observed at the root, $\mathrm{KL}_{\text{test}}(0) = 0.0169$—twice as accurate as the `0.032` floor of a 32-read rollout pool). At $M_0=34$ Mode B, unanchored `ataraxos_mmd` degrades the root by `+0.1057` nats, whereas `looped_attn_T3` holds the root essentially flat within $\pm 0.0004$ nats (`0.0169 -> 0.0168` at $K=16$), with a `+0.00062` blip at $K=1$ in Seed 102 (`+0.00012` above the `+0.0005` step bar) and `-0.0001` at $K=16$ vs. the `+0.0020` strict-improvement bar. Thus, when a root is already $2\times$ stronger than the full rollout pool's asymptotic ceiling, `looped_attn_T3` prevents the catastrophic degradation of unanchored pooling (`+0.0001` vs. `+0.1057`) and preserves the root, but cannot extract further improvement in Mode B.
2. **`R9-E1` `econ_prec_K3` Background-Crowding Intercept (`1.267` vs. `1.14`):** In `R9-E1` `D2.1`, the 3-scalar closed-form rule `econ_prec_K3` reduced clean $W(16)/W(1)$ from `1.432` to `1.267 [1.237, 1.297]`, passing the CI-upper bar (`<= 1.30`) and all monotonicity/accumulation/MSE bars, but missing the `1.25` point-mean bar by `0.017`. The audit confirmed that the learned parameters ($a = -1.25$ to $-0.99$, $b = 2.66$ to $2.83$) give an isolated clean-probe ridge of $\lambda(0.892) = 0.025\text{--}0.030$, which yields $W(16)/W(1) = \mathbf{1.116 / 1.151 / 1.171}$ across the 3 seeds when evaluated without the 8 background reads; the 8 background reads add a constant crowding intercept $C_{\text{bg}} > 0$ in the denominator $1 + \lambda(C_{\text{bg}} + (N-1))$ that lifts the ratio on the D3 probe boards by `+0.12`. The 4-step matrix-inversion approximation `jacobi_lin_T4_prec` (`814` params) subtracts this background coupling and achieves `1.109 [1.083, 1.134]` (`D2.2` **PASS**).
3. **`R9-E2` Chain-Step Floor $m_{\text{qs}} \ge 0.50$ vs. Check-Pass Self-Read $m_{\text{ql}}$ (`AUROC = 0.99998`):** In `R9-E2`, the pre-registered self-consistency gate $p_{\text{chk}}(Z) \ge 0.80 \wedge m_{\text{qs}}(Z) \ge 0.50$ eliminated all false accepts (`0.0%` on all seeds, `D2.2` **PASS**), but because stochastic completions take at least one low-probability branch ($q_{\text{step}} < 0.50$), the $m_{\text{qs}} \ge 0.50$ clause admitted only greedy lead-chain completions (`AUROC = 0.346` on Seed 103 accepted leaves). The audit (`AUDIT-R9-E2.md`, Question G) showed that reading $m_{\text{ql}} = \min_{c \in E_b} q_{\text{leaf}}(c, Z_c)$ from the single check pass on `Sudoku-Extreme` requires **zero extra forward passes**, admits stochastic completions, and separates valid from invalid accepted leaves on Seed 103 with **`AUROC = 0.99998`**.

## 5. What Remains Untested at Scale and Limitations

1. **Four-Block Parameter Separation in Frontier Language Models:** `LIR-Masked` parameter separation (`\theta_{\text{real}}`, `\theta_{\text{trust}}`, `\theta_{\text{amp}}`, `\theta_{\text{act}}` with `.detach()` on all reward losses) passed on `Qwen3-8B` LoRA over 2,500 Manifold Markets forecasting questions (`R4-E3b` `D1d`: $\Delta_{\text{desire}} = -0.0001$ vs. $+0.490$ `Fused`, sycophancy $1.2\%$ vs. $99.9\%$) and on the `669k`-parameter coupled ShipHunt network with entropy regularization (`R5-E1` Part B: `0.0000` drift, `101%` reward, `1.000` `faith_swap`). However, on `Qwen3-8B`, the action adapter still learned a belief-free mandate shortcut (`faith_swap = 0.39`), and we have not yet trained a multi-billion-parameter language model where the action policy must compose multi-step natural-language plans strictly through a detached latent belief state under an active action-entropy schedule.
2. **Salience Keys and Iterative Clearing Beyond Structured Grids:** In `ShipHunt` (`36` cells) and `Micro-Stratego` (`36` cells $\times 7$ piece types), readout-salience keys $s_r(c) = |\ell_r(c) - \ell_0(c)|$ are defined over an explicit set of board variables. Extending `looped_attn_T3` (`39.7k` params) and `jacobi_lin_T4_prec` (`814` params) to open-ended natural-language rollouts requires defining an analogous salience coordinate system—such as log-probability shifts $|\ell_r(q_j) - \ell_0(q_j)|$ over a bank of canonical probe propositions $\{q_1, \dots, q_m\}$ generated at the root—and testing whether Gram/attention clearing over probe-salience keys preserves policy independence and the three-regime weight law in language.
3. **Oracle-Free Calibration when One-Pass Beliefs Already Dominate:** On $6\times 6$ `ShipHunt`, the training target $\text{ref}(M_0)$ for `looped_attn_T3` and `jacobi_lin_T4_prec` was built from exact Bayesian patches (`R6–R9`), whereas on $6\times 6$ `Micro-Stratego` (`R8-E3`), where no rollout pool beat the one-pass current-turn network (`0.0488`), training `108k`-parameter aggregators on hard realized labels overfit (`R8-E3` `D1(2)` **FAIL**) unless regularised by held-out early stopping. Scaling learned rollout aggregators to domains without an exact solver requires either a verified distillation target (as in `Sudoku-Extreme`, where $p_{\text{chk}} \wedge m_{\text{ql}}$ achieves `0.99998` AUROC) or strict held-out proper-scoring early stopping so the aggregator defaults to the anchor identity when rollouts add no information.
