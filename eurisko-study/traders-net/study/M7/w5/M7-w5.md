# M7 w5: bankruptcy and birth (Stage 1 item 6)

Verdict: **wealth (bankruptcy+birth) does not beat DeepSeek.** At E=8 it ties on BCE (+0.15%, 1/3 paired wins) but collapses under the stop rule in 3/3 seeds. At E=64 it avoids collapse (0/3) but is 5.5% worse on mean BCE than DeepSeek u=1e-3 and 3.6% worse than no balancing. Both of those exceed the 2% stop threshold, so **the arm stops here**.
Pre-registration: `w5/PREREG.md` (written before the first run). Rerun the analysis with `python3 w5/analyze.py`.

## Setup
- Harness: w1's `w1/moe_harness.py` (sha256 2c45486b…, see `w5/gpu-job/SHA256SUMS`), used unmodified through hooks in `w5/bb.py`. The model is d=64, L=2, top-2 over E experts, with an S0 stream of T=24000 and B=8 over 3 seeds.
- Currency (CURRENCY.lock): committed-trader settlement only. Each expert slot is paid the exact log-score leave-one-out at trade time: log p_full(y) − log p_without_slot(y). Wealth is W_i += pay_i − rent, with rent = max(Σpay,0)/E.
- Bankruptcy: once warmup (200 steps) is over, the poorest expert with W < −200·rent_ema is replaced, with a 50-step cooldown per layer.
- Birth rules:
  - `bb`: split the richest expert. Weights get 0.01·std noise, the router row and Adam moments are copied, and wealth is halved between parent and child.
  - `bb-fresh`: new random init with the mean router row.
- Approximation: for the last layer the leave-one-out is exact. For layer 0, the slot is removed for all tokens jointly, so the value includes attention spill.
- Primary comparison: all 30 runs on one G1 L4 VM (job m7-w5-bb, 5 arms × E{8,64} × 3 seeds), in `w5/results/gpu/runs/*.json`. Cross-hardware reproduction: CPU runs of bb and bb-fresh at E=8 in `w5/results/cpu/runs/`, compared against w1's controls in `w1/results/controls_runs.csv`.

The CPU reproduction matches the GPU result (`w5/results/verdict_cpu.json`): bb@E8 +0.0005 ± 0.0010, bb-fresh@E8 −0.0007 ± 0.0006, all collapsed 3/3.

## Why it fails (mechanism)
- Split birth clones the richest expert into a function-preserving twin. The twins share the same tokens, so the max-load share barely drops: the worst layer at E8 goes from 0.65 (none) to 0.57 (bb), while DeepSeek gets 0.37.
- Fresh birth creates an expert that earns nothing. It goes bankrupt again, giving about 110 births per run of churn, and the router re-concentrates, so the worst layer ends at 0.80.
- Bankruptcy removes losers but does nothing to route tokens away from winners. Load balance needs a price on the *router*, and paying experts does not provide one.
