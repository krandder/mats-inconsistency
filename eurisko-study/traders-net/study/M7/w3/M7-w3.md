**TL;DR.** Exp3 shadow credit gives the router the missing gradient for unrouted experts. It does not stop
load collapse, it makes it worse: at E=8 the worst-layer max load is 0.78 ± 0.07, against 0.37 ± 0.05 for
DeepSeek and 0.65 ± 0.14 for no balancing. BCE is the same as DeepSeek within the seed sd at both E. The
pre-registered stop rule fires (max load > 50% in 3/3 seeds at E=8 and 2/3 at E=64). Verdict: exp3 does not
beat DeepSeek, so this arm gives no reason for stage 2.

## What was run
- The shared harness is `../w1/moe_harness.py` (sha256 `2c45486b…`, recorded in every run JSON). S0 streams: Q=64
  plus 8 held-out questions, T=24000, regime shifts at 10k and 20k. Single prequential pass, B=8, Adam 1e-3. MoE in
  both layers, expert d->64->d, top-2, with the gate softmax renormalised over the selected set.
- Arms: `none` and `deepseek` (u=1e-3) are same-code controls. `exp3` samples one uniform unrouted expert per token
  and credits the router logit with c_e = ĝ_e (r_e − R)/q_e. `fullinfo` is a diagnostic that gives every unrouted
  expert this credit with q=1. Code: `exp3.py`.
- Seeds: 1-3. E: 8 and 64. There were 24 runs in one G1 job, `m7-w3-exp3`, on an L4 from 07:00 to 07:19Z. It
  cost $0.27 at the unverified ceiling rate, per the COST-AUDIT line in `../../G1/GPU-LOG.md`.
- Check (`check_shadow.py` -> `results/check_shadow.json`): the credit formula matches the autograd gradient when
  e is added to S, with corr 0.90. It is first-order, because r is read at the mix without e. The exp3 sample
  mean is within 1.3% of fullinfo after 20k samples.

## Why, and what it means for Q5
- Shadow credit follows routed wealth. Per expert, cumulative shadow credit correlates with cumulative routed
  revenue at 0.64 (exp3) and 0.77 (fullinfo) at E=8, and at 0.17 (exp3) and 0.57 (fullinfo) at E=64. With full
  information at E=64, 86% of experts end with negative total credit (`results/shadow_credit_sign.json`). An
  unrouted expert is also an untrained expert, so its honest counterfactual revenue is below the mix, and the
  corrected price pushes it further out. The router is not short of information. The balancing losses exist
  because they pay for the *training* of experts that are poor today. This is an interpretation. The obvious
  test, which was not run, is to also train the shadow expert on its sampled tokens, so that it gets a gradient
  at zero gate.
- For gate_flags: exp3 does not beat DeepSeek at either E, so it does not support `wealth_beats_deepseek`.
  "DeepSeek already built the price" stands for this arm.
- Side observation (the control is w1's item): in this harness DeepSeek halves max load and dead experts, but its
  BCE is not better than no balancing at E=8 (0.3314 against 0.3301). On this task, load balance costs and buys
  little loss.

## Not verified / caveats
- The credit is first-order (corr 0.90 with exact autograd in the toy), and it is applied with a one-step lag
  because the harness hook runs after the optimiser step.
- Exp3 evaluates 3 experts per token in training, 1.5× the top-2 expert FLOPs; fullinfo evaluates all E. Eval
  FLOPs are unchanged at 174,208 per token for E=8.
- Arms run on CUDA in parallel processes; seeds are paired within a device.
- The G1 queue ran jobs serially while one job held `runner.lock` through an inherited fd. This was observed
  06:41Z and noted in `../STATUS.md`. By 07:00Z two jobs ran at once, so someone fixed it; I did not check who.
