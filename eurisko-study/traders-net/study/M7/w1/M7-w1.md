# M7 worker 1: Stage 0 and the four balancing controls

Scope: Stage 0 (the gradient-identity check) and the Stage 1 control arms: none, Switch aux, DeepSeek bias (u=1e-3 and 1e-2), expert choice, and a dense MLP matched on active FLOPs.
The shared harness `w1/moe_harness.py` is also used by w2 to w5.
Prereg: `w1/PREREG.md`, written before the first run.
All paths below are relative to `study/M7/w1/`.

## Stage 0: PASS
- Fixed gate convention (i): S = topk(z + b_sel), g = softmax(z_S) renormalised over S, b_sel used only for selection.
  Identity: -dL/dz_i = g_i (r_i - R), where r_i = <E_i(x), -dL/dh> and R = sum_S g r.
- Result: max relative error <= 1.13e-15 over E in {8, 64}, 3 seeds and both layers. Checks C0a (plain), C0b (with b_sel), C0c (expert choice) and C0e (Switch aux).
  Source: `results/stage0.json`.

Seed-paired differences against deepseek u=1e-3 (`results/controls_paired.csv`). This is the better u on BCE at both E, which makes it the prereg reference.
- E=8, arms lower in 3/3 seeds:
  - none: -0.0013

- E=64: every difference is within noise, with sd 0.008 to 0.03.

Noise floor from repeating the E=8 runs on GPU and CPU (`results/noise_floor.json`, 18 pairs): |dBCE| median 0.00042, mean 0.0019, max 0.0269.

## Reading
1. Load balancing does not change loss at this scale. Every MoE arm lies within about 1 sd of the others. The FLOP-matched dense arm is lowest at E=8 (0.308), and the gap comes from LFSR being learnt in 1 of 3 seeds.
2. Balancing does raise expert specialisation: at E=64, MI goes from 0.24 bits (none) to 0.34-0.38 bits.
   DeepSeek's step size is a real trade-off over 3000 steps. u=1e-3 is too slow (24% dead experts and one collapsed seed at E=64). u=1e-2 balances fully.
3. **The prereg criterion "beats DeepSeek in 3/3 paired seeds" is too weak**: the unbalanced `none` arm passes it at E=8, by 0.4%.
   Recommendation for the wealth arms: require the paired gap to exceed the noise floor (e.g. >1% relative, 3/3 seeds, not collapsed) and to hold at both E.
4. Caveats:
   - LFSR16 is bimodal (learnt or not per seed), which inflates the sd. Use `bce2h_nolfsr` for comparisons.
   - Per-family BCE uses the static fam0, so questions that changed regime mix into the prng family.
