## Verdict
- **Stage 0 passes.** The router gradient equals g_i(r_i − Σ_S g·r) to 1.1e-15 relative error. This holds for plain
  top-2, top-2 with the DeepSeek selection bias, expert choice and the Switch aux loss, at E in {8, 64}, 3 seeds and
  both layers (`w1/results/stage0.json`). The lead re-ran it at merge time and got an identical file.
  Gate convention, fixed for all arms: S = top-k(z + b_sel), where b_sel is selection-only and gets no gradient, and
  g = softmax(z_S) renormalised over S.
- **Stage 1: no wealth arm clearly beats DeepSeek. `wealth_beats_deepseek = false` is a judgement call (see "Gate").**
  - Exp3 shadow credit, bankruptcy and birth, the global wealth prior and the per-family prior without DeepSeek all
    fail. They collapse (stop rule), lose to DeepSeek, or both.
  - The one arm with a consistent edge is **DeepSeek bias + per-family wealth prior (w2 `dsfw`, γ=0.999)**. On
    all-position prequential BCE it is lower than DeepSeek u=1e-3 in 3/3 paired seeds:
    - E=8: −1.30%, paired sd 0.0009
    - E=64: −1.87%, paired sd 0.0049
    - It does not collapse (worst-layer max load 0.24 at E=8, 0.11 at E=64).

    The edge fails four robustness checks, listed under "Gate".
- **The substrate cannot test Q5 as asked.** At this scale load balancing buys almost no loss:
  - The unbalanced `none` arm collapses, yet it matches or beats DeepSeek on BCE in every harness, by 0.3-0.7% in the
    paired comparison (`merge/arms.csv`, `d_vs_ds` for role=none).
  - The FLOP-matched dense MLP is best at E=8.

  So "wealth replaces the anti-collapse role" cannot show a loss benefit here. The load and specialisation effects do
  show, and they mostly go the wrong way. Reading for Q5: "DeepSeek already built the price". The wealth signals
  tried here pay experts, and they do not price the router against load.

## Gate: stage 2 (`wealth_beats_deepseek`)
The plan's criterion ("a wealth arm beats DeepSeek") was pre-registered in three different forms.
`merge/gate.json` evaluates all three:
- **R1** (`w1/PREREG.md`, marked "applied by the M7 merger"): lower in 3/3 paired seeds and not collapsed.
  `dsfw` passes at both E.
- **R2** (w1's post-noise-floor recommendation, `w1/M7-w1.md`): R1, plus a paired gap above 1% relative, at both E.
  `dsfw` passes, and it is the only arm that does (`gate.json: passing_R2_primary`).
- **R3** (each arm's own PREREG, written before its runs): the gap must exceed the between-seed sd at both E
  (`w2/PREREG.md`). **No arm passes.** dsfw's gap (0.005 / 0.007) is below the seed sd (0.012 / 0.023) in
  `w2/results/decision.json`.

The lead sets the flag to **false** because the dsfw edge does not survive the checks a stage-2 run would depend on:
1. **Window.** On the late window (`bce_last10k`) at E=64, dsfw is lower in only 2/3 seeds, Δ −1.33% ± 0.011
   (`merge/arms.csv`, metric bce_last10k). At E=8 the gap shrinks to −0.34%. The gain is mostly early learning.
2. **Against no balancing.** At E=64, dsfw beats plain `none` in only 2/3 seeds (Δ −0.0057, `merge/arms.csv`
   d_vs_none). So it does not clearly add anything over dropping the balancer.
3. **Cost.**
   - dsfw is worse after regime shifts in 3/3 seeds at both E, which is lock-in. The paired Δ in post-shift BCE is
     +0.041 to +0.054 at E=8 and +0.047 to +0.092 at E=64 (`w2/results/paired.csv`).
   - 58% of its experts are dead at E=64, against 10% for ds3 (`w2/results/summary.csv`).
   - Training takes 6 forwards per step instead of 2, for slot-removal settlement (`w2/results/summary.csv`,
     forwards_per_step).
4. **Transfer.** The gain needs the generator's family labels. With random groups (`dsrw`) the prior gets
   −0.34% / +0.88%. Stage 2 (TinyStories) has no family labels, so stage 2 as specified cannot run this arm without a
   label proxy that has not been designed.

If the orchestrator treats R1 or R2 as binding, the gate flips to true for `dsfw` only. In that case the right stage 2
is a small confirmatory run, not the full plan: dsfw vs DeepSeek vs none, on a substrate with family labels, a longer
horizon, and the late-window metric pre-registered as primary.

## Caveats
- Everything ran at one small scale: d=32-64, 2 layers, 3000-5000 steps. The between-seed sd is dominated by the
  stream seed and by LFSR16 being learnt or not (`w1/M7-w1.md`). Only paired views have power.
- Harness differences: w2 and w4 used their own harnesses. w4's DeepSeek does not balance E=64 within 5000 steps at
  u=1e-3.
- Settlement: w2 and w5 remove each slot's output for the whole batch, so cross-token spill is included in the payoff
  for layer 0. w3's shadow credit is first-order (corr 0.90 with autograd, `w3/results/check_shadow.json`).
- G1 queue: it dispatched serially for a while (runner.lock fd inherited by job-run.sh; `STATUS.md` 06:40-06:54Z).
  This was diagnosed but not fixed by M7.
