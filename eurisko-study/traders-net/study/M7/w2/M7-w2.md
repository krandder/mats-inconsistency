## Side effects (per-run JSONs in results/all/)
- No arm exceeded max load 50%. dsfw E=64: max load 0.111 (=1/9, one expert group per family), dead_frac 58%, expert x family MI 2.93 bits.
- Lock-in: post-shift BCE worse for family-wealth arms (dsfw E64 0.573 vs ds3 0.509; E8 0.561 vs 0.514).
- Held-out family early (first 1k steps after release) better: dsfw E64 0.079 vs ds3 0.115.
- Cost: wealth arms need 6 forwards/step (slot-removal settlement) vs 2, ~2.5x wall time.
- The gain is family-level routing (random-group control dsrw gets ~0), bought at the price of regime-shift adaptation.
