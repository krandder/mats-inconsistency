**Why only this.** Gate B not passed (`../../M3/CURRENCY.md` "Gate B input"; `../../M2/BAKEOFF.md`

**Setup.** S0 shift stream (Q=64+8 held-out, T=40k, 25% of main questions switch family at
10k/20k/30k), S0 `Net` d=64 L=2 (2x256 MLP neurons), prequential B=4, Adam 1e-3; last 512
positions never trained (census). Income per neuron = EMA (half-life 1000 steps) of −h·∂L/∂h on each
batch after it was scored. Gate per layer w = N·[(1−α)softmax(s·η·z) + α/N], z = standardised
income, α = 1e-3, mean gate 1. Arms: `none`; `pos` (s=+1) and `neg` (s=−1, sign flipped) at η = 0.1
and 1; `shuf1` = `pos1` gate values permuted across neurons (redrawn every 1000 steps).

## Results (seeds 1-3, mean ± sd; `results/summary.csv`, `results/decision.json`)

| arm | BCE, whole run | same, lfsr16 removed | BCE, switched questions, 2k after shifts |
|---|---|---|---|
| none (ungated) | 0.3554 ± 0.0071 | 0.3079 ± 0.0190 | 0.4533 ± 0.0330 |
| pos0.1 | 0.3542 ± 0.0066 | 0.3075 ± 0.0186 | 0.4491 ± 0.0364 |
| neg0.1 | 0.3595 ± 0.0115 | 0.3077 ± 0.0188 | 0.4544 ± 0.0345 |
| pos1 | 0.3673 ± 0.0145 | 0.3145 ± 0.0189 | 0.4581 ± 0.0330 |
| neg1 | 0.3719 ± 0.0135 | 0.3197 ± 0.0169 | 0.4706 ± 0.0352 |
| shuf1 | 0.3665 ± 0.0153 | 0.3136 ± 0.0192 | 0.4590 ± 0.0349 |

1. **The sign of gradient income is not a usable gate signal (rule 1 fails).** With lfsr16 removed,
   `neg1 − pos1` = +0.0079/+0.0026/+0.0051 nats (not ≥ 0.005 in 3/3) and `shuf1 − pos1` =
   −0.0008/−0.0030/+0.0008: gating by +income is indistinguishable from the same gate values on
   random neurons.
2. **Stop rule fires (rule 2):** `pos1` does not beat `shuf1` in 3/3 seeds. M8 stops here.
3. **Gating by income hurts at all.** Ungated beats `pos1` by 0.0067/0.0066/0.0066 nats (lfsr16 removed,
   3/3). At η = 0.1 the gate is nearly flat (max 2.08 ± 0.38) and `pos0.1`, `neg0.1`, `none` agree to
   ≤ 0.001 nats per seed (lfsr16 removed, `summary.csv` `bce_no_lfsr16_seeds`). What damage there is at η = 1 comes from gate concentration, not identity
   (`shuf1` ≈ `pos1`). Flipped-sign income is heavy-tailed: `neg1` collapses to one neuron per layer
   (gate max 255.74 of 256) and still costs only ~0.005 nats more than `pos1`.
4. **Census (ungated arm, held-out single-neuron ablation):** 34.6 ± 3.3% of the top-10% most important
   neurons carry negative EMA income, vs 49.0 ± 1.9% of all neurons. Rule 3 ("anti-informative on
   important units") fails: the sign leans the right way, but a third of the units that matter are
   still paid negatively. Spearman(income, ΔBCE) 0.106 ± 0.034; the unsigned |a·g| EMA 0.203 ± 0.068.
   Consistent with M1's sign census on wealth-net (`../../M1/CURRENCY.lock` (b)) and M2's tracr
   ground truth (42-67% negative, `../../M2/BAKEOFF.md`).
5. **LFSR16 lottery.** 9 LFSR16 questions were learnt only in seed 3 of `none` and `pos0.1` (0 elsewhere);
   this is the whole-run gap in seed 3 (`none − pos1` raw −0.0242 vs −0.0066 with lfsr16 removed), so
   rules are read on the lfsr16-removed BCE (prereg amendment 2).

Prediction check: "(1) fails; neg1 within noise of or better than pos1" — (1) failed as predicted;
neg1 is slightly worse than pos1 (below the 0.005 threshold in 1/3 seeds). "Census holds or ties" —
it did not hold: important units are less often negative than the base rate, not more.

**Prior art.** Signed first-order credit vs. |Taylor|: Molchanov et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.06440 and
https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10771 use squared/absolute Taylor scores; movement pruning
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.07683 is the signed score (and the soft version is exactly exp(η·income)
gating, `../../L1/NOVELTY-FLAGS.md` F18); continual backprop https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13812 uses an unsigned utility.

**Not done / caveats.** One gate family (softmax on standardised income), one half-life, no rebirth or exit;
single-neuron zero ablation on 128 positions × 72 questions (noisy per neuron); the gate is detached, so
the net can compensate by rescaling weights; T = 40k, one model size.
