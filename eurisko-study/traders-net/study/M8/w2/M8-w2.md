2026-09-28, worker 2 of 2. Prereg `PREREG.md` (written before any run), claim `../STATUS.md`
(worker 1 owns the sign test, `../w1/`). Code `gated.py`, `family.py`, `aggregate.py`, `runall.sh`.
Data `results/` (per-run JSON, `per_seed.csv`, `summary.csv`, `family_s{1,2,3}.csv`,
`decision.json`). Check: `python3 check.py` re-reads every number below (PASS, rerun by the M8 lead at 10:36Z).
Rerun: `./runall.sh; for s in 1 2 3; do python3 family.py $s; done; python3 aggregate.py`
(PYTHONPATH = wealth-net/.pylib). CPU only, $0 GPU.

**Why only this arm.** Gate B failed: `../../M3/CURRENCY.md` "Gate B input" (no currency beats recent
|a.g| by 0.05 in 3/3 seeds) and `../../M2/BAKEOFF.md` (no coalition win); the 00:25Z RETARGET cut M8
further and `../../M1/CURRENCY.lock` rules out a bookkeeping currency for generic neurons. So no
eta/alpha/c grid: one gate currency (|a.g|) against the plan's M8 controls.

**Setup.** S0 shift stream (Q=64+8 held-out, T=40k, 25% of main questions switch family at 10k/20k/30k),
S0 `Net` d=64 L=2 (256 MLP neurons per layer), prequential B=4, Adam 1e-3. Gated arms keep 128/256 neurons
active per layer; every 100 steps the 2 lowest-score mature neurons per layer are reborn (396 births per run)
and newborns get a guaranteed slot for 200 steps. Gradient scores come from dL/d(mask), so inactive neurons
get first-order shadow credit. Arms: `absag` (EMA of per-token |a.g|), `taylor` (|sum a.g|), `movement`
(-sum a.g), `magnitude`, `cbp` (continual-backprop utility), `random` (static random gate, random rebirth),
`static128` (no mechanism), `dense256` (all active, 2x units, reference). Seeds 1-3, mean +- sd.

1. **Stop rule fires.** absag vs random on post-shift switched BCE per seed: -0.0161, +0.0007, +0.0011
   (`decision.json` `absag_vs_random`): not lower in 3/3. M8 stops: |a.g|-gated execution with rebirth
   shows no gain over random reinit after a regime shift.
2. **The whole-run gap is one family.** absag's 0.013 nats lead over random is LFSR16: with lfsr16 targets
   removed, absag's whole-run BCE is within -0.0017..+0.0042 nats of random, static128 and dense256 (worse in
   7/9 seed comparisons), and it beats no arm in 3/3 (`absag_vs_nolfsr`). On PRNG/CA30 all arms sit at 0.697-0.717 (`family_s*.csv`).
3. **The one lead worth a follow-up (exploratory, not preregistered).** LFSR16 (bimodal under prequential
   training, `../../S0/SUBSTRATE.md` section 2) was learnt in 5/12 runs of the score-gated rebirth arms
   (absag 2/3, taylor, magnitude, cbp 1/3 each) vs 0/9 for random, static128 and movement (dense256 1/3);
   one-sided Fisher exact p = 0.039 for 5/12 vs 0/9, p = 0.20 for absag alone vs random. Reading: recycling
   low-utility neurons with a guaranteed trial slot may raise the chance of finding the parity feature; it
   is not specific to |a.g| (taylor, magnitude, cbp do it too), and 3 seeds cannot separate them.
4. **absag vs cbp** meets the 3/3 criterion on the raw primaries (post-shift -0.020/-0.001/-0.004,
   `absag_vs.cbp`) but not with lfsr16 removed, so it is the same lottery, not a currency effect.
5. **Rebirth survival** (reborn neurons still active when their trial slot ends, of 396): absag 67.7 +- 23.7,
   taylor 73.0 +- 23.1, cbp 43.3 +- 5.5, magnitude 35.3 +- 5.5, movement 14.7 +- 3.5 (random 190.7 +- 4.2 is
   the static half of the gate, not selection). Signed income (movement) keeps the fewest newborns, which
   fits the sign problem (`../../M1/CURRENCY.lock`): a fresh unit's signed first-order credit starts near 0
   or negative. Dead active units: ~0 in every arm (`dead_frac_mean`).

Prediction check (PREREG): "absag ~ taylor ~ cbp within 0.01 of static128" held with lfsr16 removed;
"beats random on primary 1" failed (random is as good once the lottery is removed).
