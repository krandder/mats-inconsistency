## Findings
1. **Anchoring on the market is an initialisation, not humility.** (b) − (a): P +0.0074 ± 0.0107, ME
   +0.0008 ± 0.0012 learnable BCE (`w1/results/PAIRED-w1.csv`).
2. **Shrinkage and early stopping are the bar.** In w1's multi-epoch regime, BOOST (ν = 0.1) cuts noise
   excess from 0.0108 ± 0.0006 to 0.0017 ± 0.0008 (paired −0.0091 ± 0.0004, 3/3). ES gives the best
   learnable BCE: −0.0050 ± 0.0004 vs (a), 3/3 (`w1/results/PAIRED-w1.csv`).
3. **Convex pools buy noise humility and lose LFSR16.** cg's noise excess is 0.0022 ± 0.0002 against
   0.0118 ± 0.0009 for (a) (`w2/summary.csv`). Learned-k pools never learn LFSR16 (0.69 in 6/6 runs).
   Without LFSR16, cg is 0.1779/0.1339/0.1561 against BOOST 0.1806/0.1359/0.1528
   (`lead/M6-verdict.json` `w2_harness_exlfsr`), a tie. Pure Bayes wealth (d0) collapses to about
   one trader per question (eff. traders 1.08 ± 0.07, `w2/summary.csv`).
4. **Utility pools inherit the market's overconfidence.** In w3's harness the logistic market sits at
   0.0197 nats above ln 2 on noise (`lead/w3_detail.json` `market_noise`). elog and eexp end at
   0.014-0.015, worse than every reference. Bounded bets at b = 0.1 are the humblest arm
   (0.0031 ± 0.0002) and also lower the AND violation (0.0325 vs 0.0469 for (b), `w3/SUMMARY-w3.csv`),
   but cost 7-8% learnable BCE vs (b) in 3/3 seeds (`lead/w3_detail.json` `rel_vs_b`). At b = 0.5 the
   arm is (b) (within 0.1%).
5. **The fixed point adds nothing here.** Newton converges in 2.05 ± 0.08 iterations on the base stream
   (the clearing map is nearly flat). 1-pass, 2-pass and FP are within 1 sd on every metric.
   Pre-resolution D-family violations are 0.502 for all three vs 0.561 ± 0.046 for plain: that is
   deference to the maker's 0.5, not coherence (`w4/SUMMARY.csv`, `w4/M6-w4.md`).
6. **RETARGET: committing heads changes what wealth means, but not into importance.** Parallel
   commitment makes clones (head correlation 0.9934 ± 0.0015). Wealth then tracks stand-alone skill
   (Spearman 0.9142 ± 0.0238), not marginal value (−0.0016 ± 0.0221), and is not conserved (sum 16x the
   market gain). Sequential commitment at the trade price conserves exactly and gives the key head the
   right sign where gradient credit is negative. It costs 1.3-2.0% BCE and is dropped (`w5/M6-w5.md`,
   `w5/results/{summary,byhead,signs}.csv`).

  used above. Absolute numbers must not be compared across rows from different harnesses.

  harness. In w3 (d=32) it is worse than WD on noise (0.0080 vs 0.0043), because the offset market is
  overconfident on noise. This makes the protection clause easier to pass in w3.

  mean − 1 sd. The two tests disagree only on fb05's protection flag, and fb05 survives on the BCE clause
  either way, so the verdicts are the same (`w3/SUMMARY-w3.json`, `lead/w3_detail.json`).
