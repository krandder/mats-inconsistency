## Setup
S0 LI stream (`li_families.make_li_stream(seed, 20000)`, 68 questions: D-delay 0/64/512 with twins and
solos, AND/OR, D-prove taut_or/contra/impl/ex3 with staggered releases and deductive reveal, D-expensive
CA110/LFSR16 64/256/1024 ahead), single prequential pass, each label trained on at its reveal. One
harness for every arm (trunk d=32 L=1, maker = S0 Logistic detached, 16 heads per question with w2's
head d_i = <y, rw_i> + rc_i l0 + rb_i). Arms b, cg, cq, fb01, fb05 (the surviving base arms), reference
(a), kill controls wd (AdamW 0.1) and boost (l0 + 0.1 Σd). ES is undefined in a single pass (s1 has it).

## Findings
1. **On the D stream the probability pool is the best arm and beats both kill controls on D-learnable
   BCE, paired 3/3** (cg − min(wd, boost) = −0.0102, per seed −0.0097/−0.0113/−0.0096,
   `PAIRED-vs-kill.json`), and halves the twin violation at D=64 vs (a) (0.029 vs 0.062; vs boost
   −0.013, 3/3). This reverses the base pass, where cg lost LFSR16 and was +35-44% worse than (a): the
   LI stream has no LFSR16-luck term, and delayed labels slow learning, which favours the anchored and
   averaged arms. The pool's k never moved from uniform (effective traders 15.84-15.95 of 16, cg and cq
   identical to 3 decimals, `results/li-c*-s*.json` `pool_eff_traders`), so the gain is an anchored
   probability-space average of 16 heads, not learned trust or wealth. Boost (anchor + shrinkage) gets
   most of it: cg's margin over boost is ~0.01 nats (2.6%).
2. **Humility before resolution separates arms; coherence does not.** Before a D-prove question has any
   revealed history, unanchored or unbounded arms quote confidently wrong: prove BCE 1.66 ± 0.26 for (a),
   1.61 for (b), 1.37 for wd, vs 0.715 (cg), 0.727 (fb01), 0.726 (boost), 0.693 (maker). Anchoring on
   the market (b) is not enough; bounding the bet (fb01), shrinkage (boost) or pooling is. None beats the
   maker's 0.693: this is deference to an uninformed prior, not logical inference (exploratory metric,
   not in the PREREG stop rules; `SUMMARY.csv` rows `prove_bce_pre`).
3. **After resolution every arm learns the proofs** (prove BCE post 0.16-0.17, maker 0.176) and the
   AND/OR violations are the same for every arm within 1 sd (AND 0.023-0.036, OR 0.076-0.085).
4. **D-expensive is partly learnable** (exp64 BCE 0.38 for cg vs 0.555 for the maker, ln 2 = 0.693);
   no arm is above ln 2 on any exp family (max 0.543, fb01 exp256), so the delay-with-cost family does
   not expose overconfidence in this regime.
5. fb01 costs D-learnable BCE (+1-5% vs (a)) for the best pre-resolution violation (0.506), consistent
   with the base pass (humblest arm, pays 7-8% learnable BCE).

## Limits
- One stream size (T=20k, d=32, L=1); the delayed-label regime is data-poor, which helps averaged arms.
- 1-pass/2-pass/FP and the committed heads (w5 Cm/Cw) were not rebuilt here; w4's LI numbers for
  1-pass/2-pass/FP (`../w4/results/li-*.json`) come from a different head parameterisation and are not
  compared in absolute terms.
- Runs were produced by an earlier `run_all.sh` (xargs form) whose skip-if-done test errored but ran each
  run exactly once (`results/run.log`, 24 lines); the current `run_all.sh` is a plain bash loop that skips
  the 24 finished runs.
- Reproducibility: rerun of cg seed 2 at 10:4xZ (/tmp/traders-net/M6s3/rerun/li-cg-s2.json) reproduces P1 0.38638 and P2 0.50669 exactly.
