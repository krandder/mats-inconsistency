## TL;DR
- **Stop rule: the anchored arm survives.** (b) is ≥1% worse than (a) on learnable BCE in only 1/3 seeds in each regime
  (P: seed 3; ME: seed 1), not 3/3 (`results/verdict-w1.json`), so `base_arms_survive = true`.
- **Anchoring on the market adds nothing measurable.** Paired (b) − (a) learnable BCE: P +0.0074 ± 0.0107, ME +0.0008 ± 0.0012;
  humility P −0.0000 ± 0.0001, ME +0.0014 ± 0.0027 (`results/PAIRED-w1.csv`).
- **The kill controls set the bar, and boosting with shrinkage is the one to beat.** In ME, BOOST (l0 + 0.1·Σd) cuts the plain
  net's excess BCE on PRNG/CA30 from 0.0108 ± 0.0006 to 0.0017 ± 0.0008 (paired −0.0091 ± 0.0004, 3/3) and improves learnable
  BCE by 0.0031 ± 0.0014; early stopping gives the best learnable BCE (−0.0050 ± 0.0004 vs (a), 3/3). (b) loses to the best kill
  control on humility in 3/3 seeds (+0.008 to +0.014), so a pool arm (c)-(g) must beat BOOST/ES, not (a).
- **Caveat that limits the humility question:** in this regime the plain net barely memorises noise (excess over ln 2 is
  0.011 nats at epoch 20), so "holding PRNG/CA30 at ln 2" separates arms only by ~0.01 nats. A stronger memorisation regime
  (more epochs, smaller train set) is a stress item.

## Setup (from PREREG.md)
S0 substrate streams (`make_streams(Q=64, T=24032, heldout=0, regime_every=0)`), S0 trunk (d=64, H=4, L=2), a detached
per-question logistic market l0 trained on the same batches, and 16 trader heads per question reading the residual and l0
(`m6layer.py`, importable by siblings). Arms: A z = Σd_i; B z = l0 + Σd_i; WD = A with AdamW wd 0.1; BOOST z = l0 + 0.1·Σd_i;
ES = A's epoch chosen on validation BCE; L0 = the market alone. Regimes: P prequential single pass (6000 steps, B=4, scored on
the second half) and ME multi-epoch (train [0,16800), val [16800,19200), test [19200,24000), 20 epochs, batch 128,
`assert_disjoint` on both splits). Seeds 1-3 (stream = init seed). Learnable BCE = macro mean over periodic, lfsr16, ca110,
prime, and, or; humility = max over {prng, ca30} of BCE − ln 2.

## Readings
1. **(b) is (a).** Giving traders the market as an offset instead of as an input feature changes nothing beyond seed noise in
   either regime. With a learnable coefficient on l0 in every head, (a) can reproduce l0 itself; the anchor is only an
   initialisation. Humility has to come from how corrections are bounded or weighted (arms (c)-(g)), not from the offset.
2. **The market is a bad anchor on noise in the prequential regime.** The Adam(1e-2) logistic sits 0.020 nats above ln 2 on
   PRNG/CA30 (S0 saw the same, `S0/SUBSTRATE.md`), yet (b) still ends at 0.0026: the traders undo the market's noise. A humble
   layer that trusts l0 more (small k, bounded bets) would inherit that 0.02 unless the price is itself calibrated; siblings should
   use a better-calibrated l0 (e.g. the running base rate prior in `M1/CURRENCY.lock`) or report against both.
3. **Shrinkage already buys humility.** BOOST at ν = 0.1 (Friedman-style shrinkage on a correction to a market offset) is the
   humblest arm in both regimes with no learnable cost in ME; in P it costs learnable BCE in seed 3 (+0.058) and is within noise
   in seeds 1-2. This is the kill control that most closely matches "traders improve on the market only here and there".
4. **Coherence:** no base arm reduces AND/OR Fréchet violations below the market's (0.034 ME, 0.048 P); the net arms sit at
   0.053-0.061. D-family coherence is a stress item (not run here).
