## Layer
16 traders per question read the trunk residual and the price: belief b_i and confidence
k_i = softplus(...) are affine in the residual and in price features [2p_q - 1, (M(2p-1))_q]
(M a learned QxQ mix, so a trader can read other questions' prices). The market clears the
linear edge demands k_i (b_i - l) against the logistic market maker (l0, detached, trained on its
own loss): F(l) = (l0 + Σ k_i b_i)/(1 + Σ k_i). 1-pass = F(l0); 2-pass = F(F(l0));
FP (g) = l* with F(l*) = l*, joint Newton over the Q questions, ≤ 8 iterations, implicit
gradient (DEQ, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01377). References rerun in-harness, owned by w1:
(a) plain logit sum, (a)+weight decay (AdamW 0.1), boosting (l0 + 0.3 Σ b_i).

## Stop rules (from PREREG)
- **Drop rule, (g) vs (a):** FP/plain - 1 on learnable BCE = +0.8%, +49%, +0.02% (seeds 1-3,
  `VERDICT.json`); ≥ +1% in 1/3 seeds, so **(g) is not dropped** (`base_arms_survive_g: true`).
  The +49% is seed 2, the only run where plain learnt LFSR16 (bimodal under prequential training,
  S0 w1); without LFSR16 FP and plain tie (0.1575 ± 0.0238 vs 0.1572 ± 0.0218; per seed +1.3%,
  -1.3%, +0.05%, `SECONDARY.json`). 1-pass and 2-pass also survive; boosting would be dropped
  (+3.1%, +54%, +2.5%).
- **Keep-FP rule, (g) vs 2-pass:** FP changes pre-resolution D-coherence by +0.17%, +0.12%, -0.08%
  against 2-pass (needs -50%). **Fixed point not kept.** 1-pass, 2-pass and FP are
  indistinguishable on every metric here (differences ≤ 1 sd); the fixed point costs 1.4x the
  wall time of 2-pass.

## What it means
- The humble layers (1-pass, 2-pass, FP) do what "improve on the market here and there" predicts on
  noise: noise excess 0.0027-0.0030 vs plain 0.0038 ± 0.0007, while the maker alone is badly
  overconfident on noise (boost, which trusts it as an offset, 0.022). They match weight decay
  (0.0027 ± 0.0001), the kill control, so the noise gain is not specific to the market.
- Before resolution, the humble layers quote the maker's 0.5 on D-prove questions (0.502) where the
  plain net drifts to 0.56; that is deference to an uninformed prior, not coherence. Nothing trains
  a D-prove question before its reveal, and the learned cross-question price mix M does not create
  coherence: AND/OR violations are the same for all arms (base AND 0.039-0.046, LI OR 0.077-0.089).
  This is the planner's "expected" row for Q3/Q4, not the "exciting" one.
- No arm with the market maker learnt LFSR16 in 3 seeds (plain: 1/3). With T=24k this is a small
  sample; the stress pass should report LFSR learnt counts, not means.

## Limits
Base pass only: single-pass prequential, no multi-epoch regime, no noise traders, no
independent-evidence family (those are the stress tests, gated on base-arm survival). Traders are
linear-edge with price-dependent belief and confidence; a fixed point matters more when demands
are strongly nonlinear in price, which this parameterisation allows but training did not produce
(Newton converges in about 2 iterations on the base stream, so F is nearly flat in l).
