## Skeptic notes (results/skeptic.json)

That makes the artefact reading plausible but it is not tested: no probe was run on the PRNG-parity family (shifted-copy share 0). Status stays unverified. Seed 4 (which draws close pi offsets, X1/w1 12:14Z): probe L pi 0.119 / 0.113 and lfsr16 0.265 / 0.269 (P4 / P16), vs seeds 1-3 pi 0.186 / 0.172. The pi edge shrinks when the net itself sees shifted pi copies, which fits the artefact reading but does not test it.
- **M6**: The cg survival margin is ~0.0014 nats of noise excess (0.2% of ln 2) but consistent (paired t=-9.8, 3/3). The +35-44% learnable-BCE gap of cg vs (a) is LFSR16: (a) learnt it in 3/3 seeds, cg in 0/3 (Fisher one-sided p=0.050). Excluding LFSR16 cg ties BOOST. So cg survives on a tiny but real noise margin, and it systematically blocks LFSR16 learning in this harness; calling that seed luck is not supported by 3/3 vs 0/3. Seed 4: cg LFSR16 BCE 0.695 (not learnt; 0/4), BOOST 0.336 (learnt); cg noise still below BOOST (margin -0.0022).
- **M7**: w2 ran three dsfw variants (dsfw, dsfw_fast, dsfw_mean); the lead reports the best. Under a no-effect null a given variant wins 3/3 at E64 with p=1/8, so at least one of three does with p=0.33. The E8 edge is strong (paired t=-9.3) but E64 (t=-2.55, p=0.13, df 2) is weak; R1/R2 treat 3/3 as sufficient, which the 1/8 null rate does not support at E64. Seed 4 (CPU) adds a 4th paired win at both E: E8 -1.38%, E64 -3.70%; 4-seed paired t E8 -12.8 (p=0.001), E64 -3.3 (p=0.047), df 3; sign test 4/4 p=0.0625 per E.
