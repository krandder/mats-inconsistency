# K1: Kelly-pool economy (thousands of Kelly traders: coexist or collapse?)

Lead merge, checked at 2026-09-28T04:50Z by `K1/check_k1.py` (6/6 stages pass; result `K1/check/check_k1.json`).
Paths below are relative to `traders-net/study/`. Workers' own write-ups: `K1/K1-w1.md`, `K1/w2/K1-w2.md`, `K1/w3/K1-w3.md`.
Prereg index: `K1/PREREG.md`. All numbers are mean ± sd over seeds 1-3. The runs used CPU only; no GPU was needed
(100k traders ran at 0.12-0.17 s/step, per `K1/STATUS.md` 03:21Z).

**TL;DR (Q8).**
1. **Niches decide coexistence.** With niches on (each trader trades one question), every slot stays alive at
   1k/10k/100k and the market reaches the oracle Brier. With niches off, a single belief rule (species) holds at least
   99.7% of the wealth at every scale, which is market selection in the Blume-Easley sense. No clade monoculture forms
   except in a few small, costly arms, so the stop rule does not fire (4 of 49 arms are monocultures).
2. **The plan's "exciting" signature is inverted.** Skilled traders are *less* unequal than the beliefs-shuffled null
   (Gini 0.44 vs 0.74 at Q64 niches on). A Gini near 0.8 that "beats the null" would indicate a worse market here.
3. **Hierarchy and prices do not help.** Rebalancing funds of funds lose wealth share to buy-and-hold in 3/3 seeds
   for every rule, topology and scale. The compute price c only destroys: the best Brier is at c = 0. After a shift,
   the economy adapts by births, not learning: 98% of the wealth in the shifted niches belongs to newborns.

## Setup (engine `K1/econ.py`, rules in its docstring; tests `K1/tests/test_econ.py`, 4/4 pass)
- **Questions.** Binary questions, each driven by a hidden 4-bit-context table. The families are periodic, markov1,
  markov3, parity3, coin and noise. B = 16 questions resolve per step.
- **Traders.** Traders are Kelly bettors in a pari-mutuel pool. There are two kinds: pattern-k (KT counts, k 0..4)
  and online logistic-k. Species = kind × k.
- **Money.**
  - The price is the stake-weighted mean belief.
  - Inflow: a maker stakes 1% of the initial wealth per step at 0.5.
  - Sink: c per question traded (default 1e-4).
  - A trader splits at w ≥ 4 (the child mutates) and dies at w < 0.01.
- **Control.** A beliefs-shuffled null runs in lockstep on the same stream and the same initial population.
- **Conservation.** Money is conserved in all 141 runs: the maximum ledger or settlement residual over every logged
  row is 4.1e-12 (`check_k1.json` → stages.conservation).
- **Live view.** All 141 runs log V1-schema `metrics.csv` plus the S0 `runlog.py` long CSV. The V1 exporter
  (http://[ip-removed]:8767) serves every run as `K1.<run>` with at least one data row
  (`check_k1.json` → v1_serves_all). The Grafana dashboard `traders-net` shows wealth, Gini against the null,
  births and bankruptcies evolving over time.

The Brier sd across seeds reflects which stream each seed draws, so compare each arm with its own null and oracle
row (`tail_brier_*` columns).

- **Niches decide survival.** Niches-on keeps every slot alive and reaches the oracle at Q64 at every N (0.1195 vs
  0.1191 at 10k and 100k). Niches-off loses about 88% of its traders and stays at a Brier of 0.187-0.191. A generalist
  pays for 16 questions every step, so its compute sink exceeds its inflow. At 100k Q64 the sink is 237k against an
  inflow of 161k with niches off, and 25k against 388k with niches on (`cells.csv` sink_compute_mean, R_total_mean).
- **Monoculture is of the rule, not the lineage.** Without niches, the top species (pat4) holds at least 0.997 of
  the wealth in every multi-question cell (minimum 0.9966, `check_k1.json` → headline). Top clade share still falls
  with N (Q64 off: 0.84 → 0.51 → 0.10). With niches on, the top species holds only 0.16-0.46 (`cells.csv`
  top_species_share_mean). This matches the prereg prediction "monoculture without niches" and the plan's
  "expected" row for Q8.
- **Gini is below the null in 14/15 cells.** The exception is 1k Q4096-on, a tie (0.545±0.010 vs 0.551±0.005), where
  most questions have no trader. The prereg prediction "niches-on Gini above the null" is refuted.
- **Coverage limits Q4096.** Its Brier improves with N (0.234 → 0.176 → 0.147, oracle 0.123) as more questions get a
  trader.

## 2. Shift and compute price (worker 2; 54 runs `K1/runs/w2-*`; tables `K1/w2/results/{shift_runs,csweep_runs}.csv`, `summary.json`; plots `shift_excess_regret.png`, `csweep.png`)

**Shift.** At t = 10000 a quarter of the questions redraw their family (T = 20000). Two populations:
10k traders with niches on, and 1k with niches off, both Q64.

| metric | 10k on, shift | 10k on, placebo | 1k off, shift | 1k off, placebo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| peak excess Brier regret, shifted Qs | 0.129 ± 0.044 | 0.002 ± 0.001 | 0.032 ± 0.020 | 0.041 ± 0.008 |
| recovery to ≤10% of the peak (steps) | 833 ± 58 | n/a | not separable from noise | n/a |
| wealth in shifted niches held by post-shift newborns | 0.98 ± 0.004 | 0.45 ± 0.07 | n/a | n/a |
| clade-wealth Spearman, t = 9900 vs 20000 | 0.87 ± 0.02 | 0.91 ± 0.01 | 0.998 ± 0.004 | 0.996 ± 0.003 |
| tail market regret | 0.0003 ± 0.0001 | 0.0002 ± 0.0000 | 0.066 ± 0.009 | 0.067 ± 0.006 |

- With niches on, the market absorbs the shift in about 830 steps, by replacement: newborns hold 98% of the
  shifted niches' wealth.
- With niches off, the pat4 monoculture prices every question equally badly, so the shift is invisible.

**Compute price.** c is expressed as c/c*, where c* = subsidy per trade: 0.04 with niches on, 6.25e-4 with niches
off. T = 10000.
- **Survivors fall monotonically in c.**
  - Niches on (10k): all slots stay alive up to c/c* = 0.03. At 0.3, 3773 ± 959 survive; at 3, 30 ± 51.
  - Niches off (1k): 845 ± 17 at c = 0; 12 ± 11 at 3.
- **Brier is never helped.** The best regret is at c = 0 (niches on 0.00043 ± 0.00008; within sd up to c/c* = 0.03).
  The prereg prediction of an intermediate optimum fails.
- **Niches off goes monoculture** (top clade ≥ 0.9 in ≥ 2/3 seeds) at c/c* = 0.1, 1 and 3. Niches on never does;
  its top clade share is at most 0.16 at c/c* ≤ 1.
- **Consistency across workers.** Worker 1's niches-off default c = 1e-4 is c/c* = 0.16 in worker 2's units.
  Its 1k Q64-off survivors (119 ± 27) fall between worker 2's 144 ± 39 (at 0.1) and 84 ± 19 (at 0.3), as they should.

## 3. Funds of funds (worker 3; 42 runs `K1/runs/w3-*`, Q64 niches on, T = 10000; tables `K1/w3/out/summary_N{1000,10000}.csv`, per-seed `summary.json`; figures `fund_share_N*.png`)

Half of the groups (8 leaves each) are active funds and half are inert buy-and-hold groups, in the same market.
Premium = the change in logit of the funds' wealth share (negative = the funds lose).

| rule | premium 1k | premium 10k | null premium 1k |
|---|---|---|---|
| bh (fixed fractions) | -0.04 ± 0.24 | +0.05 ± 0.11 | +0.32 |
| crp-within | -1.34 ± 0.24 | -6.18 ± 1.19 | +1.05 |
| crp-cross | -5.92 ± 0.23 | -21.2 ± 10.5 | +1.60 |
| eg-within | -0.74 ± 0.28 | -4.74 ± 0.77 | +1.28 |
| eg-cross | -2.17 ± 0.56 | -7.05 ± 1.42 | +1.87 |
| fof (bh subfunds, crp across 4) | -0.68 ± 0.37 | -0.89 ± 0.41 | -0.37 |

- **Buy-and-hold is the flat economy.** Fixed fractions make no difference, as T1 lemma 6 predicts
  (`T1/THEORY.md`, C6c).
- **Rebalancing is anti-selection when skill exists.** Every rebalancing arm loses share in 3/3 seeds. Under shuffled
  beliefs, the same rules gain (volatility pumping).
- **The least-bad structure keeps selection inside the niche.** Two-level fof (buy-and-hold subfunds, rebalanced only
  across niches) loses least.
- **Funds barely move the price.** The largest effect is +0.0035 ± 0.0007 Brier (1k crp-within), and it is worse,
  not better.
- For Kelvin's "big trader = sum of small traders": hierarchy is neutral as accounting and harmful as a fixed
  redistribution rule.

## Stop rule and predictions
- **Stop rule: not triggered.** 4 of 49 arms are monocultures: w1 1k Q4096-off, and w2 1k niches-off at c/c* = 0.1,
  1 and 3 (`check_k1.json` → stop_rule).
- **Plan row Q8.** "Expected: monoculture without niches" holds at the species level.
  "Exciting: coexistence with Gini near 0.8 that beats the null" is half true: coexistence holds with niches on, but
  Gini is 0.44-0.65 and below the null.
- **Methodological finding for X1/D1: the shuffled-beliefs Gini null has the wrong sign for "skill".**
  - At an accurate price, skilled traders earn little and stay equal.
  - Random beliefs compound luck. In w2-S-on10k the null has 7x more births: 213k vs 30k (`births_null` column of
    `K1/runs/w2-S-on10k-s1/metrics.csv`).
  - A better test, **not run**, is the correlation between wealth and held-out log-score gain over the price,
    against the null.
- **Engine caveat.** The pari-mutuel maker loses money only when the price overshoots the truth, so the "subsidy"
  rewards overconfident collective prices.
  - The null's traders receive about 2.7x the skilled market's inflow in w2-S-on10k-s1: W_traders_null 190k vs 71k
    at t = 20000 (`K1/w2/K1-w2.md` finding 2).
  - Compare wealth totals only within a run, not between main and null.
