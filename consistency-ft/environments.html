The Belief Gym

Where can an LLM's beliefs be measured — and trained — under uncertainty, on a small budget? A 14-agent research sweep over benchmarks, hidden-information games, and RL stacks: 70 candidates, 16 deep-verified. The answer turned out to be a gap.

The meta-finding: the thing we want does not exist. Every verified candidate lands in exactly one of three buckets — (a) explicit probabilities scored by proper rules but static and single-turn (Paleka tuples, BLInD, ForecastBench); (b) multi-turn hidden-state games with verifiable rewards but beliefs only implicit in actions (TextArena, Battleship, poker); (c) RL training infrastructure with no belief content at all (verifiers, Environments Hub). Nobody has combined them. The recipe that falls out: use (c) as the vehicle, inject (a)'s elicitation and scoring into (b)'s environments — and prefer environments where the harness already knows the true posterior, so "being Bayesian" earns a dense, per-turn, verifiable reward.

Tier 3 — implicit-belief games (thesis evidence, weaker training fit)

  • SPIRAL (MIT) — self-play RL on Kuhn poker transfers to math reasoning: the strongest published evidence that game-RL trains belief machinery. But no probability elicitation, full-parameter training on ~8×H100, cannot take an API-served policy. WATCH — cite, don't build on.
  • TextArena (MIT, very active) — 100+ text games including belief-games; a Fireworks agent is literally OpenAIAgent(base_url=…). Terminal win/loss only — needs our elicitation layer on top.
  • UnstableBaselines (MIT) — LoRA-first self-play trainer for TextArena; only relevant if training on local weights (e.g. a borrowed DGX), since the policy must be locally vLLM-served.
  • Not deep-verified but noted: AvalonBench / Werewolf-Plus / MindGames (social deduction, heavier), Hanabi (cooperative beliefs, non-LLM tooling), PokerBench (solver-labeled SFT), reasoning-gym (procedural probability tasks, GRPO-ready), Rewarding Doubt (PPO on a log-score betting game — the closest published "wager your credence" recipe), ConfTuner (differentiable tokenized-Brier loss), KellyBench (bankroll sizing), ABIDES / TwinMarket / AucArena / Concordia (econ sims — beliefs load-bearing but tangled with strategy).
