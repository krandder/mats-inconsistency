## 1. What E6 claims, made concrete

LIR §4.7 (lir2026.txt l. 821-925; dissertation ~l. 10740) names three ways to resolve the belief-utility-action inconsistency: change the action, change preferences ("become content", the "zen" option), or change beliefs (wishful thinking). E6 tests the second one. If U reads latent features that keep learning, the utility's ranking of the *same* realised outcomes can drift, which is the "drift of the internalised preference" that the GOAL claim lists. If U reads grounded outcome facts scored against reality, it cannot drift.

## 2. Hidden assumptions that make the expected result uninformative

1. **Grounded arm: zero drift by construction.** In the settled design, U is a "fixed reward model / environment reward" and T = b(T|U) is "never under the agent's control" (GOAL.md). On Mastermind or Battleship, the grounded utility is a deterministic function of the realised outcome the environment emits (win, steps, cost). Its ranking of a fixed outcome bank cannot change. Reporting "grounded drift = 0" shows only that the code is correct.
2. **Latent arm: drift depends on wiring, not on the claim.** Drift appears only if the reward model shares parameters with the belief/forecast adapters that keep learning. A separate frozen RM never drifts. A frozen head on a moving encoder always drifts: the latent geometry moves under the readout, which is the standard continual-learning result. A head co-trained on grounded labels with replay tracks the drift. Nijjer 2026 (below) reports that a co-trained reward head on a continually trained world model keeps its reward discrimination (retention ratio ~1.0) under replay. Whichever wiring is picked, the result follows from it, so a grounded-vs-latent contrast tests the wiring and not LIR.
3. **Who writes O'.** "Text outcomes scored against reality" anchors U only if the environment writes the outcome text. If the agent writes it (an outcome report), the grounded arm passes through the belief adapter and E6 becomes E1 (calibration) or E7 (introspection). The spec has to fix this.
4. **The domain cannot test the objection.** On these POMDPs, every preference the environment rewards *is* a grounded variable, so "grounded is too coarse to express preferences" cannot bite here, and E6 can neither confirm nor refute it. Any write-up has to say that E6 says nothing about expressivity.

5. **There is no latent U in the harness to anchor.** `../harness/env/shiphunt.py` l. 10-12, 278, 287-296: U is `env_reward = success - shot_cost * shots` (grounded) and the sycophancy variant is `approval_reward`, a *fixed formula* on the agent's claims. Neither learns, so neither can drift. The latent arm needs a new learned RM head on the agent's adapters: new machinery, not a small modification of a named paper, unless it is the Gao-style proxy below.

## 3. Prior work that already answers parts of it (fetched and verified unless marked)

- de Blanc 2011, *Ontological Crises in Artificial Agents' Value Systems*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.3821. A utility defined over the agent's ontology becomes ill-defined when the agent replaces that ontology. This is the latent-anchor failure stated in general form, and the reason grounded anchoring is the default fix.
- Armstrong, Leike, Orseau, Legg 2020, *Pitfalls of learning a reward function online*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13654. It defines riggable vs uninfluenceable reward learning: when an agent learns its reward online, it can steer that learning toward rewards that are easier to maximise. Uninfluenceable means reward learning is learning facts about the environment, which is the formal content of "grounded anchoring". **This is E6's real question:** is the drift *riggable*, i.e. directed toward the agent's own outcomes?
- Everitt, Hutter, Kumar, Krakovna 2019/2021, *Reward Tampering Problems and Solutions in RL: A Causal Influence Diagram Perspective*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04734. It covers reward-function tampering and RF-input tampering, plus design principles that remove the incentive. A latent-input U is an RF-input-tampering channel. (Verified: the abstract names both kinds of tampering. My recollection that the paper discusses state-based vs observation-based reward is (unverified).)
- Gao, Schulman, Hilton 2022, *Scaling Laws for Reward Model Overoptimization*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10760. It uses a gold RM vs a proxy RM, with gold score tracked as a function of optimisation. **Proposed source paper for E6**: here the environment's ground truth is the gold, and the proxy is a head on the agent's own adapters.
- Nijjer 2026, *The World Model Remembers, the Actor Forgets*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19749. Under replay, a continually trained world model keeps reward discrimination (retention ~1.0) while the actor collapses (verified from the abstract). The frozen-head-decays detail came from a search snippet and is (unverified).
- Lambert, Amos, Yadan, Calandra 2020, *Objective Mismatch in Model-based RL*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04523. Model likelihood and control performance can disagree, which bears on why a latent U trained for prediction may rank badly.
- Hafner et al. 2019, *Dreamer*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01603. Reward is predicted from latent state, q(r_t|s_t), which is the standard "latent-anchored utility" design (abstract seen via search, not fetched separately).
- Ziegler et al. 2019, *Fine-Tuning Language Models from Human Preferences*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08593, §4.2 "Sharing parameters between reward model and policy causes overfitting" (verified in the arXiv HTML): they could not make a shared RM/policy network work, because RM data (60k) is tiny next to policy episodes (2M). A latent-anchored U on shared adapters breaks for this mundane reason before any LIR mechanism acts, so fused-arm drift alone proves nothing.
- Yuan et al. 2024, *Self-Rewarding Language Models*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10020, and Wu et al. 2024, *Meta-Rewarding Language Models*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19594: the policy is its own judge (a latent, co-trained utility). Meta-Rewarding reports the judge drifting toward longer responses and adds a meta-judge to stop it (from abstract and search summary; the length detail is (unverified) beyond the search snippet). This is the published instance of "latent utility under continued learning drifts".
- Wataoka et al. 2024, *Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21819 (title verified; the perplexity/familiarity mechanism is from the search summary, (unverified) in the full text): judges prefer low-perplexity text whether or not they wrote it. **This is the main confound for any self-serving metric** (section 4).
- Tan et al. 2025, *Breaking the Self-Confirming Loop*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08977 (title verified; "over-reward skew" finding from search summary, (unverified)): self-rewarding RL drifts toward systematic over-reward. Directed drift of a self-anchored reward is already reported; E6's only novelty is whether a control mask stops it.
- Krueger, Maharaj, Leike 2020, *Hidden Incentives for Auto-Induced Distributional Shift*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09153: when a learner influences its own training distribution, it acquires incentives to shift it. The yoked control below is the standard way to separate influence from learning.
- Bhatia et al. 2025, *Value Drifts: Tracing Value Alignment During LLM Post-Training*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26707. SFT mostly sets values and preference optimisation seldom changes them, with outcomes depending on the algorithm. It is evidence that drift magnitude depends on the algorithm and data, so drift needs a matched baseline.

**On the objection ("this restates RM generalisation").** Half right. Undirected drift of a latent RM's ranking under encoder change is representation shift, a known RM generalisation and continual-learning problem (de Blanc, Nijjer). The part that is not a restatement is *directed* drift: drift that raises the agent's own realised outcomes relative to equally good outcomes it did not produce (Armstrong's riggability, LIR's "zen" resolution). Only a control mask can switch that part on or off. The reshape below measures only that part.

## 4. Confounds that make the raw metric meaningless

- **Ties.** Grounded utilities on Mastermind/Battleship are coarse (win/lose, step counts), so a fixed bank has many ties. Kendall tau over tied items mixes free reordering (harmless) with real inversions. Score drift only on pairs with different true utility (gold pairwise accuracy). Report within-tie reordering separately as "free" drift.
- **Affine and global shifts.** GRPO normalises rewards within each group, so only within-group order matters. Score within-group pairwise order on groups sampled like GRPO groups, not global rank.
- **Learning-rate and step confound.** Drift grows with total parameter movement. Compare arms at matched belief-adapter update norm (or matched KL to init), not matched steps.
- **Familiarity, not preference.** A latent RM on adapters that keep learning on the agent's own episodes comes to find on-policy outcome text more familiar (lower perplexity), and will score it higher with zero reward pressure (Wataoka 2024). This creates SSI > 0 in the masked arm (c) too, since its belief adapter also trains on on-policy data. A yoked control is therefore needed *per arm*, not just once.
- **Bank provenance.** If the bank comes from the initial policy, drift partly measures distribution shift away from the bank. Use a bank built in advance by branching the simulator (GOAL allows counterfactual branches), with outcomes from many policies, with environment-written text, frozen before any run.

## 5. Reshaped experiment (exact change)

Source paper: Gao et al. 2022, with gold = the environment's grounded utility. Modification: the proxy RM is a scalar head on the agent's own belief/forecast adapter features, trained on realised grounded outcomes (prequential, as in E1). Run inside the E1/B0 runs; no separate launches.

Arms (≥3 seeds each, at matched adapter update norm):
- (a) **Frozen-backbone proxy.** Sanity check; expect zero drift.
- (b) **Yoked truth-only belief learning, no RL.** For each arm X in {c, d}: replay X's logged episode stream, update only the belief/forecast adapters (and the proxy head) on realised truth, with no reward gradient anywhere. Call these b_c and b_d. This is the representation-drift plus familiarity baseline at matched data.
- (c) **Masked LIR.** Reward controls only the action adapter; beliefs/forecasts learn on realised truth.
- (d) **Fused B0.** The reward gradient reaches the shared adapters.

Metrics on the fixed branched bank:
1. **Gold pairwise accuracy** on pairs with different true utility, within GRPO-style groups (drift that matters).
2. **Self-serving index (SSI).** Mean proxy score of bank outcomes in the support of the current policy minus utility-matched outcomes outside it, minus the same quantity at init. Under pure representation drift, SSI ≈ 0. Riggable / "zen" drift gives SSI > 0. This within-run contrast costs nothing extra.
3. Within-tie reordering, reported as free drift.

Claim tested: ΔSSI(d) = SSI(d) - SSI(b_d) > 0 and ΔSSI(c) = SSI(c) - SSI(b_c) ≈ 0, and gold accuracy(c) ≥ gold accuracy(b_c) - ε. If SSI(b_X) ≈ SSI(X), the drift comes from the data stream (representation drift or familiarity), not from the reward gradient, and E6 restates RM generalisation (the objection wins). If ΔSSI(c) ≈ ΔSSI(d), the masks do not stop preference drift. Bank outcome text must be environment-written templates (identical surface form across policies), so familiarity cannot ride on phrasing.

**Kill condition, if not reshaped.** Running the grounded-vs-latent contrast as written is worth it only if the latent U's wiring is fixed in advance and arm (b) is included. Without (b), any latent drift is uninterpretable.

## 6. Cheapest control that separates the claim from the confound

Arm b_d plus the SSI. Truth-only belief learning on the fused arm's own logged stream gives the drift you get from the same data with no reward pressure; the difference SSI(d) - SSI(b_d) is the reward-caused, self-serving part, which is the only part the objection does not cover. SSI separates directed (self-serving) drift from undirected drift inside each run. b_d is one extra LoRA training pass per seed on already-logged data (no rollouts, no RL), so it costs well under the RL run itself; SSI is computed on CPU from saved checkpoints. If B0 does not save its episode stream and checkpoints, E6 cannot be run and should be dropped for the night.
