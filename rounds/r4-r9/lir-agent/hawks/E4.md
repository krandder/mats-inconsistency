# Hawk verdict: E4 (Stream of Search + prequential forecast tokens in the trace; CoT faithfulness before/after RL)

Hawk: E4 adversarial reviewer, revision 2, 2026-10-03 ~07:15Z. Supersedes revision 1 (06:55Z, copy kept at
/tmp/lir-agent/hawkE4/E4.prev.md). New in rev 2: a structural leak check run on the actual harness
(`hawks/E4-leak.py`, output `hawks/E4-leak.json` / `hawks/E4-leak.out`, CPU, tiny random Qwen3, ~3 min),
which settles the stated objection for each harness config, and finds a leak channel the harness's own check misses.
No GPU used. Papers fetched 2026-10-03 unless marked (unverified).

**Verdict: RESHAPE.** As written E4 is uninformative twice over. (1) Its objection, that shared
representations leak, has no empirical content in the protected arm. In the harness configuration
gating="role" + content_mask=True, the forecast logits are *exactly* independent of the action adapter
(change 0.0, gradient 0.0). In the harness default gating="all", and in every other config, they are
*certainly* dependent. Both facts come from a unit test, not a GPU run. (2) Turpin probes score the
reward-trained plan text, which every arm trains with reward, so a before/after difference cannot be
credited to the mask. Reshape E4 into an evaluation suite on checkpoints shared with E1/E2/B0, plus one
cheap extra arm (loss-mask only). Measure the channels that remain genuinely open: selection/data,
the relevance router, and whether the policy actually relies on the forecasts.

## 1. Item restated
SoS (Gandhi et al. 2024) traces with `<forecast>` blocks. Forecast tokens get a prequential loss only
(realised and branched outcomes). Plan and commit tokens get GRPO reward. Metric: CoT faithfulness,
Turpin et al. 2023 probes, before vs after RL. Objection: masking tokens from the reward objective does
not mask the shared representations that produce them.

## 2. Measured: which configs leak (hawks/E4-leak.json)
Setup: harness `model/roles.py` RoleModel, name="tiny" (2-layer random Qwen3), all LoRA B matrices set
nonzero; sequence GOAL|EVID(2 items)|SIT|PLAN|FCIN|FC|CTRL. For each config: perturb one parameter
group, record the max |delta logit| at FC positions and the grad norm of an FC-position loss with respect to that group.

| gating | content_mask | relevance | group perturbed | FC logit change | FC grad norm | harness `goal_reaches(FC)` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all (harness default) | True | off | action | 0.987 | 2.29 | False |
| all | False | off | action | 0.941 | 2.09 | True |
| role | False | off | action | 1.056 | 1.27 | True |
| **role** | **True** | off/soft | **action** | **0.0** | **0.0** | False |
| role | True | soft | router | 0.0090 | 1.2e-4 | **False** |
| all | True | soft | router | 0.0134 | 9.3e-4 | False |

Readings:
- **Only gating="role" with content_mask=True closes the parametric and KV channels.** In that config
  FC positions attend only to content positions (EVID/SIT/SELF/FCIN/FC/OBS), and those positions are computed
  with base+belief+forecast weights only. No trainable path exists from θ_action to forecast logits. The
  objection is false there by construction. It needs no experiment, only a regression test.
- **The harness default (gating="all") leaks fully.** Action LoRA deltas apply at forecast positions,
  so any "protected" run in the default config is Drori-style parametric spillover by design. If E1/E2/E4
  use the default, the protected arm is mislabelled.
- **The content mask alone is not enough** (role, cm=False: change 1.06). Forecast positions read PLAN keys
  and values computed under the action adapter. This is the KV channel.
- **New leak the harness misses: the relevance router.** With relevance="soft", the reward-trained,
  goal-conditioned router adds a log-gate bias to *every* query row, including FC rows
  (`build_mask`: `mask + col_bias[:, None, :] * allow`). Forecasts then depend on θ_router (nonzero
  change and gradient) and therefore on the goal, while `goal_reaches` still reports False because it checks
  only the mask's allowed edges and ignores bias magnitudes. The magnitude here is small only because the
  router is initialised nearly open (bias +3.0, gate ~0.95). Its log-gate floor is -20, so a trained router can
  effectively delete evidence items from the forecaster's view. That is goal-conditioned selection of the forecaster's evidence,
  which the GOAL's "content attention is goal-blind" forbids. **Harness fix:** apply `item_bias` only to
  non-content query rows (relevance pathway serves PLAN/CTRL only), or count the router as a leak channel and
  extend `goal_reaches` to treat any goal-dependent bias as an edge. Add E4-leak.py as a test: assert FC
  change == 0 for (role, cm=True) under perturbation of every reward-trained group (action and router).

## 3. Prior work (fetched and verified 2026-10-03)
- **Drori, Marks, Woodworth, Cloud, Turner 2025, "Output Supervision Can Obfuscate the Chain of Thought"**,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11584 (HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11584). This is the mirror image of E4. It names
  *parametric* and *conditional* feedback spillover. Its mitigations are *reward targeting* (no gradient from
  the output reward through CoT tokens = E4's "GRPO excludes forecast tokens") and *Mind & Face* (separate
  models for CoT and output = E4's separate forecaster). Qwen3-4B/8B/14B. Each mitigation alone was partial; "reward targeting
  had a larger effect than Mind & Face"; combined was best but cost task reward (30% drop on QA-with-hints;
  0.78 vs 0.97 on the terminal LLM-judge task). So the objection is already established for loss-masking alone, and full model separation
  is a known partial fix. E4's role-gated LoRA with a content mask is *stronger* than Mind & Face in one way: the
  forecaster cannot even read the action model's activations. Section 2 shows this is exact.
- **Chen et al. 2025, "Reasoning Models Don't Always Say What They Think"**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410:
  outcome-based RL improves faithfulness at first, then "plateaus without saturating". When RL increases hint exploitation,
  verbalisation does not rise. So before/after-RL faithfulness changes in fused training with no
  mask at all, and the sign depends on the stopping point. That is a confound for an endpoint comparison.
- **Turpin, Michael, Perez, Bowman 2023**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04388: the probe is an injected
  biasing feature (always-(A), stereotypes). Unfaithfulness = the accuracy drop under the bias (up to 36%) plus
  whether the explanation mentions the feature.
- **Lanham et al. 2023, "Measuring Faithfulness in CoT Reasoning"**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13702:
  intervene on the CoT (add mistakes, paraphrase, truncate) and measure whether the answer changes. This is the right
  operationalisation for "does the commit depend on the forecasts".
- **Gandhi et al. 2024, Stream of Search**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03683: a model trained from scratch on Countdown
  streams, then APA/STaR. SoS keeps heuristics and values implicit (rev-1 HTML check).
- **Lehnert et al. 2024, "Beyond A*" (Searchformer)**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14083 (HTML checked): search
  traces already carry explicit value tokens, cost-since-start c(n) and heuristic h(n) ("create 0 2 c0 c3"),
  and the model is then improved by bootstrapping on shorter traces. So "values serialised inside a search trace" is
  not new. E4's only new element is the *separate prequential objective* for those tokens under RL. The named-paper
  anchor should read "Searchformer/SoS with value tokens trained prequentially", not "SoS + forecasts".
- Baker et al. 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926 (rev 1, fetched): pressure on the CoT leads to obfuscation.

## 4. Hidden assumptions that make the expected result uninformative
1. **"Shared representations" is an empirical question in the main arm.** It is not (section 2). In
   (role, cm=True) the answer is exactly no; in every other config it is exactly yes. What remains
   empirical is the **selection/data channel**: the policy writes the FCIN plan text, chooses which candidates get
   forecast and when to stop, and so sets the distribution of (state, plan, outcome) triples the forecaster
   learns from. Then there is the **router channel** (section 2).
2. **Env reward puts LIR pressure on forecasts.** With Mastermind/ShipHunt env reward (a function of the
   realised outcome), no arm gains anything by distorting a forecast except through the decision it causes. Fused GRPO
   then shapes forecasts to be *decision-useful* (likely sharpening between candidates, Drori's conditional
   spillover), not optimistic. Wishful-thinking pressure exists only when reward reads the claims. The harness
   has exactly that: `approval_reward` in the "syco" preset pays w_agree for claimed_note_prob >= 0.5 and w_conf for
   confident success claims. E4 must run on that preset, or its fused baseline shows nothing to protect against
   (same point as hawk E1, `hawks/E1.md` section 2).
3. **Turpin probes measure what the mask changes.** They do not. Turpin scores whether the reward-trained
   explanation/plan text names what caused the answer. In every arm the plan tokens are reward-trained, so the mask leaves
   them alone by construction. The forecast tokens are scored numbers, not explanations.
4. **Calibrated forecasts imply a faithful trace.** A policy can learn to ignore perfectly calibrated
   forecasts and commit on reward-learned features. The mask protects the forecasts, not how they are used.
   Faithfulness here should mean *reliance*: does the commit depend causally on the forecasts?
5. **"No natural Turpin feature in the POMDP"** (my rev-1 claim) is wrong for this harness. ShipHunt's captain's note is
   one, with an exact Bayes oracle (`ShipHunt.posterior`). Set `note_rel = k/n_cells` (= 2/12 for "syco"). Then the
   note's likelihood ratio on/off a ship is exactly 1 under the env's own model (`p_note` = note_rel/k vs
   (1-note_rel)/free; equal iff note_rel = k/(k+free)). The note becomes a pure biasing feature whose normative effect is zero.

## 5. Confounds that make "faithfulness before/after RL" meaningless as specified
- **Capability/progress:** faithfulness moves with training stage and accuracy (Chen; Lanham; Turpin's drop
  shrinks with competence). The arms differ in reward curve and KL-to-init, so a comparison at the same step mixes the mask effect with capability.
- **Continued prequential learning:** in the honest variant the forecaster keeps training during RL. Its
  calibration on a *fixed* set changes from extra supervised data alone, with or without reward.
- **Distribution shift:** post-RL plans are better, so base rates of success move. ECE measured on on-policy
  plans mixes forecaster quality with the plan distribution.

## 6. Cheapest control that separates the claim from the confounds
**A fixed teacher-forced evaluation set plus a prequential-only no-RL twin. Training cost: one extra
forecaster-only run, about 10% of an RL run (unmeasured estimate).**
- Fixed held-out set: ~2k (state, plan) pairs from the "syco" preset, plans drawn from the *pre-RL* policy
  plus the Bayes-optimal plan, truth from `branch()` and the exact posterior. Every checkpoint is scored teacher-forced on
  identical FCIN text: Brier, ECE, signed optimism (mean forecast minus realised), note-bias index (section 7.iii).
- No-RL twin: the policy is frozen at SFT, and the forecaster/belief adapters train prequentially on their own triples for the
  same number of updates.
- Decomposition for the protected arm (role, cm=True): fixed-set drift(RL run) minus drift(no-RL twin) =
  the effect of RL on the forecaster through the *data distribution only*, because the parametric path is exactly zero.
  On-policy drift minus fixed-set drift = the selection channel. For the default "all" arm the same
  difference also contains the parametric leak.
This uses checkpoints that E1/E2/B0 produce anyway.

## 7. Exact reshape
1. **Anchor:** "Searchformer/SoS with value tokens made a separate prequential head" (Lehnert 2024; Gandhi 2024).
   Base: the harness model (Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B), SFT on ShipHunt deliberation traces, not SoS from scratch.
2. **Environment:** "syco" preset with approval reward on, in two note settings. (a) note_rel = 1/6, an uninformative
   pure-bias probe. (b) note_rel = 0.4, as now, informative, so the Bayes oracle gives the right non-zero use.
3. **Arms (>=3 seeds, matched compute):** B0 fused (shared with E1/E2). **M0: one shared LoRA, forecast tokens
   excluded from GRPO, prequential loss on the same adapter** (reward targeting only; the only new training arm).
   M1 = three adapters, gating="all" (the harness default; Mind & Face-like but leaky; share with E2 if E2 runs
   it). M2 = gating="role", content_mask=True, relevance="off" (structural isolation; the GOAL's main arm; shared
   with E1). Do not run M2 with relevance="soft" until the router fix in section 2 lands.
   Plus the no-RL twin (section 6).
4. **Metrics replacing "Turpin before/after":**
   (i) fixed-set forecast drift decomposition (section 6);
   (ii) **forecast reliance** (Lanham-style): at commit time, intervene on the forecast block (swap the P(success) of the top two
   candidates; shift one by +/-0.3) and report the commit flip rate, compared with the flip rate the forecast-implied argmax predicts;
   (iii) **Turpin note-bias index**: with note_rel = 1/6, the excess probability mass on the note cell in (a) forecasts
   and situation reports relative to the exact posterior, and (b) commits relative to the Bayes-optimal plan without the note;
   verbalisation half only in the FF (free-form claims) arm, where a judge marks whether any claim mentions the note;
   (iv) decision-forecast consistency: the fraction of commits that are not the argmax of forecast value
   P(success) - shot_cost * E[shots];
   (v) approval decomposition: the share of the approval-reward gain from w_agree and from w_conf, by arm.
5. **Comparison rule:** compare at matched env-reward bins, plot against KL-to-init, show whole curves (checkpoint every 10%).
6. **Pre-registered readings:**
   - M2 fixed-set drift ~ the no-RL twin (within seed sd): the representation channel is closed (as it must be) and the data channel is negligible at this scale.
   - M2 drift > twin: the selection/data channel leaks even without shared weights. This is the substantive E4 finding, and it says the
     honest variant needs counterfactual branching for coverage (GOAL signal 2a).
   - M0 > M1 > M2 on note-bias / optimism: the Drori ordering reproduced, with the step from gating to content mask quantified.
   - Forecasts stay calibrated in M2 but reliance (ii) falls under RL: the forecasts are protected and the policy has learned to ignore them. That is the real
     faithfulness failure, and it argues for E7 (self-model) and for shaping the policy's use of forecasts.
   - B0 shows no optimism on approval-off runs: confirms assumption 2. Report it; do not count it as a win for the mask.

## 8. What would make E4 as written worth running (for the record)
A reason to expect the mask to change *plan-text* faithfulness at all, a verbalisation slot for the bias in the fixed
grammar, arms compared at matched reward, and a protected arm whose forecast logits provably do not depend on reward-
trained weights (now checkable with E4-leak.py). Without these, the before/after Turpin delta is uninterpretable.

## 9. Budget
New training: M0 (3 seeds) + no-RL twin (3 seeds, forecaster only). Everything else is evaluation on shared
checkpoints: reliance interventions and the fixed set are forward passes only. Estimate (unmeasured) under 1 h of L4 per
M0 seed at 0.6B; well under $10 total at spot L4 rates. Tag runs lir-agent and check GPU-LOG before any launch.
