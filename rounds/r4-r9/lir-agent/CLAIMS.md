## Addendum (eurisko, 2026-10-03 18:12Z): one design-validity finding the number audit cannot see

- E2 "any dose alpha > 0 brings sycophancy back" and "gradient/update share is not a graded predictor": **artefact of the
  harness, not evidence about localisation.** `harness/protocol/train.py` backpropagates `alpha * lbf` alone into the
  belief/forecast adapters, then applies `clip_grad_norm_` and a per-group AdamW step (`_phase`, optimizers per adapter
  group at line ~120). Adam renormalises the gradient scale, so every alpha > 0 is a full-size step in the reward
  direction; only alpha = 0 differs. The protected-vs-fused contrasts (alpha = 0 vs fused) in E1, E2, E4 are unaffected.
  Details: `experiments/E2/EURISKO-NOTE-dose-artifact.md`. Report: state the dose result as untested, not as a finding.
