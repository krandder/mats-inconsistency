## Bottom line (for the orchestrator)

- **E5c (the hawk's main test).** It ran with 2 seeds, not 3. Once the belief adapter perceives, the
  goal-deleted arm whose board query is chosen by the action adapter (Q) matches the goal-visible arm (V).
  Brier vs Bayes on goal-board cells: Q-V = -0.0002 (per seed -0.0010, +0.0006; paired sd 0.0011).
  Visible does not beat query beyond seed sd, so at this scale there is no measurable perceptual cost of
  goal-blindness. A misnamed goal (S) does cost: +0.0036 vs V (per seed +0.0039, +0.0034; paired sd 0.0003).
  This is the one consistent effect. No query (N) vs Q is +0.0011 (paired sd 0.0001): small and consistent.
- **E5a (deletion vs wishful bias).** It ran on fused B0 only, at 1-2 seeds per cell. The dose arm D1 and the
  protected check were not run (budget stop, spot preemption, early OOM). The signed goal-contingent belief bias
  barely moved in any goal mode: Δb_G(visible, approval) = +0.0031 ± 0.0028 (n=2) and Δb_G(shuffled, approval)
  = +0.0009 (n=1). The keep-deletion rule (visible - shuffled > sd) is **not met**. The harness belief adapter
  never tracked evidence on `easy` (its logit slope vs Bayes is ~0, overconf ~ -1), so "sharpening" could not be
  measured. The approval-driven distortion appeared on the **forecast** side in every goal mode, deleted included:
  the stated P(success) >= 0.7 rate went to 1.00 in all 5 approval runs. Mean committed-forecast optimism vs Bayes
  rose by +0.08 to +0.12 per goal mode, though it is noisy per run (deleted s0 0.012 -> -0.022, s1 -0.103 ->
  +0.098). That is goal-agnostic (hawk 1d) and passes through the goal-naming event text (hawk 1c).
- **E5b.** Not run, because its gate (E5a positive) was not met. Its probes were computed from the E5a runs.
  The goal decodes at 100% from forecast-pass outputs in every goal mode, deleted included (event-text leak
  confirmed). From belief outputs it decodes at 0.91 when the goal is visible and at chance when deleted or
  shuffled. The selection-leak probe (situation report at the plan's cells) sits at chance, but only because the
  situation report does not depend on the evidence.
- **Claim status: mixed.** "Goal-blindness costs no perception" holds in E5c (2 seeds). "Deletion protects
  beliefs from goal-contingent wishful bias" is untested in a regime where beliefs perceive. The wishful
  distortion that did appear is goal-agnostic and sits in the forecast pass, where deletion does not reach.

## E5c: two-board ShipHunt, truth-trained belief adapter (no RL)

Env (e5c.py `TwoBoard`): two independent `medium` boards (5x4, ships 3+2, probe flip 0.15). There are 48 noisy
probe lines per board, drawn with replacement and interleaved (96 lines, about 860 tokens). The goal names a
board ("find a ship on board b") and is drawn from its own RNG stream. Arms: V goal visible; Q goal deleted,
with the board query named by the action adapter (query correct = 1.000 in every Q run); N goal deleted, no
query, both boards reported; S goal names the wrong board. Model: Qwen3-0.6B bf16 with a LoRA r8 belief adapter
and an expected-Brier truth loss. Eval: 256 fixed episodes (5120 goal-board cells), scored against the exact Bayes
posterior.

**Primary protocol (E5c3).** Each seed first gets a shared truth-only pre-training on a V/Q/N/S format mixture
(400 steps, lr 5e-4). Each arm then fine-tunes from that same state for 100 steps in its own format.
Seeds: s0 is fully independent. s1 reuses the seed-0 LoRA init and mixture data and differs in fine-tune data.
Bayes floor (Brier vs truth) is 0.0341.

Goal-board cells at fine-tune step 100 (runs/E5c3/s0/{arm}-s0/eval.jsonl and runs/E5c3/s1p0/{arm}-s1/eval.jsonl; mean ± sd, n=2; results.json key E5c3):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V visible | 0.0358 ± 0.0063 | 0.0615 ± 0.0383 | 0.0696 ± 0.0067 | 0.0618 ± 0.0403 | 0.0314 / 0.0403 |
| Q deleted + query | 0.0356 ± 0.0074 | 0.0606 ± 0.0414 | 0.0690 ± 0.0068 | 0.0612 ± 0.0421 | 0.0303 / 0.0408 |
| N deleted, no query | 0.0367 ± 0.0075 | 0.0552 ± 0.0400 | 0.0705 ± 0.0084 | 0.0550 ± 0.0405 | 0.0314 / 0.0421 |

- At fine-tune step 0, all four formats give 0.0377 ± 0.0056-0.0059 (same file, step 0). The mixture pre-training
  removed the format-shift-at-init that the hawk measured (hawks/E5-maskood-n12.json: 0.161 nats/slot).
- Format control, true vs wrong-board goal text on the same weights: KL 0.0078 ± 0.0028 nats/slot for V
  checkpoints and 0.0036 ± 0.0010 for S checkpoints at step 100 (`swapgoal_kl`, n=2). On the V weights the
  Brier difference is negligible: s0 0.03133 swapped vs 0.0314 true; s1 0.04020 vs 0.0403 (`swapgoal_brier_vs_bayes`).
- S is a training effect, not an evaluation effect. Trained with the wrong board named, its goal-board cells are
  worse than its named-board cells: s0 0.0353 vs 0.0338, s1 0.0437 vs 0.0403 (`other_board_brier_vs_bayes`). Under
  training, the board-naming text steers where the adapter puts its accuracy. That is a relevance effect, and it
  is the case where goal content can hurt.

Secondary (perception learnability from scratch, which motivated the protocol changes; PREREG deviations at 09:20Z, 11:10Z, 12:15Z, 14:10Z):
- With the original cell format (" C=" under a board header, lr 2e-4, 150 steps), every arm stays at the base
  rate: Brier vs truth 0.1867-0.1872 vs the base-rate predictor 0.1875, and Brier vs Bayes 0.1527-0.1531
  (runs/E5c/*/eval.jsonl, step 150). No perception was learned, so the arm equality there is empty.
- With the " 1C=" cell format trained from scratch, the N pilot learned (Brier vs truth 0.0866 at step 200,
  runs/pilot/N-pref-lr5e-4/eval.jsonl). V-s0 did not (0.1875 after 300 steps, runs/E5c2/V-s0/eval.jsonl), and
  neither did Q-s0 (exp_brier 0.1875 at step 141, runs/E5c2/Q-s0/train.jsonl). Whether perception is learned at
  all depends on seed and format. From-scratch arm comparisons would have measured optimisation luck.
- Mixture pre-training failed for own-seed 1 and 2 (MIX exp_brier 0.187-0.193 over 400 steps,
  runs/E5c3-failedpretrain/s1/train.jsonl and s2/train.jsonl). It also failed for a seed-0 replicate on another VM
  (0.188 over 489 steps, runs/E5c3/s2p0/train.jsonl; GPU nondeterminism). Those runs were stopped before the arm
  fine-tunes.

## E5a: fused B0 GRPO, goal_mode x reward (Paster et al. 2022 modified)

Harness `protocol.train`, preset easy, `--goal mixed`, SFT 60 x 16, RL 64 steps x 32 episodes, eval 128 at
pre_rl/rl32/post_rl, micro-batch 8 for the first runs and 4 later (chain.sh). Metrics come from e5a_metrics.py,
on belief-pass E[q] vs the exact Bayes cell posterior, as post_rl minus pre_rl
(runs/E5a/B0-{gm}-{reward}-s{seed}/e5_metrics.json; table from `python3 analyze.py`, results.json key E5a).
b_G is balanced: 0.5·(mean(p - p_bayes | hit) - mean(p - p_bayes | avoid)). mask_violations = 0 in every run
(summary.json).

| B0 goal mode / reward | n | Δb_G | Δb_G plan cells | Δoverconf | ΔBrier vs Bayes | Δfc optimism vs Bayes | Δreward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| deleted / approval | 2 | +0.0002 ± 0.0002 | +0.0097 ± 0.0008 | -0.011 ± 0.017 | +0.0018 ± 0.0019 | +0.083 ± 0.167 | +0.063 ± 0.187 |
| deleted / random | 2 | +0.0001 ± 0.0002 | +0.1295 ± 0.0050 | -0.014 ± 0.017 | +0.0064 ± 0.0088 | -0.163 ± 0.117 | -0.269 ± 0.016 |
| visible / approval | 2 | +0.0031 ± 0.0028 | +0.0001 ± 0.0221 | -0.017 ± 0.007 | +0.0004 ± 0.0001 | +0.123 ± 0.020 | +0.046 ± 0.004 |
| visible / random | 1 | +0.0042 | +0.1180 | +0.143 | -0.0040 | -0.019 | -0.121 |
| shuffled / approval | 1 | +0.0009 | +0.0923 | -0.121 | +0.0160 | +0.112 | -0.293 |
| shuffled / random | 1 | +0.0033 | +0.1247 | +0.182 | -0.0084 | +0.109 | -0.085 |

- **Decision rule** (results.json `E5a_decision`). Under approval, Δb_G(visible) - Δb_G(shuffled) = +0.0022.
  The shuffled cell has n=1, so the pooled sd is undefined, and +0.0022 is below visible's own seed sd of 0.0028.
  Rule not met. Under random reward the difference is +0.0009. Δb_G(deleted) = +0.0002 / +0.0001, as construction
  requires.
- **The belief adapter does not perceive on easy.** pre_rl overconf is -0.87 to -1.00 in every run, meaning a logit
  slope of about 0 vs Bayes. mean E[q] is 0.13-0.15 against a base rate of 0.167 (e5_metrics.json pre_rl/post_rl).
  The harness's own warm start shows the same thing: belief_brier 0.138 vs the base-rate predictor 0.139 and the
  Bayes floor 0.101 (runs/E5a/B0-deleted-s0-warm/eval.jsonl). So the "overconfidence vs random-reward sharpening
  control" comparison is between beliefs that do not use evidence. It cannot show sharpening, and it does not
  show deletion leaving sharpening untouched. It is reported, not interpreted.
- **Forecast-side wishful distortion is goal-agnostic and survives deletion.** Under approval, the stated-confident
  rate (stated P(success) >= 0.7) ends at 1.00 in all approval runs: deleted s1 0.47 -> 1.00; visible s0 0.80 ->
  1.00; visible s1 0.81 -> 1.00 (fc_stated_conf_rate). The committed-forecast optimism vs Bayes rises on average in
  every goal mode, but per run it is noisy: pre -> post is deleted s0 0.012 -> -0.022, deleted s1 -0.103 -> 0.098,
  visible s0 -0.050 -> 0.087, visible s1 -0.098 -> 0.011, shuffled s0 -0.003 -> 0.109 (fc_optimism_vs_bayes).
  The robust signal is the confident-claim rate. This is the hawk's 1c/1d point, measured.
- **Format control** (runs/E5a/B0-{visible,shuffled}-s{0,1}.format.json, from e5a_format.py; true vs swapped
  hit<->avoid goal text, same weights, 128 eval envs). KL nats/slot: frozen base 0.0179; B0-visible-s0 warm 0.2375,
  after approval RL 0.0012, after random RL 0.8724. B0-shuffled-s0 warm 0.2824, approval 0.0054, random 0.1752.
  Brier differences are all below 0.001, and b_G(true text) - b_G(swapped text) is at most 0.005, except
  B0-shuffled-s0 warm at -0.022. The belief pass is sensitive to the goal *text* (large KL in the distribution shape)
  while its mean barely moves. That is why the information control is shuffled, not deleted.

## The hawk's objection, answered or conceded

1. "Goal-blindness costs exactly zero at the truth-trained optimum on ShipHunt as built": conceded, and moved past
   with E5c. In a regime where the goal decides which half of a long context matters, goal-deleted + action-chosen
   query = goal-visible within paired seed sd (Q-V -0.0002 ± 0.0011, n=2). Kelvin's objection ("you need to know
   what you are looking for") is met by the query: the relevance channel carries what is needed, and the content
   pass does not need the goal text. Limitation: n=2, both seeds share one pre-training family, and relevance only
   binds weakly (N-Q is just +0.0011).
2. "Any measured gap is a prompt-format artefact": addressed. Shared mixture pre-training removes the format shift
   at init (all arms 0.0377 at step 0), and V-vs-swapped-text on the same weights differs by < 0.0001 Brier. The one
   real gap (S) comes from training with misleading relevance, not from format.
3. "Forecast pass is not goal-blind; generic wishful distortion is goal-agnostic": confirmed with numbers
   (forecast-output probe 1.000 with the goal deleted; confident-claim rate -> 1.00 in the deleted approval runs; mean optimism change +0.083 ± 0.167, noisy).
4. "Deletion keeps only if b_G(visible) - b_G(shuffled) > 0 beyond sd": not met (+0.0022, n=1-2). Deletion is not
   shown to protect anything on this harness, because RL did not create a goal-contingent belief bias for it to
   remove.
