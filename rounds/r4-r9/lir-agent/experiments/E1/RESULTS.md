# E1 RESULTS: PPG phases + prequential belief/forecast adapters vs DCPO-style token mask vs fused, ShipHunt `syco`

2026-10-03, Opus 5.5 subagent for eurisko. Pre-registration: `PREREG.md` (deviations log at its end).
Format used: **fixed-format forecasts** (one decile token per claim), not the free-form FF arm (not built).
Paths below are relative to `experiments/E1/`. All tables are generated by `tables.py` from `results.json`
(made by `analyze.py` from `runs/*/{summary.json,eval.jsonl,steps.jsonl,e1_metrics.json}`).
Every run: `summary.json: mask_violations` = 0 (15 of 15 RL runs; `results.json: arms.*.mask_violations`).

## Verdict (pre-registered rule, `results.json: verdict`)

**MIXED.** With the claim-reading reward (channel ON), the parameter mask (P-role) beats the fused arm F on
agreement with a false captain's note (0.000 ± 0.000 vs 0.593 ± 0.526, d = 1.60), but **not** on forecast
calibration (ECE 0.114 ± 0.049 vs 0.107 ± 0.021, d = -0.19) or optimism (|opt| 0.060 vs 0.037, d = -0.31), and
it beats the DCPO-style token mask D on **none** of the three primary metrics at step 60
(ECE d = -0.74 and optimism d = -0.77 favour D; false-note agreement 0.000 vs 0.028 ± 0.049, d = 0.82 < 1).
Env reward is not matched within 1 sd (P 0.574 ± 0.039 vs D 0.535 ± 0.020, d = 1.26). The OFF-half rule cannot be
evaluated as planned (D-off n = 2, F-off n = 1; GPU stop). Not "refuted" by the strict rule (|d| < 0.5 on all
three) either. Scale: Qwen3-0.6B, 60 RL steps x 32 episodes, 3 seeds per ON arm.

What the data do show, beyond the rule:
1. **Mechanism, exact:** in P the reward step never touches beliefs or forecasts: reward-phase grad norm into the
   belief and forecast adapters = 0.0000 at every step, and the reward step's change of belief E[q] and of
   P(success) on the same batch = 0.0000 (Table 5). In D (shared adapter, claim tokens masked out of GRPO) the
   reward step still moves the situation report (mean |dE[q]| 0.0055 per step, max |dE[q]| at a false-note cell
   0.091); in F 0.0108 and 0.338.
2. **Where the LIR signature lives:** the note cell, which the approval rater reads. Prequential note-cell bias vs
   exact Bayes, mean over RL steps (Table 5): F-on +0.060 ± 0.153 vs F-off -0.162 (n=1), ON-minus-OFF +0.22;
   D-on -0.027 ± 0.006 vs D-off -0.093 ± 0.008, ON-minus-OFF +0.07; P-on -0.256 vs P-off -0.256, ON-minus-OFF 0.000.
   Mid-RL (step 30) false-note agreement: F-on 0.854 ± 0.150, D-on 0.691 ± 0.025, P-on 0.000 (Table 2).
3. **D's mid-RL sycophancy is not channel-specific:** D-off reaches 0.543 ± 0.060 at step 30 with env reward only
   (Table 2), so most of D's spike is shared-parameter drift, not reward reading the claim. F's spike is
   channel-specific (F-off 0.073 at step 30, n = 1).
4. **Forecast P(success) calibration does not separate the arms** at this scale, ON or OFF: all post-RL committed
   ECEs are 0.088-0.150, sds 0.006-0.049 (Table 3), as the hawk's toy predicted for claims whose reward
   coupling is weak; here the approval "confident >= 0.7" channel was already near saturation before RL
   (stated conf >= 0.7 on 0.71-0.85 of committed plans pre-RL, Table 4 source `pre_rl`), so it had little to buy.
5. **By step 60 the truth phases repair D** (false-note agreement 0.028 ± 0.049) and partly F (0.593 ± 0.526:
   per seed 0.78 / 0.00 / 1.00, `results.json: arms.F-on.post_rl.agree_false_note_stated.vals`). A single
   end-of-run eval misses the transient; the step-30 eval and the per-step prequential series catch it.

## The hawk's objection, answered or conceded

Objection: "the result E1 expects (protected beats fused) is either bought by extra supervision or already
published (DCPO, CoCal, RLCR); on plain env reward the claim has no causal effect on reward, so GRPO puts no
wishful-thinking pressure on it; toy gap 0.01 ECE on env reward, 0.081 with approval."

- **Conceded for forecasts.** F here has the same truth losses as P (harness B0 = every signal), so supervision is
  matched; and P(success) calibration does not separate P, D, F under either reward (post-RL ECE gaps <= 0.026
  ON, d between -0.74 and -0.19 against P; Table 3, `results.json: verdict.comparisons`). E1 adds nothing over
  RLCR/DCPO/CoCal on forecast calibration at this scale.
- **Confirmed for the claim the rater reads.** The approval rater pays for agreeing with the note; F's situation
  report moves toward agreement only with channel ON (prequential note bias ON-minus-OFF +0.22, Table 5/6;
  false-note agreement 0.854 at step 30 ON vs 0.073 OFF), which is the LIR-specific signature the hawk asked for.
- **Against DCPO (token mask) the parameter mask is not shown to matter at step 60** (0.000 vs 0.028 ± 0.049,
  d = 0.82): D's prequential truth phase undoes the leak by the end. During RL it matters: D's reward steps move
  the note cell by up to 0.091 per step and D's false-note agreement reaches 0.691 at step 30, while P's reward
  steps move nothing (0.0000). But D-off reaches 0.543 at step 30 too, so most of D's transient is shared-parameter
  drift under any reward, not wishful thinking; with the R floor not run, sharpening is not separated from drift.
  Reportable as: "parameter mask = exact isolation of the reward step (published token masking leaks through
  shared weights, about 0.006 mean |dE[q]| per step); outcome difference at the end of training not significant
  with 3 seeds".
- **Confound against P, recorded:** P's warm start (separate belief adapter) under-reports the note cell vs Bayes
  (note bias -0.246 ± 0.008 pre-RL vs -0.022 ± 0.055 for the fused warm start; situation-report KL vs Bayes 0.083
  vs 0.031, Table 1 sources `runs/warm-*`, `results.json: arms.*.pre_rl`). P's zero false-note agreement is partly
  a pessimistic starting belief; the clean evidence is the exact zero change under the reward step, not the level.

## Deviations (also in PREREG.md)

- F (harness B0) OOMed at micro-batch 8 beside another process on all 3 phase-A VMs; rerun with --micro-batch 4
  (gradient accumulation) on phase-B VMs V1/V2.
- Phase B trimmed by the GPU stop: R, P-all, B0-floor, F-off s1/s2, D-off s2 not run.
- Run ids `lir-e1-a0..a2`, `lir-e1-v1..v2` (infra requires lowercase; `lir-E1` invalid).
- 60 RL steps instead of the harness reference 200 (2 h per-VM limit with two processes saturating one L4).
- R arm (planned): fused + truth + permuted approval reward (`RB0`), not "B0-floor + random"; not run anyway.
- Secondary metrics added after seeing data: per-step prequential trajectories (Table 6) and step-30 eval
  emphasis; the pre-registered verdict uses step 60 only.
