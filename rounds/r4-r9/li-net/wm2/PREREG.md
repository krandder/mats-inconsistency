## Question and claims under test
1. Spec question: what is the best way to learn a world model from N training episodes and use it to train the forecaster, measured by single-cell KL to the exact posterior against the number of boards?
2. Decision rule (from the brief, made exact): the champion (selected on validation outcomes only, rule below) **beats rbtd8 at equal boards across the sweep** if its seed-paired validation outcome log-loss is below rbtd8's at every N in {100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000}; this is **confirmed** if, on TEST (5 seeds, scored once), the paired KL difference (champion minus rbtd8) has a 95% t-interval below 0 at every N. Otherwise the report names the N where it fails. The same is reported against k0.
3. T2 question: does the L0 world model learn the board-dependent probe selection, so that where a probe lands counts as evidence? Yes if (a) its location head carries information about the board (location-information diagnostic > 0 at every N, 95% interval over seeds) and (b) on TEST the dream-trained forecaster's KL to the selection-aware posterior is below both (i) the selection-unaware exact posterior (a Bayesian that ignores where probes land; KL 15.1e-3 on validation) and (ii) the same pipeline with a board-blind location head, each with a paired 95% interval below 0, at every N. Reported per N.

## Tasks and data (fixed)

- Exact posteriors: evaluation only.

## Networks and training (fixed across arms)
- Forecaster: round-1 `netdef.Net` (pre-LN transformer over 25 cell tokens, d 128, 4 layers, 4 heads, FF 256, dropout 0.1, 2-D relative attention bias; 577,433 parameters).
- World model (`wm2.WMNet`): the same backbone plus a probe-order embedding and per-cell next-probe-location and reading heads (578,715 parameters). p(episode) = p(board; any-order autoregressive over cells) x prod_t p(cell_t, reading_t | board, earlier probes). Maximum likelihood on the N real episodes; the 8 board symmetries as augmentation (board and probe cells permuted together).
- Optimiser for every phase of every arm (baselines, WM, forecaster): AdamW (0.9, 0.98), weight decay 0.05, warmup 100, clip 1, batch 64 episodes, round 1's per-N learning rate, weight EMA and evaluation cadence (N=100: 3e-4, 0.95, every 10 steps; 300: 1e-3, 0.95, 20; 1000: 1e-3, 0.99, 50; 3000: 1e-3, 0.99, 100; 10000: 1e-3, 0.99, 200). Cap 40000 steps at every N. Patience 25 evaluations at N <= 1000, 40 at N >= 3000.
- Early stopping on outcomes only, all 3000 validation episodes: the WM on the validation episodes' negative log-likelihood (board in one fixed random cell order per board + probe cells + readings); forecasters on the per-cell outcome log-loss of their single-cell forecasts against the validation boards' true cells.
- Dreams: from the early-stopped WM (EMA weights): board cell by cell in a fresh random order (25 passes), then 6 probes (cell drawn among unprobed cells, then the reading). Buffers of 4096 fresh dreams, each used about once.

## Metrics
- Primary: single-cell KL(P* || q) per cell, averaged over 25 cells x 4 stages x boards (and per stage), as round 1 (`smallnet.metrics`).
- Secondary: outcome log-loss, ECE, joint log-likelihood (independent; autoregressive for reveal arms), compute (FLOPs, forward + backward = 3 x forward; dream generation and importance sampling at 1 x forward) per training board, to the best checkpoint and in total, counting every phase (WM, dreams, importance sampling, forecaster).
- World-model diagnostics: validation NLL (board / probe cells / readings); location information = mean over validation episodes of log p(probe cells | true board) - log p(probe cells | another episode's board) (truth: 0 in T1, 1.82 nats per episode in T2); mass of the location head on already-probed cells; EVAL-ONLY: share of dreamed boards that are valid placements, and the WM's posterior restricted to the 11088 valid placements, scored by KL on 200 validation episodes.

## Selection rule (VALIDATION OUTCOMES ONLY)
- Per N: D_N = seed-paired mean of (outcome log-loss of the arm - that of B-rbtd8); G_N = max(seed-paired mean of (B-k0 - B-rbtd8), 5e-5 nats). **S_out = mean over the 5 N of D_N / G_N.** 0 = rbtd8; -1 = beyond rbtd8 by as much as rbtd8 is beyond k0; +1 = k0. Lower is better.
- Eligible: real-world-valid, L0, not flagged (arm 4 never), the full sweep (5 N, >= 3 seeds paired with both baselines). The baselines themselves are not candidates.
- Champion: the eligible tag with the lowest S_out. KL to the posterior is reported for every tag and never used for selection or any training decision.
- Per-N hyperparameters: none are tuned in this round (inherited from round 1, where the audit found the outcome-only choice identical).

## Final TEST protocol (run once, after the champion is fixed on validation)
- T1: champion, B-rbtd8 and B-k0, seeds 0-4, all 5 N, retrained with the same protocol plus `--test` (TEST scored once per run). Paired differences and 95% t-intervals per N against B-rbtd8 and B-k0; the decision rule above.
- T2: the T1 champion's pipeline if it is a world-model arm (else the best world-model arm by T1 S_out), its board-blind-location twin, B-rbtd8 and B-k0, seeds 0-4, all 5 N, `--test`; the T2 rule above.
- Arm 4 (flagged): its TEST numbers, if run, appear only in their own section.
