## What the champion actually trains on

WMr-D-rbtd8 = a split world model whose board network is trained by reveal-chain TD (WMr), plus a fresh round-1 network trained with rbtd8 on half real, half dreamed episodes. Per (N, seed), three networks are trained one after the other, each early-stopped on its own validation signal:

1. **Board network** (`WMNet`, 578,715 parameters, `wm_split=1, wm_board=rbtd, wm_rbtd=8`). Input: no evidence, plus "revealed" true cells. Each step takes 64 of the N training boards with replacement under a random board symmetry and runs round-1 rbtd8 on them at the empty-evidence stage (`SN.train_loss`, 8 passes: each cell's prediction is trained toward the same network's prediction after more true cells of that board are revealed, ending at the true cell). Early stop: validation NLL of the 3000 validation boards, autoregressive in one fixed random order per board. Best step 3400-5600 at N = 100; the 40000-step cap at N >= 3000.
2. **Probe network** (a second `WMNet`). Input: the full true board, the probes so far (cell and reading) and their order. Two heads: a softmax over the 25 cells for the next probe cell, and a logit for the reading at that cell. Maximum likelihood on the N real episodes; early stop on the validation episodes' probe NLL. It learns the flip rate from data (reading NLL 3.013 nats per episode at N >= 3k, against 6 H(0.2) = 3.002) and that probe cells do not depend on the board in T1 (location information within +-0.006 nats in every seed).
3. **Dreams.** A board sampled cell by cell in a random order (25 passes), then 6 probes, each cell drawn from the location head among unprobed cells, then its reading. Buffers of 4096 dreams, each drawn about 4 times with a fresh symmetry. 64% of dreamed boards are legal ShipHunt boards at N = 100, 97% from N = 3000 (evaluation-only statistic).
4. **Forecaster** (round-1 `Net`, 577,433 parameters, the rbtd8 objective of `smallnet.py`). Each step: 32 real episodes + 32 dreams, each at 0/2/4/6 probes (256 board-stages); targets: the realised board for real episodes, the dreamed board for dreams, through the reveal chain. Early stop on validation outcome log-loss. To its best checkpoint it draws about 619 / 260 / 186 / 129 / 86 real episodes per training board and as many dreams.

At evaluation the forecaster sees only the evidence (`SN.readout`, `rev=None`), as in round 1. For seeds 0-2 the final runs retrain the loop's board networks (validation board NLL within 1e-6 nats and the same best step in 15/15 (N, seed)); seeds 3-4 are new.

| only the evidence as input at evaluation | yes | `smallnet.py` 439 |

What the rules do not cover. The pipeline needs whole outcome vectors (all 25 cells of each training board) to train the board model, and a generative model of the evidence process. For real questions the evidence is text, and its "location model" would be a model of which facts get reported, far harder than a 25-way softmax. The gain also comes from strong dependence between outcomes, as in round 1. T2 shows that a WM which cannot see the board cannot pass a selection effect on through dreams. But plain rbtd8 trained on real episodes learns the effect without any WM.

## What the report overstates or leaves out

`RESULTS.md` changed while I audited; this is its state at 22:36Z. It already discloses the excluded evaluation compute (quoting this audit) and states the TEST failure at 3000-10000.

1. **The headline rule.** The TL;DR says the rule is "confirmed at 100-1000 boards and not at 3000-10000". The pre-registered rule is "beats rbtd8 at every N", and it is not confirmed; it fails at N = 3000 and 10000. Section 3 says this correctly ("no").
2. **Why large N failed.** Not the TEST set: each final model scores the same on validation and TEST (row 28). The loop's 3-seed validation lead at N >= 3000 was within run noise, and the audit's phase 1 said so before TEST. rbtd8 itself moved 15% between rounds at 10k with identical code, boards and protocol. "Consistent with round 1 within seed noise" understates this: the seeds are the same, so this is run-to-run noise, and at 10k it is twice the champion's effect.
