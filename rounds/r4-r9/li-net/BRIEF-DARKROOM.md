# Brief: dark room and white-noise room, solved without parameters (eurisko, 2026-10-03)

Kelvin's question, verbatim: "I'm looking for a framing where both the dark room and the white
noise problems are solved 'automatically' without the need to fit parameters."

Background he has: Richardson 2022 (PDG inconsistency = loss; sec. 7: free energy −log Z is the
inconsistency of a factor graph's PDG; local 2202.txt), Richardson & Halpern 2021 (pdg2021.txt),
and the logical induction criterion (abridged.txt, full.txt). Our reading so far: free energy =
expected factor cost − entropy; Friston's dark-room answer ("preferences are the organism's own
factors") is a definition, not a theorem: weak preference factors send the agent to the dark room,
so the principle only constrains behaviour once factors and confidences are fixed from outside.
The white-noise room is handled by the entropy term only if "informative" is read as "reducible by
some belief", which is again a modelling choice. Kelvin rejects answers that bolt on α/β or
preference weights fitted to make the problems go away.

What a satisfying answer looks like: a principle or objective from which (a) an agent does not
seek sensory deprivation and (b) an agent does not seek unlearnable noise, both as consequences of
the principle itself with no free parameters, or a proof that no such principle exists in a given
class (which is also a result). Candidate directions, none verified: expected free energy and
epistemic value (Friston 2015 onward; is the epistemic term parameter-free?); empowerment
(Klyubin, Salge); Schmidhuber's compression progress / curiosity (learning progress, which is
zero for both dark rooms and white noise by construction); Solomonoff/Levin and the logical
inductor's Occam bounds (unlearnable sequences get their frequency, no attention beyond it);
thermodynamic/physics framings where persistence of the system is the objective (nonequilibrium
steady states, Markov blankets); information bottleneck and predictive information (Bialek:
predictive information is zero for both constant and i.i.d. noise streams); active inference
critiques (Sun & Firestone "dark room problem", Klein, the Biehl/Friston exchanges); and the LIR
2026 paper's attention-and-control split (lir2026.txt): attend to inconsistencies that control can
reduce.
